# Eliminate Every Obstacle Between Your Data and Your GPUs

## 核心结论
Dell认为agent长会话让上下文/KV cache成为需治理与持久化的数据层；存储不只保存训练文件，还要避免GPU重复计算与等待。[[executive-sessions/19_Eliminate Every Obstacle Between Your Data and Your GPUs presented by .pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[executive-sessions/19_Eliminate Every Obstacle Between Your Data and Your GPUs presented by .pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
## 关键证据
Exascale方案把file/object/block/parallel的专用软件与共用PowerEdge硬件分开：每节点一种personality，可重部署；这不是同一数据路径同时满足所有协议语义。[[executive-sessions/19_Eliminate Every Obstacle Between Your Data and Your GPUs presented by .pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[executive-sessions/19_Eliminate Every Obstacle Between Your Data and Your GPUs presented by .pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]][[executive-sessions/19_Eliminate Every Obstacle Between Your Data and Your GPUs presented by .pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
## 指标与条件
- R7725xd示例为2U双AMD EPYC、24NVMe、768GB内存、4×400GbE；不能只由端口总带宽推出应用IOPS。[[executive-sessions/19_Eliminate Every Obstacle Between Your Data and Your GPUs presented by .pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- “送达至生产6小时”脚注明确为Dell委托Principled Technologies的**风冷单柜16GPU**方案测试，不能套用于多MW液冷集群或土建周期。[[executive-sessions/19_Eliminate Every Obstacle Between Your Data and Your GPUs presented by .pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
## 图表与架构
已核对p.9–13。p.9的部分产品标题/功能归属看起来错位，本笔记不沿提取顺序分配150GB/s等指标，需厂商修订后引用。p.10仍含coming soon标注。
## 关联与比较
与 [[knowledge/sources/src-f1226a6f9088|近GPU KV内存层]] 比较时，应区分缓存热度、容量、reuse命中率与持久化需求；NVMe不能简单替代每步decode的HBM带宽。
## 不确定性与待验证
无KV offload端到端TTFT/TPS、写入寿命、租户隔离和cache失效规则。“GPU永不等待”“无锁定”为营销概括；硬件复用并不自动消除软件/数据迁移成本。
