# 水足迹供应链框架：区分WUE、生命周期边界与占位演示数据

> 原始题名：Closing the Visibility Gap- An AI-Driven Supply Chain Intelligence Framework for Water Stewardship。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 14 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- Kaya AI/Ecolab提议把水属性从设计、采购、调试贯穿到运营和as-built资产记录；站内WUE不能代表芯片/材料/发电等上游生命周期用水。 [[Sustainability/Closing the Visibility Gap- An AI-Driven Supply Chain Intelligence Framework for Water Stewardship.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]、[[Sustainability/Closing the Visibility Gap- An AI-Driven Supply Chain Intelligence Framework for Water Stewardship.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]、[[Sustainability/Closing the Visibility Gap- An AI-Driven Supply Chain Intelligence Framework for Water Stewardship.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- MAP/PREDICT/REPORT框架明确“highly preliminary”，CDU演示的水量、交期和异常值全部标为placeholders，不能作为真实客户数据。 [[Sustainability/Closing the Visibility Gap- An AI-Driven Supply Chain Intelligence Framework for Water Stewardship.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 附录将冷板相对风冷的水/能耗结果限定为特定published LCA的cradle-to-grave比较，不是所有液冷站点的WUE节省。 [[Sustainability/Closing the Visibility Gap- An AI-Driven Supply Chain Intelligence Framework for Water Stewardship.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]、[[Sustainability/Closing the Visibility Gap- An AI-Driven Supply Chain Intelligence Framework for Water Stewardship.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
