# From Chip to Grid：开放标准下的全栈数据中心共设计

## 核心结论
Google将AI电力问题扩展到电网：同步工作负载的功率爬升、振荡与掉电恢复需要从silicon、近rack电容、软件控制到设施BESS覆盖不同时间尺度。来源明确2026年10月。[[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=1|PDF p.1]][[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
## 关键证据
- 近负载supercap解决高di/dt；设施BESS偏长持续时间并可兼顾备用、削峰、需求响应。不能要求某一控制层覆盖全部频谱。[[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- LVDC演进分现有AC、sidecar混合过渡、集中SST/BESS原生DC；OCP用于统一ride-through、800V、CDU和设施模块接口。[[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]][[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]][[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]][[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=20|PDF p.20]][[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]][[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=22|PDF p.22]]
## 指标与条件
- 原生LVDC对当前AC基线的**内部估计**：20–30%地板空间密度提升、9000安装工时减少、>1% chip-to-grid效率收益；不是完成项目实测且未给规模分母。[[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]]
- Google自述与美国utilities合同整合1GW需求响应容量；它不是同一时刻已调用/发电1GW。[[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
- p.8列不同电网恢复要求，p.20概括90%负载2秒恢复；这是演讲所引用时点要求，不替代项目所在地现行并网审查。[[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[executive-sessions/24_From Chip to Grid Scaling Holistic AI Data Center Co-Design Through Op.pdf#page=20|PDF p.20]]
