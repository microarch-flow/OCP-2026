# 从一次性项目到交付平台：标准化接口、物料与验证，而非冻结建筑

> 阅读范围：已阅读本文件全部可提取文字（16页）。关键页已视觉核对：10；不代表逐页视觉审核。

## 核心结论

平台化的对象是可重复交付系统，不是一栋固定建筑。把接口、controls、数据约定、测试脚本、合格替代料与采购方式稳定下来，同时保留机架密度、CDU布局和retrofit路径弹性。[[market-impact-adoption/03_From Projects to Platforms Reducing Risk in AI Infrastructure Through .pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[market-impact-adoption/03_From Projects to Platforms Reducing Risk in AI Infrastructure Through .pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[market-impact-adoption/03_From Projects to Platforms Reducing Risk in AI Infrastructure Through .pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 关键证据

- 单校园多设计/总包/集成商/供应商会放大局部决策差异；例子是representative scenario，而非真实客户审计。[[market-impact-adoption/03_From Projects to Platforms Reducing Risk in AI Infrastructure Through .pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- 让interfaces在fabrication前被各方看到，通过FAT/PFT、off-site controls和scripts把集成发现前移。[[market-impact-adoption/03_From Projects to Platforms Reducing Risk in AI Infrastructure Through .pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[market-impact-adoption/03_From Projects to Platforms Reducing Risk in AI Infrastructure Through .pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- 液冷采用facility-side统一instrumentation、冗余与维护通道，允许rack侧演进；first-of-kind要以deployable/maintainable/repeatable/trustworthy四项判断是否能复制。[[market-impact-adoption/03_From Projects to Platforms Reducing Risk in AI Infrastructure Through .pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]][[market-impact-adoption/03_From Projects to Platforms Reducing Risk in AI Infrastructure Through .pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]

## 指标与条件

MBOM例子从140+ unique part numbers/60+ spares SKUs/9approvalcycles，收敛到22coreparts/12spares/1reusablepackage；页上明确 **illustrative standardization scenario**，不是实测项目ROI。[[market-impact-adoption/03_From Projects to Platforms Reducing Risk in AI Infrastructure Through .pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

市场工期/供电需求数据来自外部预测/报告，只作问题背景；未提供平台化后实际缩短多少周、成本或事故率。[[market-impact-adoption/03_From Projects to Platforms Reducing Risk in AI Infrastructure Through .pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]]

## 图表与架构

已核对p.10的原始/平台MBOM表及illustrative标记。数字不可当作客户已经节省的库存或采购金额。[[market-impact-adoption/03_From Projects to Platforms Reducing Risk in AI Infrastructure Through .pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 关联与比较

与 firmware/modularity 的逻辑相同：固定接口而保留实现差异。技术开放若没有资格复用、备件和验收流程，仍可能只是一次性定制。该材料为商业交付提供了比“open=便宜”更具体的机制。

## 不确定性与待验证

接口标准本身也会引入共同失效、供应商集中和升级锁定；材料未做这些风险的敏感性分析。telemetry框架只是对齐OCP的机会，未证明跨vendor数据语义已经统一。[[market-impact-adoption/03_From Projects to Platforms Reducing Risk in AI Infrastructure Through .pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]
