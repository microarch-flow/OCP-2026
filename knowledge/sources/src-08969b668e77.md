# LVDC 配电子项目：电压窗口、故障协调与标准化仍在收敛

> 原始题名：The Power Distribution sub-project at OCP - Low Voltage DC Distribution in Data Centers。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 24 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- 子项目不仅选择 800 V，而是同时定义架构、接地、保护、储能、EMC、控制与组件接口；白皮书进展不等于所有基础规范已经定稿。 [[Rack & Power/The Power Distribution sub-project at OCP - Low Voltage DC Distribution in Data Centers.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]、[[Rack & Power/The Power Distribution sub-project at OCP - Low Voltage DC Distribution in Data Centers.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]、[[Rack & Power/The Power Distribution sub-project at OCP - Low Voltage DC Distribution in Data Centers.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]]、[[Rack & Power/The Power Distribution sub-project at OCP - Low Voltage DC Distribution in Data Centers.pdf#page=22|PDF p.22]]
- 电源持续运行、暂态保持与保护动作区必须对齐：示意窗口给出了部分电压边界，但多个保持时间仍为 TBD，不能直接写入采购验收合同。 [[Rack & Power/The Power Distribution sub-project at OCP - Low Voltage DC Distribution in Data Centers.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]
- 在给定故障模型中，限流方案仍有初始电容放电尖峰，主要减少随后持续馈入；快速源限流与局部故障选择性是不同职责。 [[Rack & Power/The Power Distribution sub-project at OCP - Low Voltage DC Distribution in Data Centers.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]、[[Rack & Power/The Power Distribution sub-project at OCP - Low Voltage DC Distribution in Data Centers.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]、[[Rack & Power/The Power Distribution sub-project at OCP - Low Voltage DC Distribution in Data Centers.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
- 机架 54 V 母排与 800 V PDB 路径的功率交叉区只是作者示意；需结合母排、连接器、转换损耗与维护故障域实测，不把 200 或 500–700 kW 当作通用换代阈值。 [[Rack & Power/The Power Distribution sub-project at OCP - Low Voltage DC Distribution in Data Centers.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]、[[Rack & Power/The Power Distribution sub-project at OCP - Low Voltage DC Distribution in Data Centers.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]
