# From Chips to Compute：Foxconn 的GW级交付自动化

## 核心结论
Foxconn认为瓶颈从拿到芯片转为把复杂平台稳定转成可运行算力；因此需设计冻结前DFA、并行工业化、自动测试与跨工厂golden process复制。日期2026-10-12。[[executive-sessions/25_From Chips to Compute Delivering AI Infra at GW Speed presented by Ing.pdf#page=1|PDF p.1]][[executive-sessions/25_From Chips to Compute Delivering AI Infra at GW Speed presented by Ing.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[executive-sessions/25_From Chips to Compute Delivering AI Infra at GW Speed presented by Ing.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[executive-sessions/25_From Chips to Compute Delivering AI Infra at GW Speed presented by Ing.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
## 关键证据
无内部线缆架构、board-to-board和集成manifold服务自动对接；收益不只制造速度，还包括公差、泄漏点和测试可追溯性。[[executive-sessions/25_From Chips to Compute Delivering AI Infra at GW Speed presented by Ing.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[executive-sessions/25_From Chips to Compute Delivering AI Infra at GW Speed presented by Ing.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
## 指标与条件
- VR对GB：自动化覆盖56→94%，装配FPY91→97%，测试FPY39→80%；这些差值分别38、6、41**百分点**，原图用%箭头，不应误当相对增长。NPI4.5→3.5月约缩短22%。[[executive-sessions/25_From Chips to Compute Delivering AI Infra at GW Speed presented by Ing.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- L10称1000+test bays、100+MW容量、12小时/unit、1tray/min/line；测试驻留时长与流水线节拍不是互相矛盾的同一指标。[[executive-sessions/25_From Chips to Compute Delivering AI Infra at GW Speed presented by Ing.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- top-3 CSP匿名案例包含2/7/5/20/5天gate与15周global replication；底部17周/1周标签语义不够清晰，未解读为已节省时长。[[executive-sessions/25_From Chips to Compute Delivering AI Infra at GW Speed presented by Ing.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
## 图表与架构
已核对p.4、7–9，灵活自动化“2小时换线、40%节省空间、55%减少设备”属于下一阶段方案主张，缺基线。[[executive-sessions/25_From Chips to Compute Delivering AI Infra at GW Speed presented by Ing.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
## 关联与比较
与 [[knowledge/sources/src-89c75f05fa2e|工厂作为网络架构]] 共同把制造测试、返修、cleanliness和线体复用纳入产品架构，而不是芯片PPA之后再考虑的后勤。
## 不确定性与待验证
厂商自述数据无样本数/统计窗口、FPY定义和不同复杂度归一化；需要实际交付节奏、返修闭环与现场失效率。
