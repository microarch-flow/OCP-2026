# 08_Supporting load smoothing, Fault Ride-through (FRT), and back up in 80

## 核心结论
Schneider以mission profile而非电池名称选储能：rack capacitance吸收极快di/dt，LVDC powertrain power-battery承担FRT/UPS/分钟级平滑，campus energy storage承担小时级需求响应。不同位置与时长相互补充，不能一台ESS解决全部。[[ai-open-data-center/08_Supporting load smoothing, Fault Ride-through (FRT), and back up in 80.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[ai-open-data-center/08_Supporting load smoothing, Fault Ride-through (FRT), and back up in 80.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[ai-open-data-center/08_Supporting load smoothing, Fault Ride-through (FRT), and back up in 80.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]

## 关键证据
用例还必须区分dispatch频率：AI buffering持续，FRT/backup较少，continuous smoothing频繁；相同kWh可有完全不同寿命与充电功率需求。窄800V rail使droop可用电压余量有限；active sharing提高稳压但可能引入专用通信/并联与跨vendor集成约束。[[ai-open-data-center/08_Supporting load smoothing, Fault Ride-through (FRT), and back up in 80.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[ai-open-data-center/08_Supporting load smoothing, Fault Ride-through (FRT), and back up in 80.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]

## 指标与条件
p.14 AC Galaxy UPS示例含120%/100ms、80%/0.5s、30%/0.5s profile及1%/s ramp；是既有AC经验，不是800VDC系统已经同样验证。p.15持续平滑30–75%、1Hz、50%duty：不同LMO/LFP充电C-rate和cabinet/MW带来7–10年厂商预估；不能脱离profile引用寿命。[[ai-open-data-center/08_Supporting load smoothing, Fault Ride-through (FRT), and back up in 80.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]] [[ai-open-data-center/08_Supporting load smoothing, Fault Ride-through (FRT), and back up in 80.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]
