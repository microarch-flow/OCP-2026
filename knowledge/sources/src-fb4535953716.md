# Clock-Aligned Telemetry for AI Clusters: From BMC to GPU to Fleet

## 核心结论
可操作的遥测不只是时间戳+数值：必须携带支持状态、时钟映射、freshness、Window of Uncertainty（WOU）和原始证据。没有这些条件，把不同host的GPU stall与network pause排成因果顺序可能是时钟伪影。[[Time Appliances/Clock-Aligned Telemetry for AI Clusters- From BMC to GPU to Fleet.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[Time Appliances/Clock-Aligned Telemetry for AI Clusters- From BMC to GPU to Fleet.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[Time Appliances/Clock-Aligned Telemetry for AI Clusters- From BMC to GPU to Fleet.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 关键证据
- 六条证据通道：GPU、host、BMC、scheduler placement、fabric、time；单一GPU utilization无法解释host压力或功耗/热状态。[[Time Appliances/Clock-Aligned Telemetry for AI Clusters- From BMC to GPU to Fleet.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- 样本契约包含identity、observed_at/received_at/time-map、metric+unit+typed value+quality、workload/topology、API/原始证据/errors/schema。[[Time Appliances/Clock-Aligned Telemetry for AI Clusters- From BMC to GPU to Fleet.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- unsupported/inaccessible/disabled/unavailable应显式区分，不能以0代替。跨host/rack/fleet/global比较前先匹配单位、capability、时钟不确定窗口、工作负载和collector版本。[[Time Appliances/Clock-Aligned Telemetry for AI Clusters- From BMC to GPU to Fleet.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[Time Appliances/Clock-Aligned Telemetry for AI Clusters- From BMC to GPU to Fleet.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- actionable四门：supported、fresh、time-valid、traceable；失败则补证据而非合成看似合理的值。[[Time Appliances/Clock-Aligned Telemetry for AI Clusters- From BMC to GPU to Fleet.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[Time Appliances/Clock-Aligned Telemetry for AI Clusters- From BMC to GPU to Fleet.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 指标与条件
1秒采样是live path目标，不是证明能捕获微秒级burst；触发式forensic path用eBPF/NCCL等补细粒度证据。[[Time Appliances/Clock-Aligned Telemetry for AI Clusters- From BMC to GPU to Fleet.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] 示例+8µs/±12µs与+143ms/±160ms时钟数字明确标为illustrative，不是实验性能。[[Time Appliances/Clock-Aligned Telemetry for AI Clusters- From BMC to GPU to Fleet.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
