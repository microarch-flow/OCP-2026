# Everpure NeoCloud存储：把闪存管理上移到主机软件

## 核心结论
方案不是另一个存储API，而是在现有文件/对象/存储软件下加入host-resident DirectFlash层，聚合NAND并全局管理闪存，意图降低逐盘FTL造成的过度预留与写放大。规模化收益依赖工作负载、故障恢复及软件资源消耗。[[expo-hall-sessions/07_Hyperscale Storage Solutions for NeoClouds presented by Everpure.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[expo-hall-sessions/07_Hyperscale Storage Solutions for NeoClouds presented by Everpure.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- 图中客户端经网络接scale-out数据节点及metadata；Ceph仅作为MON/OSD/RGW分层示例，不能据此认为开源Ceph已原生接入该产品。[[expo-hall-sessions/07_Hyperscale Storage Solutions for NeoClouds presented by Everpure.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[expo-hall-sessions/07_Hyperscale Storage Solutions for NeoClouds presented by Everpure.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- DirectFlash可配置IU size和flash type，宣称提高有效容量、韧性并降低over-provisioning/write amplification。文件无WA、耐久或尾延迟数值。[[expo-hall-sessions/07_Hyperscale Storage Solutions for NeoClouds presented by Everpure.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 开发周期缩短3–6月、存储效率+10–20%、exabyte级多源NAND均为厂商主张：未定义效率是有效/原始容量、功率还是成本，也未给相同保护级别/读写负载基线。[[expo-hall-sessions/07_Hyperscale Storage Solutions for NeoClouds presented by Everpure.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- 产品路线把FlashArray/FlashBlade的block/file/object与hyperscale的“客户自有filesystem/object store”分开，迁移对象是底层闪存管理而非必然替换上层语义。[[expo-hall-sessions/07_Hyperscale Storage Solutions for NeoClouds presented by Everpure.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 图表与架构
已核p7：DirectFlash位于Storage Software和一组闪存模块之间；“global”至少指节点所聚合NAND，图未证明跨整个集群统一FTL。因此不推断任意跨机NAND池化。[[expo-hall-sessions/07_Hyperscale Storage Solutions for NeoClouds presented by Everpure.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
