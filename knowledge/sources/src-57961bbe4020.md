# Node-to-Rack Efficiency for Real-World AI Inference Arm & FuriosaAI for Scalable Open Infrastructure

## 核心结论
Arm/Furiosa主张CPU orchestration与专用推理加速器协同，而不是只优化GEMM；但本讲最具体RNGD吞吐/能效数据来自**x86 host**，不能当成Arm联合系统已经测得的性能。[[ai-clusters/Node-to-Rack Efficiency for Real-World AI Inference Arm & FuriosaAI for Scalable Open Infrastructure.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[ai-clusters/Node-to-Rack Efficiency for Real-World AI Inference Arm & FuriosaAI for Scalable Open Infrastructure.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]] [[ai-clusters/Node-to-Rack Efficiency for Real-World AI Inference Arm & FuriosaAI for Scalable Open Infrastructure.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]

## 关键证据
联合方案为5U双Arm AGI CPU加8RNGD，和p.10既有RNGD NXT 4U系统不是同一配置。软件清单含llm-d、NIXL、Mooncake/LMCache及CCL等，需要区分支持方向与已完成验证。[[ai-clusters/Node-to-Rack Efficiency for Real-World AI Inference Arm & FuriosaAI for Scalable Open Infrastructure.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[ai-clusters/Node-to-Rack Efficiency for Real-World AI Inference Arm & FuriosaAI for Scalable Open Infrastructure.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]] [[ai-clusters/Node-to-Rack Efficiency for Real-World AI Inference Arm & FuriosaAI for Scalable Open Infrastructure.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 指标与条件
RNGD厂商列512TFLOPS FP8、48GB HBM3、1.5TB/s、180W、PCIe5；NXT 8卡为4PF/384GB/3kW，属于产品规格/厂商主张，不是整套Arm实测。[[ai-clusters/Node-to-Rack Efficiency for Real-World AI Inference Arm & FuriosaAI for Scalable Open Infrastructure.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
p.12 Qwen3-32B FP8、4卡、1K输入/1K输出、vLLM-compatible且x86 host：20/30/40tok/s用户SLO对应63/31.6/15.3 users/kW；30tok/s处约1950tok/s、TTFT0.35s。没有说明TTFT为p99，也没有Arm host结果。[[ai-clusters/Node-to-Rack Efficiency for Real-World AI Inference Arm & FuriosaAI for Scalable Open Infrastructure.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
p.8联合目标机型含每CPU最多136核/300W、24DIMM/6TB、8Gen5×16插槽、6×2700W PSU；PSU额定功率不是整机用电。p.14<30kW、8nodes/64cards为目标机架。[[ai-clusters/Node-to-Rack Efficiency for Real-World AI Inference Arm & FuriosaAI for Scalable Open Infrastructure.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[ai-clusters/Node-to-Rack Efficiency for Real-World AI Inference Arm & FuriosaAI for Scalable Open Infrastructure.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]

## 图表与架构
已看配置与p.12性能脚注，确认跨host限制。“60% agent steps是CPU工作”不是60%端到端时间；1.2–1.4×每usable thread改善混合测量与projection，不能作为同任务整机加速。[[ai-clusters/Node-to-Rack Efficiency for Real-World AI Inference Arm & FuriosaAI for Scalable Open Infrastructure.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
