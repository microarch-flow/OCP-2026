# 可信Agentic固件流水线：可复现构建先于生成、签名和发布

> 阅读范围：已阅读本文件全部可提取文字（18页）。关键页已视觉核对：14；不代表逐页视觉审核。

## 核心结论

- 模块化HPM/DC-SCM/riser组合把固件集成变成平台交付瓶颈；作者的解法不是单个聊天机器人，而是版本化上下文→确定构建→AI辅助→安全产物→Agent编排的分层。[[Open Platform Firmware (OPF)/Trusted, AI-Ready Open Firmware- From Auditable Foundations to Agentic Lifecycle Automation.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[Open Platform Firmware (OPF)/Trusted, AI-Ready Open Firmware- From Auditable Foundations to Agentic Lifecycle Automation.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]
- Agent可build/test/stage，但信任不由模型自创：merge/sign/promote/deploy保留人工门禁，权限分离限制Build签名、Security改代码。[[Open Platform Firmware (OPF)/Trusted, AI-Ready Open Firmware- From Auditable Foundations to Agentic Lifecycle Automation.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]][[Open Platform Firmware (OPF)/Trusted, AI-Ready Open Firmware- From Auditable Foundations to Agentic Lifecycle Automation.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]

## 关键证据

- 仓库持有AGENTS.md、scoped rules、playbook、guardrail及真实日志证据入口，openSIL POC拟作OPF贡献。[[Open Platform Firmware (OPF)/Trusted, AI-Ready Open Firmware- From Auditable Foundations to Agentic Lifecycle Automation.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[Open Platform Firmware (OPF)/Trusted, AI-Ready Open Firmware- From Auditable Foundations to Agentic Lifecycle Automation.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[Open Platform Firmware (OPF)/Trusted, AI-Ready Open Firmware- From Auditable Foundations to Agentic Lifecycle Automation.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 构建要求manifest、固定commit、隔离toolchain和相同输入相同digest；SBOM与image digest绑定，产物签名/证明/SBOM作为不可变bundle。[[Open Platform Firmware (OPF)/Trusted, AI-Ready Open Firmware- From Auditable Foundations to Agentic Lifecycle Automation.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[Open Platform Firmware (OPF)/Trusted, AI-Ready Open Firmware- From Auditable Foundations to Agentic Lifecycle Automation.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]][[Open Platform Firmware (OPF)/Trusted, AI-Ready Open Firmware- From Auditable Foundations to Agentic Lifecycle Automation.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
- 所提AI边界是自托管推理、源和原理图不出enclave、prompt/context审计；不是所有AI固件工具天然具备这些限制。[[Open Platform Firmware (OPF)/Trusted, AI-Ready Open Firmware- From Auditable Foundations to Agentic Lifecycle Automation.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[Open Platform Firmware (OPF)/Trusted, AI-Ready Open Firmware- From Auditable Foundations to Agentic Lifecycle Automation.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
