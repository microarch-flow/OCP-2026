# MEGMEET 800VDC：rack、power-center与SST三条部署路径

## 核心结论
方案将既有48/54V机柜、480VAC→800V侧柜、集中power center及中压SST并置，强调迁移而非单一替换。需要分别核实输入转换、储能、800V配电及IT柜降压的容量/冗余。[[expo-hall-sessions/Engineering-the-Transition-to-800-VDC-presented-by-MEGMEET.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Engineering-the-Transition-to-800-VDC-presented-by-MEGMEET.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Engineering-the-Transition-to-800-VDC-presented-by-MEGMEET.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Engineering-the-Transition-to-800-VDC-presented-by-MEGMEET.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- rack方案为480VAC输入，110kW风冷AC→800V电源与BBU，GPU柜再800→54V；p6/7后级标50V，属于需确认的输出版本/范围，不自行视作完全相同料号。[[expo-hall-sessions/Engineering-the-Transition-to-800-VDC-presented-by-MEGMEET.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Engineering-the-Transition-to-800-VDC-presented-by-MEGMEET.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 已看p7四象限：110kW/6RU风冷AC→800V，含fuse/contactor及约200W 50V辅助输出、热插拔/N+1；100kW/5RU液冷AC→800V称EDPP180%负载、无风扇、UQD08；未给180%持续时间。[[expo-hall-sessions/Engineering-the-Transition-to-800-VDC-presented-by-MEGMEET.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 90kW/1RU风冷800→50V有6×15kW模块、19英寸机架/可选21英寸支架、峰值最高98%；95kW/1RU液冷后级列CAN/PMBus/USB、ORing、black-box、N+1及IEC62368主张。认证与效率不得跨四个产品互相套用。[[expo-hall-sessions/Engineering-the-Transition-to-800-VDC-presented-by-MEGMEET.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 图表与架构
已核p7/8。p8上半是多机柜power-center布局，下半并列传统变压器+480VAC电源柜与室内SST选项；其中“MVDC-LVDC Transformer”文字与35/13.8kV AC输入、480V AC输出不符，应索取单线图，不照抄为DC变压器实物。[[expo-hall-sessions/Engineering-the-Transition-to-800-VDC-presented-by-MEGMEET.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/expo-evidence-to-deployment]]：与LITEON比较须统一电源柜可用容量、冷却方式及后级电压，而不是比110与220kW铭牌。分散与集中路径各自改变故障域和维护停机范围。

## 不确定性与待验证
缺量产时间、完整效率曲线/辅助功率、BBU时长、冗余切换和动态波形。产品支持热插拔不代表可绕过高压安全程序；文件无现场可靠性或推理p99证据。
