# 03_Beyond Physical Digital Twins Why Logical Digital Twins Are Essential 

## 核心结论
NVIDIA/Keysight区分logical digital twin与单纯performance simulation：前者运行较真实软件/API以验证配置、编排、隔离和变更，后者估计性能影响。多种仿真应共存，不能一个模型同时证明所有事实。[[ai-clusters/03_Beyond Physical Digital Twins Why Logical Digital Twins Are Essential .pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[ai-clusters/03_Beyond Physical Digital Twins Why Logical Digital Twins Are Essential .pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]] [[ai-clusters/03_Beyond Physical Digital Twins Why Logical Digital Twins Are Essential .pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]

## 关键证据
logical twin进入plan/deploy/operate与CI/CD，每次基础设施变更先验证；p.13–14视觉图把front-end、安全过滤、DPU、LLM、RAG/storage链作为完整系统，并以simulate→validate→analyze闭环定位瓶颈。[[ai-clusters/03_Beyond Physical Digital Twins Why Logical Digital Twins Are Essential .pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[ai-clusters/03_Beyond Physical Digital Twins Why Logical Digital Twins Are Essential .pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[ai-clusters/03_Beyond Physical Digital Twins Why Logical Digital Twins Are Essential .pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]] [[ai-clusters/03_Beyond Physical Digital Twins Why Logical Digital Twins Are Essential .pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]

## 指标与条件
p.8声称6months→1.5weeks、24×faster validation；p.9另称60–70%faster与3–6months早go-live，缺样本/人员/任务范围，不能合成一个统一加速比。p.9 twin setup30min而近重复p.10表为10min；两页标题也minutes/hours不同，保留内部不一致。表内80K→100K+tokens/s为emulation context，不证明实GPU推理加速。[[ai-clusters/03_Beyond Physical Digital Twins Why Logical Digital Twins Are Essential .pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[ai-clusters/03_Beyond Physical Digital Twins Why Logical Digital Twins Are Essential .pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]] [[ai-clusters/03_Beyond Physical Digital Twins Why Logical Digital Twins Are Essential .pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
