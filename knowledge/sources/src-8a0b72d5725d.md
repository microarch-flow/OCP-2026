# PPS 轻量化冷板与歧管：质量收益已展示，寿命资格仍待补全

> 原始题名：Rack-Scale-Weight-Reduction-and-Reliability-Validation-for-Liquid-Cooled-Server-Systems-Using-Lightweight-Materials。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 13 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- Wiwynn 将减重集中在冷板上盖、机内歧管和管材，而保留承担热传导的金属底板；不是把整个冷板变成低导热塑料。 [[cooling-environments/Rack-Scale-Weight-Reduction-and-Reliability-Validation-for-Liquid-Cooled-Server-Systems-Using-Lightweight-Materials.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]、[[cooling-environments/Rack-Scale-Weight-Reduction-and-Reliability-Validation-for-Liquid-Cooled-Server-Systems-Using-Lightweight-Materials.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]、[[cooling-environments/Rack-Scale-Weight-Reduction-and-Reliability-Validation-for-Liquid-Cooled-Server-Systems-Using-Lightweight-Materials.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]、[[cooling-environments/Rack-Scale-Weight-Reduction-and-Reliability-Validation-for-Liquid-Cooled-Server-Systems-Using-Lightweight-Materials.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- 示例冷板减重 66%，机内歧管减重 52%；这是部件级对比，不能等同整机架质量、地板载荷或部署费用减少同样比例。 [[cooling-environments/Rack-Scale-Weight-Reduction-and-Reliability-Validation-for-Liquid-Cooled-Server-Systems-Using-Lightweight-Materials.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- 材料数据展示 PPS+GF 在 20–80°C 保留较高弯曲强度与模量，但这不证明长期浸液、蠕变、接缝、压力循环及热循环均已通过。 [[cooling-environments/Rack-Scale-Weight-Reduction-and-Reliability-Validation-for-Liquid-Cooled-Server-Systems-Using-Lightweight-Materials.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]、[[cooling-environments/Rack-Scale-Weight-Reduction-and-Reliability-Validation-for-Liquid-Cooled-Server-Systems-Using-Lightweight-Materials.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]、[[cooling-environments/Rack-Scale-Weight-Reduction-and-Reliability-Validation-for-Liquid-Cooled-Server-Systems-Using-Lightweight-Materials.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]、[[cooling-environments/Rack-Scale-Weight-Reduction-and-Reliability-Validation-for-Liquid-Cooled-Server-Systems-Using-Lightweight-Materials.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
- 明确给出的初步结果是两个部件在 100 psi 下无泄漏，上盖平面度变化 0.0018 mm；完整可靠性测试在总结中仍被列为计划。 [[cooling-environments/Rack-Scale-Weight-Reduction-and-Reliability-Validation-for-Liquid-Cooled-Server-Systems-Using-Lightweight-Materials.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]、[[cooling-environments/Rack-Scale-Weight-Reduction-and-Reliability-Validation-for-Liquid-Cooled-Server-Systems-Using-Lightweight-Materials.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]

## 不确定性与待验证

- 需获得完整原始质量、热阻/压降前后数据、样本数、失效分布和保持载荷下蠕变结果。
- PG25 相容性应按具体 PPS 牌号、玻纤、助剂、连接件及流体添加剂验证；不能对所有聚合物概括。
- 轻量化 CAPEX 收益还需包含模具、装配、检测、维修与地板/搬运改造，演讲无币种、单价和回收期。
