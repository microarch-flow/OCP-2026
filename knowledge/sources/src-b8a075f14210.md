# Copper Interconnect Innovations for Realizing 400G Electrical

## 核心结论
400G/lane铜通道将长距离部分交给twinax，连接器/过渡成为限制。本文提供elastomeric CPC与twinax-to-twinax初样的S-parameter证据，而不是完整400G实流量/误码合规报告。[[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]] [[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]

## 关键证据
stubless CPC、360°shielding与1.9×1.7mm diff-pair pitch是密度/SI手段。twinax mating可用于cable backplane或front DAC，也可接paddle card承载AOC/AEC/ACC。[[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]

## 指标与条件
- 300mm CPC–CPC assembly，VNA测到110GHz，展示raw与de-embedded IL/ILD、RL、mode conversion。**p.5所称120+GHz无共振，与p.6实测110GHz仪器上限应分开**，不能写“实测120GHz”。[[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 5次remove/reseat，min-max SI差0.51dB；这是小样本重复装配，不是大量插拔寿命/量产公差验证。[[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- Crosstalk结果为2×3 diff-pair subset的**simulation**，2个NEXT+3个FEXT邻居；峰移110→120GHz且约低15dB，不代表完整高radix系统总串扰。[[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- cable-backplane初测段330mm31AWG+940mm27AWG+330mm31AWG及两个mated connectors；LR front-panel初测240+525+240mm，含CPC/前面板连接器。两种组合不能按同长度比较。[[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]] [[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]

## 图表与架构
p.14仍称preliminary sample、制造tolerance正在优化；PAM6@90GHz“near-term viable”、PAM4@112GHz“within reach”是展望，不是已完成全链路qualified。[[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]] [[server/Copper-Interconnect-Innovations-for-Realizing-400G-Electrical.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/compute-memory-channel-evidence|高速通道证据分层]]：测量reference plane、de-embedding、邻近通道数量、温度和机械状态必须进入模型元数据。

## 不确定性与待验证
无TX/RX/FEC/BER、eye margin、误码分布或系统吞吐。OIF/IEEE活动只说明规范制定中；本笔记不把行业速率名称等同于每lane实测payload。
