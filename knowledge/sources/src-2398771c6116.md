# Ciena 相干光：光谱效率、功率密度与整纤部署速度

## 核心结论
相干光没有单一最佳形态：受fiber容量限制时performance transponder可能胜出；受空间/功率限制时pluggable可能更合适；大量纤维重复交付时full-spectrum把“每波长”改为“每纤维”部署单元。优化目标不同，不应只按pJ/bit选型。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The Future of Coherent Optics in the AI Era.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The Future of Coherent Optics in the AI Era.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The Future of Coherent Optics in the AI Era.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The Future of Coherent Optics in the AI Era.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- 2250km Tasman Express、44000ps/nm色散示例：Nano29×800G@150GHz=23.2Tb/s，Extreme27×1T@162GHz=27Tb/s（+16%）。这是两种模式/频谱设计组合，不是同波长间隔下单纯芯片提速。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The Future of Coherent Optics in the AI Era.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
- Metro示例Nano64×800G@150GHz=51.2T，Extreme48×1.6T@200GHz=76.8T（+50%）；未给该页确切距离、功率和OSNR，不能推成所有100km线路容量。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The Future of Coherent Optics in the AI Era.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- AI scale-across实例称数百fiber pairs、每pair64×800G、C+L、800ZR+ interoperable PCS；无客户/期间/可用性测量，因此只记录架构规模陈述。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The Future of Coherent Optics in the AI Era.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- Direct-to-plug integrated coldplate去除dry/sliding接触层，相比riding heatsink/coldplate降低热路径阻抗；不自动证明全设施省水/低PUE。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The Future of Coherent Optics in the AI Era.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- 51.2T C+L full-spectrum是“could look like”概念：4×8channel服务模块，每channel1.6T、每服务模块2×6.4T NPO client界面；不能写成已发布产品。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The Future of Coherent Optics in the AI Era.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

Mini-QD贡献仅abstract获接受且in progress，不是已完成OCP规范。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The Future of Coherent Optics in the AI Era.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]] 需按纤维稀缺度、光谱、reach、故障换件粒度、扩容步长与部署工时比较TCO，未有价格数据。
