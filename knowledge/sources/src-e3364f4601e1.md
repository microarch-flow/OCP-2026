# 从单相到两相：工质替换之外的系统工程

> 原始题名：System-level Challenges and Considerations in Transitioning from Single-Phase to Two-Phase Cooling。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 13 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- 两相转型同时改变工质、流动稳定性、串并联分配、汽液管理和CDU结构；潜热优势不自动转化为更低结温、更小CDU或更低泵功。 [[Cooling：Cold Plate/System-level Challenges and Considerations in Transitioning from Single-Phase to Two-Phase Cooling.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/System-level Challenges and Considerations in Transitioning from Single-Phase to Two-Phase Cooling.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/System-level Challenges and Considerations in Transitioning from Single-Phase to Two-Phase Cooling.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/System-level Challenges and Considerations in Transitioning from Single-Phase to Two-Phase Cooling.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/System-level Challenges and Considerations in Transitioning from Single-Phase to Two-Phase Cooling.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- AVC/Wiwynn在各自PG25设计冷板及相同一次侧条件的实验中，PG25热表现优于R515B或R1233zd(E)。这是冷板/系统适配的重要反例，不是对优化后两相技术的最终排名。 [[Cooling：Cold Plate/System-level Challenges and Considerations in Transitioning from Single-Phase to Two-Phase Cooling.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- 低压冷媒允许较低部件压力等级，但相同压降引起更大的饱和温降，可能压缩与设施水的换热温差；回流汽体积、NPSH与均流措施决定实际部署空间。 [[Cooling：Cold Plate/System-level Challenges and Considerations in Transitioning from Single-Phase to Two-Phase Cooling.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/System-level Challenges and Considerations in Transitioning from Single-Phase to Two-Phase Cooling.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/System-level Challenges and Considerations in Transitioning from Single-Phase to Two-Phase Cooling.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/System-level Challenges and Considerations in Transitioning from Single-Phase to Two-Phase Cooling.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]

## 不确定性与待验证

- 尚需两相优化冷板的公平对照、瞬态载荷、跨机架稳定性与失效恢复测试。
- 缺少工质全生命周期排放/泄漏率、材料寿命、维护技能和完整CAPEX/OPEX模型。
