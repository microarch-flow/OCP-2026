# The Future of Fuel Cells in Datacenters - High Temperature Fuel Cells

## 核心结论
Microsoft/Google/Net Zero Innovation Hub panel材料把高温fuel cell定位为长期供能与800VDC集成候选；natural-gas发电、替代燃料、CCS、reversible LDES的成熟度不同，不能用“已部署GW”覆盖所有应用。[[ai-open-data-center/The Future of Fuel Cells in Datacenters - High Temperature Fuel Cells.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[ai-open-data-center/The Future of Fuel Cells in Datacenters - High Temperature Fuel Cells.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[ai-open-data-center/The Future of Fuel Cells in Datacenters - High Temperature Fuel Cells.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]

## 关键证据
p.7作者TRL矩阵把natural-gas power generation置于TRL9，reversible LDES约TRL6–7，替代燃料/CCS约TRL7–8；ARL仍列high cost与few mature suppliers。技术可工作与可规模采购/运维是不同问题。[[ai-open-data-center/The Future of Fuel Cells in Datacenters - High Temperature Fuel Cells.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 指标与条件
p.10厂商资料汇编给SOFC约300–330kW/module、LHV/net AC电效率寿命口径约53–65%、平均约54%，heat recovery后total efficiency>90%；不能把总热电效率当电效率。p.8另称hybrid cycles电效率90%+，缺具体系统/实验，应标待验证而不沿用作采购收益。[[ai-open-data-center/The Future of Fuel Cells in Datacenters - High Temperature Fuel Cells.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[ai-open-data-center/The Future of Fuel Cells in Datacenters - High Temperature Fuel Cells.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
p.11 SOFC25/50/100MW/acre分别单层/两层/四层stack，不能把四层机组占地与全站燃气、储存、维护区域混为一谈；p.12 Li-ion制造量由2500GWh/年除4h转625GW/年，只是比较假设而非统一交付能力。[[ai-open-data-center/The Future of Fuel Cells in Datacenters - High Temperature Fuel Cells.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]] [[ai-open-data-center/The Future of Fuel Cells in Datacenters - High Temperature Fuel Cells.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]

## 图表与架构
已看readiness、效率、占地与integration。p.18把fuel cell、battery、ultracap按long/medium/short duration经DC/DC并入800V rail，说明慢热系统仍需快buffer。p.24 CCUS/吸收制冷整体图是方案，不证明已达到其中排放目标。[[ai-open-data-center/The Future of Fuel Cells in Datacenters - High Temperature Fuel Cells.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]] [[ai-open-data-center/The Future of Fuel Cells in Datacenters - High Temperature Fuel Cells.pdf#page=24|PDF p.24]]
