# Open Standard to AI Factories：Supermicro DCBBS、模块化与液冷

## 核心结论
Supermicro将DCBBS分成system、rack、data-center积木；冷却选型应先回答rack负载、设施水温流量、冗余边界、新建或改造，再选择CDU。日期2026-10-12。[[executive-sessions/29_From Open Standards to AI Factories DCBBS, Modular Design, and the Lat.pdf#page=1|PDF p.1]][[executive-sessions/29_From Open Standards to AI Factories DCBBS, Modular Design, and the Lat.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[executive-sessions/29_From Open Standards to AI Factories DCBBS, Modular Design, and the Lat.pdf#page=19|PDF p.19]]
## 关键证据
rack→POD→设施热分配→pump skid→排热是一条完整热链；“N+1”不是只多一个CDU，而是要定义哪一层失效仍可持续服务。[[executive-sessions/29_From Open Standards to AI Factories DCBBS, Modular Design, and the Lat.pdf#page=20|PDF p.20]]
## 指标与条件
- 标称组合：in-rack至250kW、in-row1.8/2.5/5MW、L2A sidecar200–500kW；缺共同工况，不做横向效率排名。[[executive-sessions/29_From Open Standards to AI Factories DCBBS, Modular Design, and the Lat.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]
- 网络roadmap：102.4T pluggable目标CQ4/26，CPO目标CQ1/27；不是已出货状态。[[executive-sessions/29_From Open Standards to AI Factories DCBBS, Modular Design, and the Lat.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]
- p.8 HGX Rubin NVL8约210kW，而p.16列约227kW；配置/口径差异未解释，保留差异，不能任选一数构建TCO。[[executive-sessions/29_From Open Standards to AI Factories DCBBS, Modular Design, and the Lat.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[executive-sessions/29_From Open Standards to AI Factories DCBBS, Modular Design, and the Lat.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]
## 图表与架构
已核对p.5、7、8、16、20、21，纠正提取文本把362kW对应到错误rack的风险：p.8中VR NVL4为约362kW、VR NVL72约230kW。
## 关联与比较
与 [[knowledge/sources/src-f3ca61efcfee|Rittal模块化]] 具有相同复用逻辑，但POD接口、设施排热和冗余条件要统一后才可比较集成成本。
## 不确定性与待验证
多处图注明illustrative/final design may change。缺具体交付清单、热/电共同峰值、动态负载、客户现场可靠性；5MW→1GW是扩展架构范围，不是已交付容量。[[executive-sessions/29_From Open Standards to AI Factories DCBBS, Modular Design, and the Lat.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[executive-sessions/29_From Open Standards to AI Factories DCBBS, Modular Design, and the Lat.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
