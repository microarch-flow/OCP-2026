# From Training to Tokens- Revolutionizing AI Inference with In-Network Compute

## 核心结论
Astera把in-network compute定位为将all-reduce等归约的一部分下沉到switch，减少重复搬运；收益取决于消息尺寸、拓扑层级、partial结果驻留和同步，并不是所有MoE通信都能直接替换成归约。[[ai-clusters/From Training to Tokens- Revolutionizing AI Inference with In-Network Compute.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[ai-clusters/From Training to Tokens- Revolutionizing AI Inference with In-Network Compute.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[ai-clusters/From Training to Tokens- Revolutionizing AI Inference with In-Network Compute.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 关键证据
p.9区分push posted writes与pull reads，switch需保存partial并等待贡献者；因此buffer容量、并行context、慢rank和失败后的状态回收是架构成本。p.10画出256节点的L1/L2 PCIe fabric归约层级。[[ai-clusters/From Training to Tokens- Revolutionizing AI Inference with In-Network Compute.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]] [[ai-clusters/From Training to Tokens- Revolutionizing AI Inference with In-Network Compute.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 指标与条件
p.7小消息曲线覆盖1KB–8MB和最多256节点，纵轴标A.U.；p.8大消息8MB曲线到64节点也为A.U.。没有原始耗时、带宽或建模假设，不能读作“最高500×实测加速”或跨产品性能排序。[[ai-clusters/From Training to Tokens- Revolutionizing AI Inference with In-Network Compute.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[ai-clusters/From Training to Tokens- Revolutionizing AI Inference with In-Network Compute.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 图表与架构
已看p.7–8坐标与p.10拓扑。节点数增加时潜在通信节省与context/同步负担一起增长，必须以完整collective completion time而非单次ALU速度验收。

## 不确定性与待验证
跨协议适用性是作者主张，缺互通矩阵、浮点累加顺序/误差、故障语义、安全隔离、buffer上限及软件fallback成本。library hooks和轻量配置仍需端到端集成。[[ai-clusters/From Training to Tokens- Revolutionizing AI Inference with In-Network Compute.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]] [[ai-clusters/From Training to Tokens- Revolutionizing AI Inference with In-Network Compute.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
