# 外置激光源 ELS：部署边界独立于光引擎位置

## 核心结论
OE放哪与laser放哪是两条独立架构轴。ELS/ELSFP把激光热区与维修边界从昂贵ASIC移出，代价是光功率输送、PM fiber、连接器与共享失效域；不能把“CPO”自动等同外置laser或更可靠。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/External-Laser-Source-Architectures-for-Deployable-CPO-Based-AI-Networks.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/External-Laser-Source-Architectures-for-Deployable-CPO-Based-AI-Networks.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/External-Laser-Source-Architectures-for-Deployable-CPO-Based-AI-Networks.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- 同OE的ILS减少光接口但紧密热耦合/难维修；board-level ELS隔热却不是前面板FRU；faceplate ELSFP便于更换与spare，但增加光供电复杂度。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/External-Laser-Source-Architectures-for-Deployable-CPO-Based-AI-Networks.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- 作者建议六类验收指标：每λ功率/损耗/老化margin、laser温度/冷却、laser/module/system FIT、FRU工作流、telemetry/RAS、冗余/MTTR/workload影响。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/External-Laser-Source-Architectures-for-Deployable-CPO-Based-AI-Networks.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 30W→9W/port约3.5×仅Lumentum illustrative estimate，未给速率/模块边界；按算术30/9约3.33，不能用近似比值当高精度实测。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/External-Laser-Source-Architectures-for-Deployable-CPO-Based-AI-Networks.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]]
- DWDM UHP/ELSFP平台“成熟血统”并无本deck里的burn-in样本、失效率或寿命曲线；NVIDIA CPO宣布是应用方向而非这套系统已运营结果。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/External-Laser-Source-Architectures-for-Deployable-CPO-Based-AI-Networks.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/External-Laser-Source-Architectures-for-Deployable-CPO-Based-AI-Networks.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 不确定性与待验证
独立可换laser是否真的缩小故障影响，取决于一源喂多少OE/λ、冗余选择器与供电域。需实际故障注入、断源时降级、MTTR分布、维修是否扰动其他光口，以及泵/TEC的总功率。
