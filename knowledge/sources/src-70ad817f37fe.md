# 04_Can the Industry Sustain AI The Water-Energy Nexus Debate

## 核心结论
Water–Energy Nexus panel材料强调site water与source water一起算：dry cooling把部分耗水转为上游发电耗水，现场WUE=0不能自动称整个系统无水。[[ai-open-data-center/04_Can the Industry Sustain AI The Water-Energy Nexus Debate.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[ai-open-data-center/04_Can the Industry Sustain AI The Water-Energy Nexus Debate.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[ai-open-data-center/04_Can the Industry Sustain AI The Water-Energy Nexus Debate.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 关键证据
作者用WUEsource=(EWIF×PUE)+WUE纳入发电用水，再以流域stress factor构造WUI，提出WUC credits把核算连接到治理。这些层次是作者建议框架，不应把WUC当已经普遍实施的市场/法规。[[ai-open-data-center/04_Can the Industry Sustain AI The Water-Energy Nexus Debate.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[ai-open-data-center/04_Can the Industry Sustain AI The Water-Energy Nexus Debate.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 指标与条件
p.4示例以gal/MWh列air-cooled现场0、source300–500；evaporative现场450–740、总750–1240。来源给范围但未统一气候、发电组合、IT/设施分母和年份，不能当任意站点固定排放因子；p.5WUE又使用L/IT-kWh，使用前须统一单位/边界。[[ai-open-data-center/04_Can the Industry Sustain AI The Water-Energy Nexus Debate.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[ai-open-data-center/04_Can the Industry Sustain AI The Water-Energy Nexus Debate.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 图表与架构
已看site/source表、指标层次和流域边界。WUC图要求registry、third-party verification、additionality、避免double-counting且把发电厂纳入；这是治理设计，不是硬件节能测试。[[ai-open-data-center/04_Can the Industry Sustain AI The Water-Energy Nexus Debate.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 不确定性与待验证
无panel发言实录；p.7水价/建设用水/自定义water-positive批评属演讲论点，未作本地法规与价格外核。具体设计需站点水源、气象和电力组合数据。[[ai-open-data-center/04_Can the Industry Sustain AI The Water-Energy Nexus Debate.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[ai-open-data-center/04_Can the Industry Sustain AI The Water-Energy Nexus Debate.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
