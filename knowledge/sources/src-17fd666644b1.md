# OCP Sustainability 与 DCF 项目更新：工作流、已有输出与未来路线图区分

## 核心结论
这是多位项目负责人提供的 **2026 年工作快照**，把循环性、隐含碳、功耗指标、风险与韧性连成一套工作流；它能帮助寻找证据与合作入口，但不能把所有列出的路线图当成已完成标准或已实现减排。[[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=1|PDF p.1]][[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]

## 关键证据
- OpRes 旨在将气候数据转成机电/IT 的失效机理与运行阈值；材料称 Google、Meta、Microsoft、Schneider Electric、USRA 参与启动，不提供已验证的阈值表。[[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 隐含碳基础披露规范标为 2025 年 8 月发布；Marketplace 中集成的是披露准备度功能。设备 PCR 初稿正在征求反馈，公共咨询与批准的目标为 **2027 年中**，不能当成已生效统一规则。[[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- 循环性指南与数据清除指南列为可用；垂直循环生态白皮书审阅完成但仍待发布，循环性碳核算指南尚计划启动。PUE/IUE/CUE 仍包含定义改进工作。[[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- Clean Backup 0.3 标为已发布，1.0 目标 2026 Q4；燃料电池 Q3–Q4 2026、铝空气 Q4 2026–Q1 2027 属测试计划，不是结果报告。[[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]][[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]]
- ILM 关心 EPD 输入差异、PCR 可比性与单指标优化造成的取舍；低碳混凝土框架/现场试点材料列为已发布，但本 PDF 没有展开其实验数据。[[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]][[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]

## 指标与条件
本文件没有足以核算项目净节能、净节水或净减碳的统一基线。p.17 的冷却比较表区分现场/上游/总水、运行/隐含/总碳，分别使用 U/D/? 表示增/减/待评估；蒸发冷却可能降低能耗却提高现场耗水，**总水与总碳并未被表格宣称一定下降**。[[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]

## 关联与比较
阅读顺序可按“定义数据字段 → 收集供应链证据 → 用真实运行数据建模 → 做碳/水/成本权衡 → 规定退出与再利用”展开，而不是按组织工作流各自独立优化。该顺序是跨工作流归纳。[[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]][[Sustainability/Updates from an exciting year with the OCP Sustainability Project, DCF Sustainability Project.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]
