# MRC: A Purpose-Built RDMA Transport for AI Training Networks

## 核心结论
MRC将多路径与拥塞控制作为一个反馈系统：路径选择改变拥塞信号，拥塞控制又改变路径负载。异构NIC互通最终需要**反应时序兼容**，不仅是同一wire format。[[networking/MRC- A Purpose-Built RDMA Transport for AI Training Networks.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[networking/MRC- A Purpose-Built RDMA Transport for AI Training Networks.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]][[networking/MRC- A Purpose-Built RDMA Transport for AI Training Networks.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]

## 关键证据
- SMaRTT以window控制结合ECN、RTT与可选trimming，分别处理underload、steady-state与overload。[[networking/MRC- A Purpose-Built RDMA Transport for AI Training Networks.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- ReSS维护少量active subflows，随机替换最拥塞subflow，CC在flow层工作；不是将所有包无条件等概率喷洒。[[networking/MRC- A Purpose-Built RDMA Transport for AI Training Networks.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- 规范缺口：reserved bits归属、retransmission/RTO策略、从data/SACK/probe何处测RTT。Level1格式→Level2算法→Level3信号频率/反应延迟/动态行为。[[networking/MRC- A Purpose-Built RDMA Transport for AI Training Networks.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]][[networking/MRC- A Purpose-Built RDMA Transport for AI Training Networks.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]

## 指标与条件
- 生产Cluster A：GB200，50K/75K GPU jobs，ConnectX-8 800Gb/s拆4×200Gb/s planes，两层Clos、Spectrum-4与Tomahawk-5混合；作者称链路flap影响可忽略，但没有每次恢复时延分布。[[networking/MRC- A Purpose-Built RDMA Transport for AI Training Networks.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- Cluster B是另一个accelerated 20-link-failure实验，图显示约1.5Tb/s双向吞吐在links-down/restored附近基本保持；不能把该曲线当75K生产job曲线。[[networking/MRC- A Purpose-Built RDMA Transport for AI Training Networks.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- p.6的p99 10.5µs/p99.9 13µs是**前置Fungible FCP all-to-all工作**，不是本MRC生产集群尾延迟，更不是推理request p99。[[networking/MRC- A Purpose-Built RDMA Transport for AI Training Networks.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 不确定性与待验证
训练规模证明可部署性，不证明所有mixed-NIC公平性或推理小流p99。应验证不同NIC信号节奏、RTO、选择性重传、hotspot和故障反应时间；p.15“No Go-Back-N”是拟互通要求，不能仅凭格式一致跳过行为验收。
