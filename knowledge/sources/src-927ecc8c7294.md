# Unified Full-Stack Telemetry for AI Systems- From Silicon to Cluster

## 核心结论
Intel提议统一full-stack telemetry的身份、时间基准与语义，而不是替换PLDM/Redfish/OTel等传输或采集器。真正缺口是跨层join：同一组件、同一时刻、同一指标定义。[[ai-clusters/Unified Full-Stack Telemetry for AI Systems- From Silicon to Cluster.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[ai-clusters/Unified Full-Stack Telemetry for AI Systems- From Silicon to Cluster.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[ai-clusters/Unified Full-Stack Telemetry for AI Systems- From Silicon to Cluster.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 关键证据
Normalization contract包括canonical component ID、声明clock/offset/skew、unit/scope/sampling方法，并关联job/rank。in-band、OOB与bulk snapshots统一后仍可导出OTLP/Prometheus，保留原owners与工具。[[ai-clusters/Unified Full-Stack Telemetry for AI Systems- From Silicon to Cluster.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[ai-clusters/Unified Full-Stack Telemetry for AI Systems- From Silicon to Cluster.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[ai-clusters/Unified Full-Stack Telemetry for AI Systems- From Silicon to Cluster.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]] [[ai-clusters/Unified Full-Stack Telemetry for AI Systems- From Silicon to Cluster.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 指标与条件
p.9从rack thermal excursion→单device throttle→straggler→collective阻塞是**明确标注illustrative的构造场景**，没有声称测得performance或time-to-resolution改善。不能据图宣称部署后MTTR下降。[[ai-clusters/Unified Full-Stack Telemetry for AI Systems- From Silicon to Cluster.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
