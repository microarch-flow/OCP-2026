# µLED Interconnects: Addressing the Future of Scale-In and Scale-Up Connectivity

## 核心结论
Avicena用1–8Gb/s大规模并行µLED避免高速SerDes/DSP/多电平调制，目标是以面积阵列突破shoreline限制。系统收益依赖两端都采用native wide interface；仅换光模块并不自动去掉现有高速SerDes。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/LED-Interconnects-Addressing-the-Future-of-Scale-In-and-Scale-Up-Connectivity.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/LED-Interconnects-Addressing-the-Future-of-Scale-In-and-Scale-Up-Connectivity.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 关键证据
µLED+PD+microlens+fiber bundle/MCF形成scale-in/scale-up统一构想；与laser相比无threshold current，可用array spare lanes，但未给冗余机制/修复覆盖测试。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/LED-Interconnects-Addressing-the-Future-of-Scale-In-and-Scale-Up-Connectivity.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/LED-Interconnects-Addressing-the-Future-of-Scale-In-and-Scale-Up-Connectivity.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/LED-Interconnects-Addressing-the-Future-of-Scale-In-and-Scale-Up-Connectivity.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]

## 指标与条件
p.8宣称>10Tb/s/mm shoreline、>10Tb/s/mm²、<1pJ/bit、>200°C、<$0.10/Gbps；这些缺少完整系统边界、temperature lifetime与报价条件，不能当采购保证。Near-zero latency不等于光传播零延迟。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/LED-Interconnects-Addressing-the-Future-of-Scale-In-and-Scale-Up-Connectivity.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
实际展示1Tbps LightBundle eKit：335channels×3Gb/s、约1mm² µLED array、客户partner实验室评估。1.6–6.4T engines coming soon，purpose-built NPO/CPO future，不能将路线图记为已量产。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/LED-Interconnects-Addressing-the-Future-of-Scale-In-and-Scale-Up-Connectivity.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/LED-Interconnects-Addressing-the-Future-of-Scale-In-and-Scale-Up-Connectivity.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

没有BER/eye测试条件、寿命曲线、全部link energy测量、manufacturing yield、repair工时。评审需补ASIC wide IO面积、clock/skew、spare-lane控制与fiber bundle端接成本。
