# Dense, Reliable, and Energy Efficient Interconnects for AI

## 核心结论
Ranovus强调scale-up光学的决策轴是radix、shoreline密度、error/flap、量产与TCO，不仅单lane速率。laser/mux/split位置决定共享故障域，应与energy一并设计。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Dense-reliable-and-energy-efficient-interconnects-for-AI.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Dense-reliable-and-energy-efficient-interconnects-for-AI.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Dense-reliable-and-energy-efficient-interconnects-for-AI.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 关键证据
MSA速度快但审查范围较窄，SDO更细但慢；快速标准化需要明确application requirements，而非固定过早技术路线。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Dense-reliable-and-energy-efficient-interconnects-for-AI.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Dense-reliable-and-energy-efficient-interconnects-for-AI.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] 200G/fiber可由200G单波长或4×50G BiDi达成；electrical D2D/50G/200G选项会引入shoreline或gearbox代价，COI-BiDi1明确speculative。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Dense-reliable-and-energy-efficient-interconnects-for-AI.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 指标与条件
p.3为**假想72GPU、100GBd PAM4 pod**：scale-out72links×800G=57.6T、scale-up2592×400G=1036.8T，图采用4/6/10/15pJ/bit方案。文中“scale-out约1/10”是概括，表中此例约1/18；不要把柱图当部署实测或整机功耗比例。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Dense-reliable-and-energy-efficient-interconnects-for-AI.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]

## 图表与架构
已核p.9 DFB array、DFB+mux、comb、comb+SOA分配结构；在modulator入光相同假设下，作者主张mux/split进入PIC、ELS与OE尽量1:1，以减共享故障/非线性/高功率连接器风险。没有同平台实测证明哪种laser绝对更可靠。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Dense-reliable-and-energy-efficient-interconnects-for-AI.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
