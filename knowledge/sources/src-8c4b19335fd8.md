# 02_Automation-Ready Infrastructure Design From Light to Compute

## 核心结论
Google/Microsoft/Meta 的 automation-native 设计把光模块、线缆、托盘与机架的物理操作视为 AI 扩容瓶颈。机器人可靠性取决于上游机械/标识/接口设计，不只是末端部署一个机器人。[[ai-open-data-center/02_Automation-Ready Infrastructure Design From Light to Compute.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[ai-open-data-center/02_Automation-Ready Infrastructure Design From Light to Compute.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]

## 关键证据
光学接口应有刚性可抓位置、统一标签/拉环、lead-in、positive seating verification 和可预测线缆管理。[[ai-open-data-center/02_Automation-Ready Infrastructure Design From Light to Compute.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] 托盘要减少 handle engagement/retention 的自由度和摆角，提供视觉基准、前置 retention 与被动定位，避免线缆遮挡 touch points。[[ai-open-data-center/02_Automation-Ready Infrastructure Design From Light to Compute.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]] [[ai-open-data-center/02_Automation-Ready Infrastructure Design From Light to Compute.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]] [[ai-open-data-center/02_Automation-Ready Infrastructure Design From Light to Compute.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
液冷托盘的 quick-disconnect 与重量会影响自动化和人体工学；验收覆盖 deployment QA、运行健康、故障确认与修后验证。[[ai-open-data-center/02_Automation-Ready Infrastructure Design From Light to Compute.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[ai-open-data-center/02_Automation-Ready Infrastructure Design From Light to Compute.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]

## 指标与条件
“design for 3mm accuracy”是 general tolerance 的设计建议，不是机器人定位实测精度/成功率。无节省人时、MTTR、故障率、投资回报或吞吐数据。[[ai-open-data-center/02_Automation-Ready Infrastructure Design From Light to Compute.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 图表与架构
本卡只审阅提取文字，不从现场照片推定自动化成熟度、机械结构或识别成功率。

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/ai-infra-deployment-readiness]]：自动化应与 supplier qualification、commissioning、serviceability 同时设计。双重可操作性要求同时支持人和机器人，安全/人体工学不能因追求无人化而省略。[[ai-open-data-center/02_Automation-Ready Infrastructure Design From Light to Compute.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]

## 不确定性与待验证
两份 optics/fiber 与 server automation-native 白皮书仍为“stay tuned”；不能当作已经发布完整规范。需要具体连接器/FRU 的 tolerancing、验证传感、恢复路径与真实动作成功率。[[ai-open-data-center/02_Automation-Ready Infrastructure Design From Light to Compute.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]]
