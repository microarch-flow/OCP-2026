# SONiC for OTN：将开放 NOS 延伸至光传送设备

## 核心结论
开放北向接口的 gray-box 不等于开放 NOS；此方案试图把 SAI、SWSS/Syncd、监控和管理框架一起复用于光放大器、线路系统、未来 transponder，重点是可运维性而不是改变光传输物理性能。[[networking/09_SONiC for OTN Expanding SONiC and OCP SAI into Optical Transport Netwo.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[networking/09_SONiC for OTN Expanding SONiC and OCP SAI into Optical Transport Netwo.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[networking/09_SONiC for OTN Expanding SONiC and OCP SAI into Optical Transport Netwo.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- OTN 对象被列为 SAI experimental，与现有 IP/packet 对象没有重叠交互；共用 switch root 和 CRUD 模型，并不意味 OTN 是包转发 ASIC。[[networking/09_SONiC for OTN Expanding SONiC and OCP SAI into Optical Transport Netwo.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[networking/09_SONiC for OTN Expanding SONiC and OCP SAI into Optical Transport Netwo.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- Phase 1 OA/VOA 的 PR #2217 已合并；Phase 2 完整 open line system（OCM/OTDR/WSS/APS/OSC/port）进行中；Phase 3 transponder/logical/optical channel 尚计划。不能把全套 OTN 写成已完成。[[networking/09_SONiC for OTN Expanding SONiC and OCP SAI into Optical Transport Netwo.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- SWSS/Syncd 的 OTN orchestration、FlexCounter、序列化，以及多数据库北向接口仍为进行中 PR；多槽位设备的每张 optical linecard 映射一个 ASIC namespace。[[networking/09_SONiC for OTN Expanding SONiC and OCP SAI into Optical Transport Netwo.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]][[networking/09_SONiC for OTN Expanding SONiC and OCP SAI into Optical Transport Netwo.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
- KVM 可无硬件试 CLI/Restconf/gNMI；这验证软件路径而非光链路性能或保护倒换。[[networking/09_SONiC for OTN Expanding SONiC and OCP SAI into Optical Transport Netwo.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 不确定性与待验证
“成本下降、无锁定、管理数千设备”是主张，没有 TCO/现场故障/跨厂商一致性数据。应要求每种对象的 capability、alarm 清除语义、升级不中断条件和真实线路验证；多厂商 adapter 仍需各家投入。
