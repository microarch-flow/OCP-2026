# Molex近封装生态：共用PCB footprint不等于任意模块即插即用

## 核心结论
Molex同时支持NPO mezzanine、NPC铜缆与Open CPX，试图把PCB平台冻结时间与光/铜选型解耦。SROI是系统需求框架，OIF NPO/CPX是接口工作；不能因参与同一生态就认为机械、电源、PHY与管理全部互换。[[expo-hall-sessions/Enabling-an-Open-Near-Package-Ecosystem-for-AI-Scale-Up-presented-by-Molex.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Enabling-an-Open-Near-Package-Ecosystem-for-AI-Scale-Up-presented-by-Molex.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Enabling-an-Open-Near-Package-Ecosystem-for-AI-Scale-Up-presented-by-Molex.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- Mirror Mezz Enhanced self-mating、5mm stack、224G capable，沿用已在OAI/OAM1.0/1.5量产的家族架构；历史家族量产不等于所有224G NPO配置均已获得同样现场经验。[[expo-hall-sessions/Enabling-an-Open-Near-Package-Ecosystem-for-AI-Scale-Up-presented-by-Molex.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- Gemini 8×13外框、100DP容量：64DP=32Tx+32Rx支持6.4T，加8DP=4Tx+4Rx支持7.2T；16DP用于32个单端控制pin，12DP用于36A电源。8×13=104但图四角留空，实际100；不是100条224G数据lane。224G是信号能力，6.4/7.2T按200G有效lane标称。[[expo-hall-sessions/Enabling-an-Open-Near-Package-Ecosystem-for-AI-Scale-Up-presented-by-Molex.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Enabling-an-Open-Near-Package-Ecosystem-for-AI-Scale-Up-presented-by-Molex.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- CuOperate共享host land-pattern、差分对/接地/escape/机械基准，但NPO和NPC用不同receptacle：NPC有螺钉保持、应力释放；NPO有额外电源/sideband/导向。共footprint不代表不换连接器就现场直接换介质。[[expo-hall-sessions/Enabling-an-Open-Near-Package-Ecosystem-for-AI-Scale-Up-presented-by-Molex.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Enabling-an-Open-Near-Package-Ecosystem-for-AI-Scale-Up-presented-by-Molex.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
- 示意16×64DP=1024DP，总512Tx+512Rx，以200Gb/s计算**102.4Tb/s每方向**。不能再把Tx/Rx相加冒称204.8T单向ASIC容量。[[expo-hall-sessions/Enabling-an-Open-Near-Package-Ecosystem-for-AI-Scale-Up-presented-by-Molex.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]
- Si-Fly HD是Molex获许可的Open CPX互操作连接器系统；二供协议/模具承诺有助供应链，但不等于所有光引擎的协议/寿命验证。[[expo-hall-sessions/Enabling-an-Open-Near-Package-Ecosystem-for-AI-Scale-Up-presented-by-Molex.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]

## 图表与架构
已核p11/12/14，pin-map注明proposed。Module placement、铜/光与tight/discrete integration是三个独立优化轴，不是从铜到NPO到CPO的一条必然路线。[[expo-hall-sessions/Enabling-an-Open-Near-Package-Ecosystem-for-AI-Scale-Up-presented-by-Molex.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Enabling-an-Open-Near-Package-Ecosystem-for-AI-Scale-Up-presented-by-Molex.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]
