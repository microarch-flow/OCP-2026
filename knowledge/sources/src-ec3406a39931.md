# Evolution of Congestion Control in AI Ethernet Fabrics

## 核心结论
Broadcom把AI Ethernet拥塞控制演化描述为DCQCN之后的receiver-driven RCCC与sender-driven NSCC；真正应比较的是反馈信号、控制闭环RTT、启动行为和重传，而非“有无PFC”的单项标签。[[ai-clusters/Evolution of Congestion Control in AI Ethernet Fabrics.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[ai-clusters/Evolution of Congestion Control in AI Ethernet Fabrics.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[ai-clusters/Evolution of Congestion Control in AI Ethernet Fabrics.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[ai-clusters/Evolution of Congestion Control in AI Ethernet Fabrics.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 关键证据
RCCC允许line-rate启动、首个RTT后按credit发送，并用trimmed packet触发NACK与带credit重传；作者限定在充分供给的AI fabric。NSCC用RTT和ECN调window，包含比例/指数增大与乘法减小。两者机制不同，不代表在任意超售或WAN条件下等效。[[ai-clusters/Evolution of Congestion Control in AI Ethernet Fabrics.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[ai-clusters/Evolution of Congestion Control in AI Ethernet Fabrics.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 指标与条件
“一RTT收敛”“steady-state last-hop queue为0且full utilization”是作者机制/目标陈述，没有本演讲实验曲线、模型、样本量和p99支撑，不能当普遍保证。[[ai-clusters/Evolution of Congestion Control in AI Ethernet Fabrics.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 图表与架构
已看p.8、10控制图。p.12 trim保留优先转发的小包并由接收端NACK；p.13 CSIG承载min available bandwidth、min capacity、max hop delay等沿途聚合信号。信号可用不等于算法已收敛。[[ai-clusters/Evolution of Congestion Control in AI Ethernet Fabrics.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]] [[ai-clusters/Evolution of Congestion Control in AI Ethernet Fabrics.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/comparisons/ai-infra-mrc-uet-and-fabric-layers]]。本讲p.5明确PCIe瓶颈不在讨论范围，不能外推为端到端无拥塞；结合[[knowledge/concepts/ai-infra-observability-contract]]检查NIC—HBM路径。[[ai-clusters/Evolution of Congestion Control in AI Ethernet Fabrics.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 不确定性与待验证
缺混合RTT、公平性、incast恢复及控制稳定性数据。PFC作用于priority，不宜把简化幻灯片的“整条链路阻塞”描述当精确协议定义。
