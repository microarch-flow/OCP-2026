# 高速铜与光互连共设计：被动互连视角

## 核心结论
- Amphenol 主张尽早离开 PCB、从封装附近启动铜/光通道，以把损耗预算留给可达距离；比较对象不是“铜或光”单选，而是模块化位置与 RAS 的折中。[[executive-sessions/14_Co-Designing High-Speed Copper and Optical Interconnects.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[executive-sessions/14_Co-Designing High-Speed Copper and Optical Interconnects.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[executive-sessions/14_Co-Designing High-Speed Copper and Optical Interconnects.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 在全 lane 活动下，串扰可能比单 lane 损耗更限制线性光链路；单通道漂亮 BER 不足以证明整交换机可用。[[executive-sessions/14_Co-Designing High-Speed Copper and Optical Interconnects.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
## 关键证据
- NPO 电通道预算示意约 9 dB，前面板 XPO-LPO 约 19 dB；后者可维护性较好，前者电损耗与密度较好。[[executive-sessions/14_Co-Designing High-Speed Copper and Optical Interconnects.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 64-lane XPO-LPO 开发例：3nm switch host、host+cable 15 dB IL，单 lane BER 1e-10–1e-12，全64 lane约1e-7；该差距归因串扰，未给所有测量条件。[[executive-sessions/14_Co-Designing High-Speed Copper and Optical Interconnects.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
## 指标与条件
- 53GHz、40dB预算、0.8m Twinax 14dB/m 的 package-to-package 铜通道约用22dB，余18dB；3.8m@6dB/m 是下一代介质推导，不是同线缆实测距离。[[executive-sessions/14_Co-Designing High-Speed Copper and Optical Interconnects.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
- 光链路示例 500m SMF 总预算2.5dB，六个可拆连接约1.8dB、光纤0.25dB；接头类型和清洁度决定余量。[[executive-sessions/14_Co-Designing High-Speed Copper and Optical Interconnects.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
- 四柜256 GPU概念：每GPU64 lanes，16铜/48光；总4096铜、12288光 lanes，不能把 lane 计数直接当双向 Tbps。[[executive-sessions/14_Co-Designing High-Speed Copper and Optical Interconnects.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]][[executive-sessions/14_Co-Designing High-Speed Copper and Optical Interconnects.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]
