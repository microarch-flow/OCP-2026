# DCEM：PUE 可能掩盖服务器风扇与利用率的真实能耗

## 核心结论
这份24页文件包含服务器功耗拆解、SLAC/Stanford多层测量案例和DCEM工作流更新。重要结论是PUE分母包含服务器风扇，减少IT内部开销可能让PUE变差，却让总能耗降低。[[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]][[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=22|PDF p.22]]

## 关键证据
- 示例假设hyperscaler/colo利用率60%/15%，基础PUE1.1/1.3；DLC每服务器分摊20 W CDU，来自200 W泵/10服务器，非所有机柜通用值。[[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 32°C示例：风冷IT387 W+非IT110 W，总497 W，PUE1.28；DLC IT355 W+非IT110 W+CDU20 W，总485 W，PUE1.37。绝对功率更低但PUE更高，不能只按PUE排名。[[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- 测量/估算方法分别为PSU效率曲线、风扇转速立方、扣除损耗和风扇后的idle、运行负载后的剩余compute；分量并非全部独立直接测得。[[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 指标与条件
SNN表中Neuro ASIC峰值2.883 W、29.877 J，Ryzen7为60.2 W、18.479 J；低峰值功率不等于更低任务能量。矩阵乘表GPU15.076 J对ASIC45.329 J；负载改变可改变能效排序，模型/矩阵规模、精度未完整披露。[[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 图表与架构
p.5的IUE公式仍以PSU loss+fans+idle+compute为分母，重点是把IT内部份额显式拆开，不宜仅凭此页声称已形成独立、定稿的有效计算效率指标。温度/声场图只表示给定点位与测量条件；风扇曲线中的RAM、SSD及fan部分含估算。[[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]][[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]][[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]][[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=19|PDF p.19]][[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=20|PDF p.20]][[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]]

## 关联与比较
应把服务器/设施功耗分解与推理tasks/s@SLA连起来：冷却位置变化不应通过移动PUE分母掩盖真实能量；同时记录利用率和绝对电量。

## 不确定性与待验证
gPUE0.1、HDCC0.2、IUE0.1.0在此是白皮书/草案状态，计划2027完成可用指标；不能当作全部已正式采用的规范。测量方法和外部论文未独立复核。[[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=22|PDF p.22]][[Sustainability/DCEM workstream update- Case studies of metric measurements from servers to data centers.pdf#page=23|PDF p.23]]
