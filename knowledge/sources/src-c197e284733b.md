# 聚合与级联电源架：可用容量、故障域和控制权限的权衡

> 原始题名：Scaling Hyperscale Power Delivery- Managing Aggregated and Cascaded Power Shelf Architectures。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 11 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- 可用 GPU 功率不能用所有电源铭牌相加：冗余、温度降额、电流路径和预留共同限制可调度容量，且本地可用容量可能无法跨区共享。 [[Rack & Power/Scaling Hyperscale Power Delivery- Managing Aggregated and Cascaded Power Shelf Architectures.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]、[[Rack & Power/Scaling Hyperscale Power Delivery- Managing Aggregated and Cascaded Power Shelf Architectures.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]、[[Rack & Power/Scaling Hyperscale Power Delivery- Managing Aggregated and Cascaded Power Shelf Architectures.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
- 聚合架构扩展共享池，但可能扩大公共母线故障域；级联/分区可限制影响范围，却增加协调点并可能产生闲置容量。本文没有证明某一种架构普遍更优。 [[Rack & Power/Scaling Hyperscale Power Delivery- Managing Aggregated and Cascaded Power Shelf Architectures.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]、[[Rack & Power/Scaling Hyperscale Power Delivery- Managing Aggregated and Cascaded Power Shelf Architectures.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
- 机架协调器分配功率、优先级、爬坡和有效期；快速电源本地控制与保护始终有最终权限，不能依赖失联或过期的中央预算维持安全。 [[Rack & Power/Scaling Hyperscale Power Delivery- Managing Aggregated and Cascaded Power Shelf Architectures.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]、[[Rack & Power/Scaling Hyperscale Power Delivery- Managing Aggregated and Cascaded Power Shelf Architectures.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 故障恢复应按隔离→稳定电压/电流/温度→重新分配→渐进恢复验证；容量利用率、瞬态、影响域、恢复时间需要分别测试。 [[Rack & Power/Scaling Hyperscale Power Delivery- Managing Aggregated and Cascaded Power Shelf Architectures.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
