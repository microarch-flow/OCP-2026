# Breaking the Walls：面向 AI 的开放 Scale-Across

## 核心结论
- Cisco/Meta 区分传统 DCI 的独立业务互通与 scale-across 的同一协调 AI 作业：后者把站间网络放到训练关键路径，掉速可能让大量 accelerator 等待。封面为 2026 年 10 月。[[executive-sessions/11_Breaking the Walls Open Scale-Across for the AI Era presented by Cisco.pdf#page=1|PDF p.1]][[executive-sessions/11_Breaking the Walls Open Scale-Across for the AI Era presented by Cisco.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]][[executive-sessions/11_Breaking the Walls Open Scale-Across for the AI Era presented by Cisco.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]
- Meta 用光纤真实 RTT 而非地理距离组织区域 ring；区域共享服务栈可以省基线资源，却把本地访问变成骨干流量，形成算力、电力和网络的新权衡。[[executive-sessions/11_Breaking the Walls Open Scale-Across for the AI Era presented by Cisco.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[executive-sessions/11_Breaking the Walls Open Scale-Across for the AI Era presented by Cisco.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]][[executive-sessions/11_Breaking the Walls Open Scale-Across for the AI Era presented by Cisco.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
## 关键证据
- RBB ring 图标出 1/5/10 ms RTT，并自述首对试点释放数 MW 基线功率；没有公开具体 workload 或能量计量。[[executive-sessions/11_Breaking the Walls Open Scale-Across for the AI Era presented by Cisco.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- 3 km FR、10 km LR、>10 km ZR+DWDM 是该架构的 reach 选择；长距离会同时引入光学保护路径、拥塞控制及训练并行策略问题。[[executive-sessions/11_Breaking the Walls Open Scale-Across for the AI Era presented by Cisco.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[executive-sessions/11_Breaking the Walls Open Scale-Across for the AI Era presented by Cisco.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
## 指标与条件
- 光学图列无 ILA 120–150 km、有 ILA 500 km+；不是所有模块/光纤条件下的距离保证。[[executive-sessions/11_Breaking the Walls Open Scale-Across for the AI Era presented by Cisco.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
- coherent pluggable 对比传统 transponder 架构标称 CAPEX -66%、能源 -90%、空间 >65% 节省，并引 ACG 的 TCO 40–60%；未给完整测算假设，不能套用于整个 AI 数据中心。[[executive-sessions/11_Breaking the Walls Open Scale-Across for the AI Era presented by Cisco.pdf#page=22|PDF p.22]]
- multi-rail OLS 比较列 350W/4×C+L rail 对 350W/单 C+L rail，属特定设备口径。[[executive-sessions/11_Breaking the Walls Open Scale-Across for the AI Era presented by Cisco.pdf#page=23|PDF p.23]]

## 不确定性与待验证
缺少跨站训练损失、token/step 吞吐、光纤路由冗余实际相关性以及故障切换 SLO；更远 reach 不意味着所有模型并行方式仍高效。
