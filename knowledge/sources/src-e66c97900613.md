## 核心结论

研究把OCP从开放部件扩展到chip-to-grid的系统平台，指出下一阶段价值应由集成reference architecture、cluster validation与跨vendor互操作体现。市场规模是研究模型，不是OCP收入或已交付订单。[[market-impact-adoption/Designing-Rack-Scale-AI-The-OCP-Advances-its-Vision-of-a-Single-Chip-to-Grid-AI-Production-System.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[market-impact-adoption/Designing-Rack-Scale-AI-The-OCP-Advances-its-Vision-of-a-Single-Chip-to-Grid-AI-Production-System.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[market-impact-adoption/Designing-Rack-Scale-AI-The-OCP-Advances-its-Vision-of-a-Single-Chip-to-Grid-AI-Production-System.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]][[market-impact-adoption/Designing-Rack-Scale-AI-The-OCP-Advances-its-Vision-of-a-Single-Chip-to-Grid-AI-Production-System.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[market-impact-adoption/Designing-Rack-Scale-AI-The-OCP-Advances-its-Vision-of-a-Single-Chip-to-Grid-AI-Production-System.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]

## 关键证据

- Rack从机械外壳转向compute/cooling/power/telemetry的平台；network从独立设备走向scale-up/out与光互连；power转向grid-to-chip架构。[[market-impact-adoption/Designing-Rack-Scale-AI-The-OCP-Advances-its-Vision-of-a-Single-Chip-to-Grid-AI-Production-System.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[market-impact-adoption/Designing-Rack-Scale-AI-The-OCP-Advances-its-Vision-of-a-Single-Chip-to-Grid-AI-Production-System.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[market-impact-adoption/Designing-Rack-Scale-AI-The-OCP-Advances-its-Vision-of-a-Single-Chip-to-Grid-AI-Production-System.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- 多种cooling与legacy retrofit并存，说明不是所有采用都要求新建高密度机房。[[market-impact-adoption/Designing-Rack-Scale-AI-The-OCP-Advances-its-Vision-of-a-Single-Chip-to-Grid-AI-Production-System.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
- conclusion强调buildblocks之后必须验证single-system AI、展示互操作，且需覆盖更广rack尺寸/环境。[[market-impact-adoption/Designing-Rack-Scale-AI-The-OCP-Advances-its-Vision-of-a-Single-Chip-to-Grid-AI-Production-System.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]][[market-impact-adoption/Designing-Rack-Scale-AI-The-OCP-Advances-its-Vision-of-a-Single-Chip-to-Grid-AI-Production-System.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]

## 指标与条件

模型预测OCP-recognized infrastructure/solutions spending从2025的$154B到2030的$892B、CAGR42%；类别包括servers及其accelerators/interconnect/memory等，不可再把组件金额额外相加。预测不是保证。[[market-impact-adoption/Designing-Rack-Scale-AI-The-OCP-Advances-its-Vision-of-a-Single-Chip-to-Grid-AI-Production-System.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]]

2026survey中31%称已用液冷、33%计划12个月内实施；后者不能计为已部署。未给样本n、地区/行业分层，不能把比例直接乘全球rack数。[[market-impact-adoption/Designing-Rack-Scale-AI-The-OCP-Advances-its-Vision-of-a-Single-Chip-to-Grid-AI-Production-System.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
