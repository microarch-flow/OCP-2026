# OpenCPX：可更换近封装接口与铜光兼容

## 核心结论
OpenCPX把可维护性、铜光共用和短电通道结合，试图在前面板模块与不可拆光封装之间提供选择；它不是单一硅光/激光/光纤PHY规范。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/CPO-solution-for-AI-that-checks-all-the-boxes.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/CPO-solution-for-AI-that-checks-all-the-boxes.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/CPO-solution-for-AI-that-checks-all-the-boxes.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/CPO-solution-for-AI-that-checks-all-the-boxes.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- 可承载SMF硅光（ELS或集成laser、WDM/MCF）、MMF VCSEL，也支持flyover DAC、ACC/AEC和RF转换；模块接口开放不保证这些光学技术彼此兼容。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/CPO-solution-for-AI-that-checks-all-the-boxes.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/CPO-solution-for-AI-that-checks-all-the-boxes.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
- 两种connector/module类型分离或集成power，32/36lane，列举Ethernet、OCI、PCIe/CXL等；这是封装接口承载能力，不是协议透明互通已测。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/CPO-solution-for-AI-that-checks-all-the-boxes.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
- 短host通道15dB对比32dB、ERL10dB对比8.5dB；声称65%节能、SiPh3.8pJ/bit、VCSEL1–1.9pJ/bit，主要引用外部2025/2026材料，缺同一system boundary，不当作本演讲新增实测。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/CPO-solution-for-AI-that-checks-all-the-boxes.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/CPO-solution-for-AI-that-checks-all-the-boxes.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- 可靠性引用Meta15M device-hours/36M与Oracle讨论，不能重复计为独立现场证据；device-hours与“unserviceable MTBF”定义需要回原始实验。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/CPO-solution-for-AI-that-checks-all-the-boxes.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
