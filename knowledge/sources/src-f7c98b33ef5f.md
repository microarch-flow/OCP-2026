# 欧洲 OCP 采用障碍：技术兴趣不自动转化为可采购可支持的平台

> 阅读范围：已阅读本文件全部可提取文字（16页）。未完成图表视觉核对；不把文字提取视作图形关系验证。

## 核心结论

运营方经验指出采用漏斗的瓶颈不只是技术：19英寸既有设施、技能、5年24/7/4小时响应期望、融资、迁移成本、MOQ与本地渠道会决定采购。开放可降低锁定，但可能把集成/生命周期责任转给买方。[[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]][[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]][[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 关键证据

- ScaleUp从2rack试点开始、再迁移更多workload和建立experiencecenters；这是渐进采用案例，不是全欧洲统计。[[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
- 门高/楼板/机柜合同是physicalfit障碍；OpenBMC、busbar、ODM integration是技能障碍；spares/warranty/firmwareownership是运营责任障碍。[[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]][[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
- 小批量价格优势会被MOQ和stockingdistributor稀缺侵蚀；multi-vendor spec-based procurement也可能不适配原有招标流程。[[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 指标与条件

2experiencecenters、14products solutionprovider与3integratedofferings是该公司业务范围；不是采用率。6PSUs vs60是架构简化举例，没有相同负载/冗余/效率的定量对照。[[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 关联与比较

与Marketplace认可、OAC企业生命周期和RackRenew方案构成商业落地链：发现产品→现场试用→采购融资→迁移→长期维护。技术开放的TCO应扣除前三年迁移/培训/库存等实际成本。后者为分析建议，不是已给测量。

## 不确定性与待验证

“迁移吃掉第一年节省”是来源经验判断，无金额或样本。没有说明所有OCP设备都不具OEM级SLA，也不代表所有欧洲客户都有同一障碍。供应商/集成商支持合同需逐项核实。[[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]][[market-impact-adoption/Overcoming OCP Adoption Barriers in European Data Centers.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
