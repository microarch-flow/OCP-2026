# LLM 冷却控制模拟：能效奖励改善并不保证热安全

> 阅读范围：已阅读本文件全部可提取文字（33页）。关键页已视觉核对：14, 16, 21, 24, 27, 29；不代表逐页视觉审核。

## 核心结论

最重要结果不是“AI节能”，而是 **所有agent能效都改善，但多动作/多agent时多数跨过热阈值**。这是chip-to-chiller模拟器的探索，材料也明确真实BAS、多episode和GW级验证尚未完成；不能称已部署3,360GPU自动冷却控制。[[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]][[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=24|PDF p.24]][[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=27|PDF p.27]][[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=29|PDF p.29]][[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=30|PDF p.30]]

## 关键证据

- 四级耦合模型为B200RCthermalgrid/DVFS、coldplate、CDU、air-cooled chiller，以真实8×B200 telemetry及设备曲线校准，模拟420nodes×8GPU、2MWIT。[[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]
- 8个zero-shot模型，动作每600s一次，每episode10次=6000s；单chiller setpoint、加pump、拆两agent是三种情形。CHWS15–30°C是试验动作范围，不是通用运行建议。[[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]][[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=19|PDF p.19]]
- 92°C是episode硬终止，85°C是结果“safe/hot”使用的throttle threshold。没有跨过92不等于保持85以下；两个边界必须分开。[[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]][[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=29|PDF p.29]]

## 指标与条件

单动作所有8模型低于85°C，最高reward约+10.7%、partialPUE−9.7%（baselineCHWS15°C）。双动作单agent为5/8安全，最高reward+11.5%、partialPUE−10.3%（baseline另设pump65%）。拆成两agent仅2/8安全，最高reward+10.9%。这是有限模拟场景、非统计通过率。[[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]][[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=24|PDF p.24]][[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=27|PDF p.27]][[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=29|PDF p.29]]

500MW总功率、IT325→425MW、tokens/day363T→476T的+31%是独立 **illustrative model**；不是上述2MW试验的实测收益，也未计时间电价/新增IT资本/真实应用质量。[[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]

## 关联与比较

与统一power management/SCMP相连：优化目标必须受独立安全保护、fallback、权限和控制稳定性约束。最佳reward在本例也安全不证明这种相关性普遍成立；多agent更差也不是对所有分布式控制的结论。

## 不确定性与待验证

无多episode随机种子、置信区间、sensorfailure、网络/API失联、极端天气或长期设备老化验证。BlueGeneFP64与GB300FP4sparse的跨代倍率不可作为等精度能效结论；来源本身已有该限定。[[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[market-impact-adoption/More-Tokens-Same-Megawatts-How-Agentic-AI-Rewrites-AI-Factory-Economics.pdf#page=30|PDF p.30]]
