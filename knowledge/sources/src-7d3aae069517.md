# Die-to-Die Solutions for Optical Chiplets

## 核心结论
光链路中仍有关键的 **electrical D2D边界**。选UCIe、BoW或3D direct-bond接口，需看EIC/PIC分工、retimer/FEC终止位置、fiber radix和时钟域，而非只比较总Tb/s。

## 关键证据
- pluggable/NPO把module放在ASIC附近PCB上，ASIC→module是C2C；CPO将光chiplet与ASIC放同package，可走D2D。module内部EIC↔PIC也有D2D。[[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/Die-to-Die Solutions for Optical Chiplets.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/Die-to-Die Solutions for Optical Chiplets.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/Die-to-Die Solutions for Optical Chiplets.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 演讲所述标准UCIe PHY采用single-ended NRZ、forwarded clock、16–64data lanes/direction及对称TX/RX；BoW允许lane数/方向不对称、更灵活data-to-clock比例。此为该演讲选型讨论，完整版本限制仍需查规范。[[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/Die-to-Die Solutions for Optical Chiplets.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/Die-to-Die Solutions for Optical Chiplets.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
- SiPho例：octal212.5G C2C → UCIe-AP64×25.6G → 单fiber1.6T，标注Ethernet FEC overhead及单clock PPM。slow-wide例：8组BoW-SP×4，每组4×53.1G约200G，8fiber groups合计1.6T、8个独立clock PPM。数字是接口配置示例，不是整模块效率测量。[[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/Die-to-Die Solutions for Optical Chiplets.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/Die-to-Die Solutions for Optical Chiplets.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]
- COUPE图中top EIC经PIC TSV到substrate，再沿2.5D D2D到ASIC；3D UCIe/BoW.Flexi连接还要求EIC/PIC共同匹配bump map。[[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/Die-to-Die Solutions for Optical Chiplets.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/Die-to-Die Solutions for Optical Chiplets.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]

## 指标与条件
slow-wide示例25–50Gb/s/element，ultra-slow-wide microLED示例2–8Gb/s；tradeoff涉及fiber数、radix和clocking，而非“低lane rate一定低成本”。[[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/Die-to-Die Solutions for Optical Chiplets.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

p.4对>400G/lane、>10m铜能力的判断是讲者对future AI pod的技术定位，不能视作任意调制/重定时/线缆条件下的严格物理禁区。本材料无BER、pJ/bit、失效率或TCO实测。[[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/Die-to-Die Solutions for Optical Chiplets.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]

## 图表与架构
已视觉核对C2C/D2D位置、UCIe/BoW例、COUPE剖面与选型页。p.17认为retimed/FEC-terminated或single-fiber EIC适合UCIe，高radix direct-drive、多独立时钟域可利用BoW灵活性；这是设计建议而非所有产品的唯一选择。

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/compute-memory-chiplet-link-efficiency|有效带宽与时钟/封装边界]]；[[knowledge/concepts/compute-memory-package-memory-wall|shoreline与封装瓶颈]]。应把electrical D2D、electrical-to-optical转换和fiber分别建模，不将光传输时延覆盖掉电侧CDC/FEC。
