# LVDC 拓扑语言：危险分区、保护扇区和多源电路不能混用

> 原始题名：LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 21 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- 本演讲把三套互补语言放在一起：zone 描述危险/限能层级，sector 描述功能与服务连续性分组，circuit 描述电源连接数量。它们不是同一棵分类树，也不能互相替代。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- 电流流向与保护方向性是独立属性；允许双向能量流不等于具备双向故障切断，eFuse 也不自动提供物理隔离。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 限流型源仍可能具有极高的最初电容放电峰值；之后是否继续馈入、负载是否反灌和保护速度决定故障持续能量。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]
- 快速器件只有在源、方向性、区域隔离与选择性协同下才改变整区风险；把不同 zone 混在同一未隔离空间，不能只引用局部最快器件的较低危险等级。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/LVDC-Architecture-Fundamentals-How-Topology-Protection-and-Standardization-Define-DC-Power-Systems.pdf#page=19|PDF p.19]]
