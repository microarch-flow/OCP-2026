# Fluid Oxidation and Electronic Reliability: A Multi-Fluid EID Measurement Study

## 核心结论
500h实验室升温氧化中TAN上升，板表面电阻并未一致下降；两种在役回收液却在相近TAN下显示更低电阻。**TAN是bulk acidity指标，不能单独作板级失效/寿命代理。** 本试验明确不是service-life prediction或产品pass/fail评级。[[cooling-immersion/Fluid-Oxidation-and-Electronic-Reliability-A-Multi-Fluid-EID-Measurement-Study.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[cooling-immersion/Fluid-Oxidation-and-Electronic-Reliability-A-Multi-Fluid-EID-Measurement-Study.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[cooling-immersion/Fluid-Oxidation-and-Electronic-Reliability-A-Multi-Fluid-EID-Measurement-Study.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]

## 关键证据
8家fluid厂商贡献fresh/in-service样品，盲码报告；四种package geometry、continuous5V bias，由leakage换算表面电阻。80°C浴温、每条件500h，0/168/336/500h取液，连续电学记录；统一液量/位置、独立器具、环境/温度记录和电阻校准。不是实际服务器完整负载试验。[[cooling-immersion/Fluid-Oxidation-and-Electronic-Reliability-A-Multi-Fluid-EID-Measurement-Study.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[cooling-immersion/Fluid-Oxidation-and-Electronic-Reliability-A-Multi-Fluid-EID-Measurement-Study.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[cooling-immersion/Fluid-Oxidation-and-Electronic-Reliability-A-Multi-Fluid-EID-Measurement-Study.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[cooling-immersion/Fluid-Oxidation-and-Electronic-Reliability-A-Multi-Fluid-EID-Measurement-Study.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 图表与架构
离子、water、金属/颗粒、析出物、酸种类和服役历史都是待分解因子；TAN/FTIR/RI相似不能排除其差异。全谱ATR相关度0.9996–0.9998不等于无氧化；作者建议band-specific与确定光程。p.17区分供应商多种方法与本研究colorimetric TAN，不能假设所有表值均同ASTM D664程序。[[cooling-immersion/Fluid-Oxidation-and-Electronic-Reliability-A-Multi-Fluid-EID-Measurement-Study.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]] [[cooling-immersion/Fluid-Oxidation-and-Electronic-Reliability-A-Multi-Fluid-EID-Measurement-Study.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]

## 关联与比较

## 不确定性与待验证
field samples仅两种、年龄/环境不同，无各条件重复数/不确定度与故障阈值。不能据500h声称证明多年寿命或“氧化峰在几年后”；run-until-failure、添加材料、SI/BER后续阶段仍计划中。[[cooling-immersion/Fluid-Oxidation-and-Electronic-Reliability-A-Multi-Fluid-EID-Measurement-Study.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]] [[cooling-immersion/Fluid-Oxidation-and-Electronic-Reliability-A-Multi-Fluid-EID-Measurement-Study.pdf#page=19|PDF p.19]]
