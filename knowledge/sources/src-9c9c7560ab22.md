# Scaling Open Collaboration for Immersion Cooling Adoption

## 核心结论
41页合辑同时包含市场/采用观点、可靠性论据摘要、项目治理、社区活动与参与入口，**不是41页同一实验**。对架构研究最有价值的是：只冷却xPU可能留下memory/NIC/SSD/power conversion的分布热，以及标准/流体/维护边界影响部署采用；具体预测和可靠性倍数仍需独立证据核实。[[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]] [[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]

## 指标与条件
- p.3–4到2035的市场曲线明确forecast，标“since mid-2025 no updates”；不当事实销量或投资依据。[[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- p.9 distributed heat曲线含未来投影，air practical ceiling标illustrative；不是实测所有rack在40kW残余热必然失效。[[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- p.12的1.45×/0.96×/0.72×为连接数量/失效率假设下的白皮书模型摘要，非三个现场试验。[[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
- p.13区分3.4–5×mechanism-share model、up to12×转引3000+server/30月fleet observation、Natick1/8失败案例。**三者不能当同一浸没冷却随机对照结果**；Natick页内描述本身为nitrogen/no handling，不是直接证明fluid效果。[[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
- p.15 200MW年度成本最低/可靠性占半收益为模型案例主张；250→600kW为shipping→announced，非同等验证成熟度。[[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]

## 图表与架构
作者以“冷板受热点物理约束推动、浸没受分布负载/空间/经济性拉动”解释不同采用节奏；这是分析框架，非普遍自然定律。p.16的2029–2030 mainstream是路线图，不是确定部署日期。[[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]] [[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]

## 不确定性与待验证
未提供白皮书全部假设、fleet基线/失效定义/置信区间，不能用3–12×直接填系统可靠性模型。社区人数也有内部快照冲突：p.32称YoY23.9%，p.34由1101→1342为21.9%；p.33写March而p.17为October，不能静默合并口径。[[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]] [[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=32|PDF p.32]] [[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=33|PDF p.33]] [[cooling-immersion/OCP-Immersion-Project-Scaling-Open-Collaboration-for-Immersion-Cooling-Adoption.pdf#page=34|PDF p.34]]
