# Config-Driven FBOSS: From Board Bring-up to Qualified Platform

## 核心结论
Nexthop AI/Meta把board-specific代码转为可配置描述与topology-based tests，价值在可重复交付和可追责qualification；演讲自身结果仍有未通过与未接入测试，不能写成全栈已合格。[[networking/07_Open, AI-Ready FBOSS From Platform Bring-up to Solution Qualification .pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[networking/07_Open, AI-Ready FBOSS From Platform Bring-up to Solution Qualification .pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[networking/07_Open, AI-Ready FBOSS From Platform Bring-up to Solution Qualification .pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 关键证据
- 平台服务从每板代码变成每subsystem JSON；switch agent从约两打文件/重编译转向runtime descriptor；optics lane map按SFF-8024推导。[[networking/07_Open, AI-Ready FBOSS From Platform Bring-up to Solution Qualification .pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]
- Distro把服务、启动顺序、配置和测试装在一个artifact；CLI联动依赖配置、rollback并区分需要coldboot的变更。[[networking/07_Open, AI-Ready FBOSS From Platform Bring-up to Solution Qualification .pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
- Test-as-a-Config：single-DUT snake与multi-node topology+BGP+traffic+disruption+health checks；新平台交拓扑数据，不重新写整套脚本。[[networking/07_Open, AI-Ready FBOSS From Platform Bring-up to Solution Qualification .pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 指标与条件
- 展示AI辅助HW spec→descriptor，clean system iteration约5分钟，以及8×800G、1000 routes的多节点示例；不是证明AI自动化无需人工审核。[[networking/07_Open, AI-Ready FBOSS From Platform Bring-up to Solution Qualification .pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 25.6T Wedge800：235 tests passing、50 failing、200 being onboarded；102T TH6P：50 passing、400 being onboarded，multi-DUT未建。分母尚在增长，不宜算统一通过率。[[networking/07_Open, AI-Ready FBOSS From Platform Bring-up to Solution Qualification .pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 图表与架构
已核对p.8结果表：Wedge800不是“无Meta帮助”，TH6P列才标Done without Meta=Yes；102T只有single-DUT验证。测试发现service/firmware bugs是实际收益，尚未完成的qualification也是证据。

## 不确定性与待验证
缺少每项failure性质、阻塞严重度、生产load与SLO。应把AI生成descriptor当待验证输入；golden image、拓扑校验、故障注入与coldboot/rollback规则必须保持确定性。
