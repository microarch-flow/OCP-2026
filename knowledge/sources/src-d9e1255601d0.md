# AiVRES液冷AI数据中心：机柜规格、液路隔离与交付案例

## 核心结论
AiVRES将一次冷源、CDU、二次环网、阀门漏液控制、冷却液维护与计算机柜打包。关键不是单冷板功率，而是机架故障隔离、设施供回水条件与安装交付边界。下一代Rubin配置和欧洲B200案例应分开看。[[expo-hall-sessions/02_Aivres Liquid-Cooling AI Data Center Solutions Based on NVIDIA GPUs, p.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[expo-hall-sessions/02_Aivres Liquid-Cooling AI Data Center Solutions Based on NVIDIA GPUs, p.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[expo-hall-sessions/02_Aivres Liquid-Cooling AI Data Center Solutions Based on NVIDIA GPUs, p.pdf#page=24|PDF p.24]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- CDU标称≥1200kW，脚注工况为一次EG25 41→54°C、二次PG25 45°C供/65°C回；表主体却写60/45°C，存在条件不一致。一次最大1400L/min、二次泵≥1200L/min、压差0.3–2bar、电功率需求≤15kW。所谓“效率比近100”不是99–100%电转换效率，且不可凭1200/15直接证明实测比值。[[expo-hall-sessions/02_Aivres Liquid-Cooling AI Data Center Solutions Based on NVIDIA GPUs, p.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- VR NVL72 KRS8000V4：48U、36Vera/72Rubin、27.648TB CPU内存/20.736TB HBM4、1843.4kg；MaxQ/MaxP GPU分别1800/2300W，机柜TDP约187/227kW，EDP198/240kVA。4×110kW电源在两模式采用2+2/3+1，不能把440kW装机当耗电。[[expo-hall-sessions/02_Aivres Liquid-Cooling AI Data Center Solutions Based on NVIDIA GPUs, p.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
- 托盘2CPU+4GPU；规格写4×CX9/8OSFP，拓扑图实际画8个CX9块、4组1.6T OSFP，每组2×4×200G，与文字命名/端口计数有歧义。图中CPU各经PCIe6 x16接NIC，另有BF4南北向双400G，应索取实际BOM与lane-map。[[expo-hall-sessions/02_Aivres Liquid-Cooling AI Data Center Solutions Based on NVIDIA GPUs, p.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]] [[expo-hall-sessions/02_Aivres Liquid-Cooling AI Data Center Solutions Based on NVIDIA GPUs, p.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]
- HGX NVL8两类：KL2288 2U全液冷；KL3288 3U CPU/GPU冷板、其余风冷。p21给4/9个2U节点96/216kW，8个3U节点192kW，均为**估计TDP**。九个NVL8虽同为72GPU，不能据密度等同一个NVL72 scale-up域。[[expo-hall-sessions/02_Aivres Liquid-Cooling AI Data Center Solutions Based on NVIDIA GPUs, p.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]] [[expo-hall-sessions/02_Aivres Liquid-Cooling AI Data Center Solutions Based on NVIDIA GPUs, p.pdf#page=20|PDF p.20]] [[expo-hall-sessions/02_Aivres Liquid-Cooling AI Data Center Solutions Based on NVIDIA GPUs, p.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]]
- 商用案例自述欧洲20MW、约100柜，3×32柜POD、每POD3CDU(2+1)，6144个B200，60天模块化交付、5年NBD服务；13824PFLOP/s未给精度/稀疏性及实际应用吞吐，不作推理性能基线。[[expo-hall-sessions/02_Aivres Liquid-Cooling AI Data Center Solutions Based on NVIDIA GPUs, p.pdf#page=24|PDF p.24]]

## 图表与架构
已看p6/13/15/21/24。二次环网和双阀门使单柜维护可隔离；RJ45漏液/阀控与热功率反馈构成机柜级控制，流量“最多降50%”没有工况/样本。上层故障仍须校验环网、CDU与控制器共享失效域。[[expo-hall-sessions/02_Aivres Liquid-Cooling AI Data Center Solutions Based on NVIDIA GPUs, p.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[expo-hall-sessions/02_Aivres Liquid-Cooling AI Data Center Solutions Based on NVIDIA GPUs, p.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
