# GPU管理标准2026：遥测二进制数据面、更新Fast Fail与MinSVN拆分

> 阅读范围：已阅读本文件全部可提取文字（19页）。关键页已视觉核对：8, 17；不代表逐页视觉审核。

## 核心结论

- 离散GPU用MCTP/PLDM/SPDM，Managed Accelerator Subsystem用Redfish；不能只要求“支持Redfish”而忽略产品层次。[[systems-management/From-Scale-to-Security-Evolving-GPU-Management-Standards-in-2026.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- 遥测提议保留Redfish配置面、以FlatBuffer替代高流量JSON数据，sensor manifest和time series用sensor_id关联。[[systems-management/From-Scale-to-Security-Evolving-GPU-Management-Standards-in-2026.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[systems-management/From-Scale-to-Security-Evolving-GPU-Management-Standards-in-2026.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[systems-management/From-Scale-to-Security-Evolving-GPU-Management-Standards-in-2026.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- 更新安全两个重点：先做完整资格pre-check减少激活时失败；将MinSVN不可逆抬升与常规固件rollout解耦，保留观察期回滚空间。[[systems-management/From-Scale-to-Security-Evolving-GPU-Management-Standards-in-2026.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[systems-management/From-Scale-to-Security-Evolving-GPU-Management-Standards-in-2026.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]

## 关键证据

- Fast Fail的PreUpdateValidation为可选参数且需厂商实现，适用于full bundle；Target设备更新行为implementation-specific。[[systems-management/From-Scale-to-Security-Evolving-GPU-Management-Standards-in-2026.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
- 失败类包含缺组件/缺设备/设备断电或recovery/管理接口不可用。[[systems-management/From-Scale-to-Security-Evolving-GPU-Management-Standards-in-2026.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]
- MinSVN方案要查询active/staged SVN、RoT MinSVN并安全更新阈值；TrustedComponent上扩展仍proposal。[[systems-management/From-Scale-to-Security-Evolving-GPU-Management-Standards-in-2026.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]][[systems-management/From-Scale-to-Security-Evolving-GPU-Management-Standards-in-2026.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]
- event discovery暂用供应商schema，SubmitTestEvent可模拟后台流程但不是所有硬件故障都能注入。[[systems-management/From-Scale-to-Security-Evolving-GPU-Management-Standards-in-2026.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 指标与条件

管理接口2.0与FW update1.9为2026目标/本演讲计划，不是本文外部发布核验。无JSON→FlatBuffer压缩率、遥测采样保证数值、更新失败率或停机改善。[[systems-management/From-Scale-to-Security-Evolving-GPU-Management-Standards-in-2026.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[systems-management/From-Scale-to-Security-Evolving-GPU-Management-Standards-in-2026.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 图表与架构

已视觉核对p.8 manifest/time-series关联与p.17 RoT→受保护组件→image slots模型，明确SVN属于slot，MinSVN是受保护组件/RoT策略。

## 关联与比较

与Owner授权微码更新对照：Fast Fail解决资格检查时间，Owner auth解决权限，MinSVN解决回滚底线；三者都需要，不能用包签名一次覆盖。

## 不确定性与待验证

延后burn改善可回滚性但留下旧版可用窗口，需定义安全风险批准与burn时机；传感器ID生命周期/重启后绑定也需验证。
