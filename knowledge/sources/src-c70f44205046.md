# Vertiv 800VDC过渡：固定容量与可更换接口分别规划

## 核心结论
本讲主要提供设施迁移方法，不是转换器效率实验：楼板载荷、总供电、馈线路径、母线安培容量等难改接口应按未来规划；整流器、tap-can、rack input和液路manifold则预留可替换能力。[[expo-hall-sessions/Any-Rack-Everywhere-Future-Readiness-for-the-800-VDC-Transition-presented-by-Vertiv.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Any-Rack-Everywhere-Future-Readiness-for-the-800-VDC-Transition-presented-by-Vertiv.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- p3以145kW GB300、230–330kW Vera Rubin、570kW及1MW后续代际构成规划包络；后二者未给确定产品/日期，且来源引自其他材料。不是本讲实测或所有机柜必走路径。[[expo-hall-sessions/Any-Rack-Everywhere-Future-Readiness-for-the-800-VDC-Transition-presented-by-Vertiv.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]
- 四种路径：LVAC到rack、现有AC加800V sidecar、LVAC集中整流到pod、MV→800VDC到hall。上游资产可复用程度、转换点与备电位置不同，不应仅用“都是800V”比较。[[expo-hall-sessions/Any-Rack-Everywhere-Future-Readiness-for-the-800-VDC-Transition-presented-by-Vertiv.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- **Dual-rated busway只能AC或DC择一运行，不能同时供两种电。** 混合负载要两条独立AC/DC busway，或同一外壳内两套独立busbar的hybrid busway。[[expo-hall-sessions/Any-Rack-Everywhere-Future-Readiness-for-the-800-VDC-Transition-presented-by-Vertiv.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Any-Rack-Everywhere-Future-Readiness-for-the-800-VDC-Transition-presented-by-Vertiv.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Any-Rack-Everywhere-Future-Readiness-for-the-800-VDC-Transition-presented-by-Vertiv.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
