# 无化学添加水管理双讲：铜腐蚀台架与旁路处理现场证据的边界

> 原始题名：Chemical-Free Water Chemistry Management for Reliable Operation of Copper Cold Plates。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 26 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- 此PDF包含Goaland铜冷板水化学管理与Watts/EasyWater的旁路处理两场短讲，不是一个连续实验；要分别保存台架与建筑闭环现场证据。 [[Cooling：Cold Plate/Chemical-Free Water Chemistry Management for Reliable Operation of Copper Cold Plates.pdf#page=1|PDF p.1]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/Chemical-Free Water Chemistry Management for Reliable Operation of Copper Cold Plates.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/Chemical-Free Water Chemistry Management for Reliable Operation of Copper Cold Plates.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]
- Goaland图中展示约1500h运行的低电导、近中性pH、低DO和两块铜coupon失重；仍计划并行测试和pilot，不能当作多年机柜寿命验证。 [[Cooling：Cold Plate/Chemical-Free Water Chemistry Management for Reliable Operation of Copper Cold Plates.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/Chemical-Free Water Chemistry Management for Reliable Operation of Copper Cold Plates.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/Chemical-Free Water Chemistry Management for Reliable Operation of Copper Cold Plates.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/Chemical-Free Water Chemistry Management for Reliable Operation of Copper Cold Plates.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/Chemical-Free Water Chemistry Management for Reliable Operation of Copper Cold Plates.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- EasyWater声称旁路过滤/物理电子处理可降低DO和颗粒，但其引用现场主要是建筑闭环，真正TCS第三方/客户循环验证仍进行中。 [[Cooling：Cold Plate/Chemical-Free Water Chemistry Management for Reliable Operation of Copper Cold Plates.pdf#page=19|PDF p.19]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/Chemical-Free Water Chemistry Management for Reliable Operation of Copper Cold Plates.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]]、[[Cooling：Cold Plate/Chemical-Free Water Chemistry Management for Reliable Operation of Copper Cold Plates.pdf#page=24|PDF p.24]]

## 不确定性与待验证

- “chemical-free”描述运行添加策略，不代表零耗材、零维护或免材料兼容审查。
- 两厂商的装置、测试液、金属、温度、流速、暴露期与现场类型不同，不可并成一个证据集。
- 需要实际TCS带热通量的铜微通道、混合金属、微生物与供水变化长期对照。
