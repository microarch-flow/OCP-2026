# MHS: Onward Towards Industry Adoption — Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play (M-PNP) Tools and POC Work

## 核心结论
本讲把 M-PNP 从愿景落到可执行资料链：netlist/BOM/RTL→self-description JSON→校验/签名/FRU封装→BMC读取→管理功能。自动生成描述仍依赖命名规则、vendor-specific提取器和正确硬件源数据。[[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]

## 关键证据
JSON 描述总线参数、MUX/bridge、connectors、PD memory map/register/virtual wire。FRU可内嵌文件或指出其他位置；若FPGA模拟EEPROM，需在reset时也可读，不能依赖尚未配置好的user logic。签名防篡改并不证明描述内容正确。[[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 指标与条件
|工具|演讲时状态与输入约束|
|---|---|
|JSON validator|已发布；校验schema及跨节identifier/link，不是完整电气/行为验证。[[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=12\|PDF p.12]]|
|FRUPack|已发布；合并IPMI/DMTF DSP0220，包含JSON/cert/signature。[[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=13\|PDF p.13]]|
|Netlist→JSON|发布中，目标Q4’26；Cadence Allegro netlist+BOM+命名约定。[[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=10\|PDF p.10]]|
|fpga2json|目标lateQ4’26；先requirements模板，再结合RTL/image/build artifacts；核心解释器可为vendor-specific。[[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=11\|PDF p.11]]|
|JSON→Linux device tree|未来开发，初版目标Q1’27。[[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=14\|PDF p.14]]|

## 图表与架构
单/双node LTPI连接SCM/HPM FPGA，M-PESTI fanout扩展endpoints；p.18/19示范power management/system reset和JSON驻embedded flash。PoC是特定板卡/参考逻辑互通，不代表任意HPM全部功能免适配。[[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]] [[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]] [[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=19|PDF p.19]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/compute-memory-modular-platform-contract|模块化平台契约]]：应将数据来源、schema、真实性签名、版本和行为测试绑定。工作组overview称v0.7已发布，本讲p.22仍写targeted Oct’26，属于材料版本快照差异，不能静默统一。[[server/06_Onward Towards Industry Adoption – Demonstrating Modular Plug-and-Play.pdf#page=22|PDF p.22]]

## 不确定性与待验证
缺少覆盖矩阵、故障注入、cross-vendor规模与节省工时统计。reset可读性、错误FRU处理、签名信任链、错误电源序列如何安全拒绝仍需验证。
