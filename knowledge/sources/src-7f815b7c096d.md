# From LPO to NPO to CPO：开放光互连路线与维修边界

## 核心结论
Micas的核心不是单向替代时间表，而是按距离、密度、功耗、软件和维修模式组合pluggable/LPO/NPO/CPO；光学靠近硅的方向与具体量产时点要分开判断。[[executive-sessions/28_From LPO to NPO​ to CPO​ The open optical roadmap for AI infrastructur.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[executive-sessions/28_From LPO to NPO​ to CPO​ The open optical roadmap for AI infrastructur.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[executive-sessions/28_From LPO to NPO​ to CPO​ The open optical roadmap for AI infrastructur.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
## 关键证据
- NPO保留OE替换边界，CPO缩短电通道却把光学故障带入更昂贵系统；共同风险包括封装良率、返修、冷却、fiber清洁和软件bank mapping。[[executive-sessions/28_From LPO to NPO​ to CPO​ The open optical roadmap for AI infrastructur.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[executive-sessions/28_From LPO to NPO​ to CPO​ The open optical roadmap for AI infrastructur.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- 外置激光利于独立更换/冗余但增加光纤与管理对象；内置激光降低plumbing复杂度却将热/老化纳入OE。[[executive-sessions/28_From LPO to NPO​ to CPO​ The open optical roadmap for AI infrastructur.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
## 指标与条件
2026验证、2027原型/生态、2028早期部署及2027–2029 switch CPO增长均为演讲展望；没有给统一BER、pJ/bit、成本或现场维修率。[[executive-sessions/28_From LPO to NPO​ to CPO​ The open optical roadmap for AI infrastructur.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[executive-sessions/28_From LPO to NPO​ to CPO​ The open optical roadmap for AI infrastructur.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
## 图表与架构
已核对p.6–8、10；p.8区分OCI的光PHY、XPO的pluggable form factor、Open CPX的optical-engine边界，三者不能互换叫作同一标准。
## 关联与比较
与 [[knowledge/sources/src-6746624f9361|XPO]]、[[knowledge/sources/src-6b3929fc1d91|链路预算]] 相接：评估“bandwidth per operational risk”需要给出故障单元、备件、维护窗口和诊断接口，而非只报告功耗。
## 不确定性与待验证
这是系统厂商路线判断，非市场部署统计。需要实际OE/socket互换规则、CMIS实现、SONiC错误传播和有代表性的热循环/返修数据。
