# OpenRMC GraphQL代理：北向减少请求，不消除南向Redfish成本

> 阅读范围：已阅读本文件全部可提取文字（15页）。关键页已视觉核对：11, 12；不代表逐页视觉审核。

## 核心结论

- 提案让OpenRMC-DM提供GraphQL北向schema，在多厂商Redfish遥测支持不齐时统一字段选择/聚合，不要求改每个BMC。[[systems-management/Graph-Query-Language-APIs-for-OpenBMC-Metrics-OpenRMC-DM-as-a-Proxy-GraphQL-Server.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[systems-management/Graph-Query-Language-APIs-for-OpenBMC-Metrics-OpenRMC-DM-as-a-Proxy-GraphQL-Server.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[systems-management/Graph-Query-Language-APIs-for-OpenBMC-Metrics-OpenRMC-DM-as-a-Proxy-GraphQL-Server.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 作者明确只优化Client→DM；DM→BMC仍是既有Redfish流，不能把北向210x少请求宣传为全系统210x低负载。[[systems-management/Graph-Query-Language-APIs-for-OpenBMC-Metrics-OpenRMC-DM-as-a-Proxy-GraphQL-Server.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 关键证据

- 研究比较顺序请求且无client parallelism；只测client boundary API数与request/response payload，排除wall-clock时间和南向路径。[[systems-management/Graph-Query-Language-APIs-for-OpenBMC-Metrics-OpenRMC-DM-as-a-Proxy-GraphQL-Server.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- GraphQL先批量拿chassis/sensor名，再一次取全部Reading；不是从无上下文直接单请求全部操作。[[systems-management/Graph-Query-Language-APIs-for-OpenBMC-Metrics-OpenRMC-DM-as-a-Proxy-GraphQL-Server.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
- Redfish MetricReport本来支持多metrics；适用场景是假定目标设备该功能缺失/不一致，不能证明GraphQL优于所有Redfish优化。[[systems-management/Graph-Query-Language-APIs-for-OpenBMC-Metrics-OpenRMC-DM-as-a-Proxy-GraphQL-Server.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]

## 指标与条件

2 BMC、30 chassis、390 sensors：Redfish421次、payload227,951 B；GraphQL2次、response54,625 B+request13,924 B=68,549 B，总payload约3.3x少。Redfish请求payload计0说明不是所有HTTP/TLS线缆字节口径；不应写总网络流量必降3.3x。[[systems-management/Graph-Query-Language-APIs-for-OpenBMC-Metrics-OpenRMC-DM-as-a-Proxy-GraphQL-Server.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]][[systems-management/Graph-Query-Language-APIs-for-OpenBMC-Metrics-OpenRMC-DM-as-a-Proxy-GraphQL-Server.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]

## 图表与架构

已视觉核对p.11测量边界和p.12表格，确认2次而非1次、payload而非延迟。

## 关联与比较

与Redfish Alias/TelemetryBundle路线是不同层优化：Alias降低发现，Bundle优化流式编码，GraphQL代理解决字段选择与异构客户端契约；可以组合但需要实测总成本。

## 不确定性与待验证

p.13“exponentially fewer”没有复杂度证明，应理解为此例显著减少。需proxy缓存新鲜性、并发/限流、授权、失败语义和BMC负载数据。[[systems-management/Graph-Query-Language-APIs-for-OpenBMC-Metrics-OpenRMC-DM-as-a-Proxy-GraphQL-Server.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
