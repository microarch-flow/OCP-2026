# Flex/Lightmatter 开放硅光参考设计：从光密度到制造合同

## 核心结论
CPO架构在纸面闭合还不够，BGA/warpage、reflow后FAU装配、纤维弯曲、激光blind-mate与冷却必须共同闭合。可拆FAU消耗封装边缘空间，但更高每纤维带宽能为对准/固定机构腾空间。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/High-Speed-Open-Silicon-Photonics-Reference-Designs.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/High-Speed-Open-Silicon-Photonics-Reference-Designs.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/High-Speed-Open-Silicon-Photonics-Reference-Designs.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- p.6把纤维信息密度与封装边缘逃逸能力分开：以单排/忽略角落为假设，CPO可用约0.9Tb/s/dir/mm，socketed NPO铜逃逸约0.25；8/4/2 dFAU给768/384/192 data fibers，吞吐是随每纤维带宽变化的设计区间，不是三款已实测系统。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/High-Speed-Open-Silicon-Photonics-Reference-Designs.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/High-Speed-Open-Silicon-Photonics-Reference-Designs.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]
- **图内实测**：PPK有181 FAU，<2dB判据下99.5% yield、mean facet IL1dB；package test另为500+units、99.5%。两个分母不同，不能合并。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/High-Speed-Open-Silicon-Photonics-Reference-Designs.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- Multi-reticle ATV为4000mm²3D chip-on-wafer，62 FAU、<2dB optical attach yield95%、mean facet IL1.5dB；single-reticle的>99%yield/IL<1.5dB明确基于simulation，不是实测。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/High-Speed-Open-Silicon-Photonics-Reference-Designs.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- tray合同包括机械、电源、信号、热和管理；p.12示例CPO的FRU为package，不代表所有socketed CPO都必须换整包。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/High-Speed-Open-Silicon-Photonics-Reference-Designs.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]

## 不确定性与待验证
99.5%FAU合格率不等于多FAU整包总良率，且相关缺陷/返工可改变乘法模型。白皮书已发布，规格草案延续至2027，非完整规范定稿；未读取外部白皮书，不据其标题补内容。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/High-Speed-Open-Silicon-Photonics-Reference-Designs.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/High-Speed-Open-Silicon-Photonics-Reference-Designs.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]]
