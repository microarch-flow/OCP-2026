# Microsoft Newport：结构钢直接复用的碳收益与交付证据链

## 核心结论
Newport Imperial Park是既有钢构件直接再用的单站试点，区别于钢材回炉再生或废物转移。关键可复制能力是招标前的目标/责任分工，以及逐梁检测、认证、接受和安装追踪。[[Sustainability/Closing the Loop- Structural Steel Reuse in Hyperscale Datacenter Construction.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[Sustainability/Closing the Loop- Structural Steel Reuse in Hyperscale Datacenter Construction.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[Sustainability/Closing the Loop- Structural Steel Reuse in Hyperscale Datacenter Construction.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]

## 关键证据
- 初期约13%再用目标，最终再用249 tonnes、约占结构框架10%；其余辅以高再生含量钢，不能把13%目标记作实现值。[[Sustainability/Closing the Loop- Structural Steel Reuse in Hyperscale Datacenter Construction.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 演讲报告直接再用相对新生产节省约520 tCO₂e；钢材整体策略约4,400 tCO₂e，包含再用与再生含量，不能全归功于249吨旧钢。[[Sustainability/Closing the Loop- Structural Steel Reuse in Hyperscale Datacenter Construction.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 因缺原厂mill certificate，采用Class B再用路径、强化检测与追踪；设计要容忍库存长度/组合差异。[[Sustainability/Closing the Loop- Structural Steel Reuse in Hyperscale Datacenter Construction.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]][[Sustainability/Closing the Loop- Structural Steel Reuse in Hyperscale Datacenter Construction.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- 施工阶段无成本超支或进度影响，作者将其归因于招标阶段已纳入再用。该结论不等于检测、仓储和前期工程没有成本。[[Sustainability/Closing the Loop- Structural Steel Reuse in Hyperscale Datacenter Construction.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 指标与条件
76%是结构钢包的再生含量口径，10%是直接再用占比；>80%施工/拆解废物转移和>90%拆解转移也各有范围，不能互相替换或相加。碳估算未充分给排放因子、运输/检测边界和敏感性。[[Sustainability/Closing the Loop- Structural Steel Reuse in Hyperscale Datacenter Construction.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 图表与架构
p.8串联业主、工程/再用专业方、拆解与施工承包商；p.11把构件原位置、状态、测试、接受和最终安装关联。p.14生境+260%/树篱+620%为规划预期，非已测生态改善。[[Sustainability/Closing the Loop- Structural Steel Reuse in Hyperscale Datacenter Construction.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[Sustainability/Closing the Loop- Structural Steel Reuse in Hyperscale Datacenter Construction.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]][[Sustainability/Closing the Loop- Structural Steel Reuse in Hyperscale Datacenter Construction.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]

## 关联与比较
与IT硬件循环利用和LCA数据质量相同：复用不是只凭“旧件可用”的口号，必须有身份、状态与验收证据；缩短采购交付与降低隐含碳可以同向，但需当地供应链条件。

## 不确定性与待验证
单站结果不能证明所有地区都无成本/工期影响；扩展需供体建筑、储存、测试能力、采购条款和地域接受条件。本卡不将讲稿列出的标准视为本项目独立认证审计。[[Sustainability/Closing the Loop- Structural Steel Reuse in Hyperscale Datacenter Construction.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]
