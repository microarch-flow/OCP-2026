# Deploying Immersion Native ITE in AI Neocloud: An OCP Collaboration Story

## 核心结论
本文有三类不能混合的证据：H200实际LLM服务运行点、集群多日压力/冷却响应、Blackwell同平台浸没与DLC短时功率/链路比较。它支持具体部署可行性，但没有证明浸没令相同推理负载p99更低。[[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]] [[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]] [[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]] [[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]

## 关键证据
基于Dell XE9680重设计H100/H200 chassis、铜heatsink、电源及firmware，作者称6U→3OU、每tank14台服务器、约800W/server节省。p.6“14×H200s”应结合p.7理解为14台H200 SXM服务器，不是14颗GPU。2.5年可靠性测试支撑warranty为作者陈述，没有此页失效统计。[[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 指标与条件
### H200推理：有p99，但不是冷却优劣对照
8×H200-SXM5-141GB Denvr MSC1，GPU TDP700W；Llama-3.1-Instruct70B，TP8、continuous batching，每序列3072输入+1024输出、最多150并发。报告**8068 output tok/s、7.9 responses/s、p99完整1024-token响应19.82s**。不是TTFT、不是per-token p99；无同负载air/DLC推理基线，也未给precision、runtime、到达过程/测量时长。[[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

### Blackwell：600秒targeted-power，不是LLM benchmark
|同平台8×SXM、每GPU目标1000W|浸没35°C inlet|DLC 30°C PG25|
|---|---:|---:|
|平均GPU power|996W|995W|
|平均clock|1567MHz|1594MHz|
|峰值Tcore|77°C|64°C|
|DCGM pass|8/8|8/8|
两方案均接近功率目标、低于83°C core limit，但入口温度/介质不同；不能说散热效果或应用性能完全相同。浸没N+1 direct-flow manifold + server-managed flow，12.5–75W cooling consumption为作者给定系统范围，未完整分解facility overhead。[[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]] [[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]] [[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]

## 图表与架构
DCGMI NVBandwidth：CPU→GPU55.34GB/s双方相同，GPU→CPU57.87/57.14；directional NVLink约766–775GB/s，双向peak约1.53–1.54TB/s。独立监控28360 samples报0 PCIe replay、0 EDATL errors，不代表无限运行零故障。p.17正文“PCIe差1.27%”与标签1.28%存在舍入差异，以原值为准。[[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]
多日HPL/OEM/idle phase展示flow随IT负载变化、入口/出口ΔT约10°C；15min–1h FMEA response时间仅特定场景描述，没有完整故障矩阵。[[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]] [[cooling-immersion/Deploying-Immersion-Native-ITE-in-AI-NeoCloud-An-OCP-Collaboration-Story-Continued.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]

## 不确定性与待验证
p.4 PUE<1.04、zero-water、面积/部署周期为厂商方案主张，缺计量边界、年度季节条件和独立核验；128 GPUs与p.7的128-server scalable unit是不同口径。约800W节省不能直接推算固定SLO下tokens/J或总TCO。
