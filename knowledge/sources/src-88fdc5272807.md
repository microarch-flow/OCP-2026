# 06_From AI PC to AI Infrastructure Platform Contracts for Scalable CPU-Ac

## 核心结论
Arm强调AICC宏观部署与node-level platform contract分工：共同硬件/固件/管理基线减少每站定制，但不能替代跨域网络和orchestration。[[ai-computing-continuum/06_From AI PC to AI Infrastructure Platform Contracts for Scalable CPU-Ac.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[ai-computing-continuum/06_From AI PC to AI Infrastructure Platform Contracts for Scalable CPU-Ac.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 关键证据
节点层次包含CPU/accelerator/memory/coherency、PCIe/CXL/network、firmware/telemetry、OS/runtime，以及跨层security/isolation/power/lifecycle。Sync-Across需要可依赖的本地控制接口，并不等于Arm节点自动实现跨站一致性。[[ai-computing-continuum/06_From AI PC to AI Infrastructure Platform Contracts for Scalable CPU-Ac.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[ai-computing-continuum/06_From AI PC to AI Infrastructure Platform Contracts for Scalable CPU-Ac.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 指标与条件
没有CPU/加速器性能、功耗或成本数据；“效率、软件可移植、减少集成”是架构目的，未给量化改善。

## 图表与架构
已看p.8–9：BSA/SBSA等硬件基线、BBR/SBBR固件、BBSR/SBMR安全/管理、ACS/SystemReady验证分层。FCSA位于chiplet标准层，不能与操作系统可启动验证混成一个认证。[[ai-computing-continuum/06_From AI PC to AI Infrastructure Platform Contracts for Scalable CPU-Ac.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[ai-computing-continuum/06_From AI PC to AI Infrastructure Platform Contracts for Scalable CPU-Ac.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/ai-infra-deployment-readiness]]；[[knowledge/concepts/ai-infra-observability-contract]]。加速器设计应交付枚举/错误处理/IOMMU/firmware更新/管理计数器契约，而非只有驱动和矩阵算子。

## 不确定性与待验证
来源称SBSA/SBMR SPW已建立，未提供具体版本或某机型认证结果；采用标准/ACS工具不等于任意CPU–accelerator组合已经SystemReady或满足p99。[[ai-computing-continuum/06_From AI PC to AI Infrastructure Platform Contracts for Scalable CPU-Ac.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]] [[ai-computing-continuum/06_From AI PC to AI Infrastructure Platform Contracts for Scalable CPU-Ac.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
