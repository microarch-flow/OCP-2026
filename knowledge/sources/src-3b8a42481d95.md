# Reimagining Data Center Power: High-Density Architectures for Next-Gen AI Compute

## 核心结论
低core voltage下的kA电流使PDN R/L和散热成为加速器能力上限。VPD缩短高电流路径、释放HSIO/plane空间并支持双面冷却；需要与IBC、multiphase控制、passives和制造可靠性共同设计。[[server/Reimagining-Data-Center-Power-High-Density-Architectures-for-Next-Gen-AI-Compute.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[server/Reimagining-Data-Center-Power-High-Density-Architectures-for-Next-Gen-AI-Compute.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[server/Reimagining-Data-Center-Power-High-Density-Architectures-for-Next-Gen-AI-Compute.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 关键证据
会议展示两级方案：8:1 IBC输入44–59.5V→5.5–7.4V、约6.5V nominal；二级多相VPD。顶侧GPU+IBC、底侧VPD独立冷却，与单级48V-to-core研究路线不同。[[server/Reimagining-Data-Center-Power-High-Density-Architectures-for-Next-Gen-AI-Compute.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[server/Reimagining-Data-Center-Power-High-Density-Architectures-for-Next-Gen-AI-Compute.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 指标与条件
- 表列MI300X/MI355X/MI455X对应current density0.88/1.2/up to2 A/mm²，power density0.11/0.15/0.33 W/mm³。为作者产品/平台演进数据，无完整统一测量条件。p.3文字“连续两代Y2Y50%”不能由该跨2023/2025/2026表逐项直接验证，不另作确定年化规律。[[server/Reimagining-Data-Center-Power-High-Density-Architectures-for-Next-Gen-AI-Compute.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]
- p.4 `I²R`曲线为电阻值不同的解析损耗；<50µΩ、>2kA、>80%E2E是设计要求/主张，不是只要R<50µΩ就数学保证转换总效率。[[server/Reimagining-Data-Center-Power-High-Density-Architectures-for-Next-Gen-AI-Compute.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- 下一代目标：3A/mm²、profile<4mm、thermal resistance<1°C/W、E2E>80%。**3A/mm²是目标，不是本讲已测达到。**[[server/Reimagining-Data-Center-Power-High-Density-Architectures-for-Next-Gen-AI-Compute.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[server/Reimagining-Data-Center-Power-High-Density-Architectures-for-Next-Gen-AI-Compute.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 图表与架构
VPD缩短路径帮助降R/L，但更高密度改变cooling与failure consequences。共同footprint利于多vendor，DPPM/ELFR/telemetry强调大规模下小概率失效也重要。[[server/Reimagining-Data-Center-Power-High-Density-Architectures-for-Next-Gen-AI-Compute.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[server/Reimagining-Data-Center-Power-High-Density-Architectures-for-Next-Gen-AI-Compute.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/compute-memory-power-path-and-dynamics|供电路径与动态约束]]：对比Cambridge单级原型时需对齐效率测量边界、电流/电压/高度/温度，不能用peak90.6%直接胜负判定对比目标E2E80%。

## 不确定性与待验证
缺transient step、load-line、完整E2E损耗分解和热阻参考点。演讲路线图不作为当前产品规格保证；>2kW采用VPD是作者技术方向，不是所有设计必须遵守的标准。
