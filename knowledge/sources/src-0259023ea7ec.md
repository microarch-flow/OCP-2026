# CPACT诊断合规：统一Recipe、DRC→DRD与可定位FRU日志

> 阅读范围：已阅读本文件全部可提取文字（11页）。关键页已视觉核对：5, 6；不代表逐页视觉审核。

## 核心结论

- CPACT服务CPU impactless更新与RAS/debug/diagnostic规范，当前工作重点为诊断0.7→0.9；它检查供应商collateral能否交付/接入，不直接证明所有故障都可诊断。[[Test & Validation/Product Intercept learnings and Compliance measurement for Diagnostics Standardization.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[Test & Validation/Product Intercept learnings and Compliance measurement for Diagnostics Standardization.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- v0.9以recipe分类、DRC→DRD映射和OCPTV扩展日志把供应商工具结果变成运营方可执行维修流程。[[Test & Validation/Product Intercept learnings and Compliance measurement for Diagnostics Standardization.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[Test & Validation/Product Intercept learnings and Compliance measurement for Diagnostics Standardization.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]

## 关键证据

- DRC–DRD同文件既做静态文档又做runtime dictionary，运营方可覆盖供应商repair policy；日志包含DRC、action/urgency/confidence和hardwareInfoIds/FRU关联。[[Test & Validation/Product Intercept learnings and Compliance measurement for Diagnostics Standardization.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[Test & Validation/Product Intercept learnings and Compliance measurement for Diagnostics Standardization.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[Test & Validation/Product Intercept learnings and Compliance measurement for Diagnostics Standardization.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 供方checkout与需方checkin用同类合规报告，目标减少跨hyperscaler定制并支持分层条件诊断。[[Test & Validation/Product Intercept learnings and Compliance measurement for Diagnostics Standardization.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[Test & Validation/Product Intercept learnings and Compliance measurement for Diagnostics Standardization.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 指标与条件

示例confidence99是样例字段，不是经过校准的99%准确率；无MTTR实际降幅或CPACT测试覆盖率。[[Test & Validation/Product Intercept learnings and Compliance measurement for Diagnostics Standardization.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[Test & Validation/Product Intercept learnings and Compliance measurement for Diagnostics Standardization.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 图表与架构

已视觉核对p.5映射JSON与p.6 DiagnosticIssue extension，确认FRU定位是显式字段而不是只输出文本错误。

## 关联与比较

与GPU CTAM同属共同验收语言，但CPACT聚焦CPU诊断/更新；与诊断efficacy合读，schema通过与最终修复成功是两层指标。

## 不确定性与待验证

需case/recipe覆盖、结果码版本兼容、FRU身份稳定、confidence定义、重复故障与人工policy覆盖审计。
