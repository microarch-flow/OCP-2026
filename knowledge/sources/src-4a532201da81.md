# 软管材料对冷却液化学的影响：加速老化初步结果

## 核心结论
- Gates 的初步实验说明“过氧化物硫化 EPDM”这个材料类别不足以保证液冷化学兼容性；具体配方和制造洁净度会显著影响析出物。来源明确为 2026-10-12，且结果 directional, not final。[[executive-sessions/13_Characterization of Hose Material Effects on Coolant Chemistry During .pdf#page=1|PDF p.1]][[executive-sessions/13_Characterization of Hose Material Effects on Coolant Chemistry During .pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[executive-sessions/13_Characterization of Hose Material Effects on Coolant Chemistry During .pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
- 污染是混杂因素，不应把该实验直接读成不同匿名供应商材料固有优劣排名；第一阶段因污染疑虑结束，后续改进清洗、DI 水和泄漏控制。[[executive-sessions/13_Characterization of Hose Material Effects on Coolant Chemistry During .pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[executive-sessions/13_Characterization of Hose Material Effects on Coolant Chemistry During .pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
## 关键证据
- 方法基于 ASTM D471：1m、-8 规格软管，80°C 8 周，静态无流动；同批冷却液作 control。[[executive-sessions/13_Characterization of Hose Material Effects on Coolant Chemistry During .pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- 第二阶段方法加入冲洗、受控干燥、称重/封口、1344小时老化、漏液检查、GC-MS 和 SEM；需区分实验已执行与后续计划。[[executive-sessions/13_Characterization of Hose Material Effects on Coolant Chemistry During .pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[executive-sessions/13_Characterization of Hose Material Effects on Coolant Chemistry During .pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
## 指标与条件
- 第一阶段 DI 水非挥发残留：control 24 ppm；硫磺硫化 EPDM 538 ppm；三种 pEPDM 分别 352、6379、2062 ppm。它们不是腐蚀速率、冷板堵塞率或寿命。[[executive-sessions/13_Characterization of Hose Material Effects on Coolant Chemistry During .pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- 作者将部分金属归因制造污染和实验室 DI 水，将增塑剂析出/分解作为可能机制，不是已完成因果证明。[[executive-sessions/13_Characterization of Hose Material Effects on Coolant Chemistry During .pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 不确定性与待验证
缺少样本数、重复性、置信区间、流动/剪切作用与加速老化到现场寿命换算。需要受控第二阶段结果和真实回路故障关联。
