# Toward-Quantum-Superconducting-with-IQM-Quantum-Processors

## 核心结论
IQM把quantum supercomputer定义为hybrid workflow：QPU只是子程序加速器，HPC承担应用、mitigation/compilation和持续QEC。最有架构价值的是T/M/L三种耦合域，而不是单一“已经接入HPC”的标签。[[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Toward-Quantum-Superconducting-with-IQM-Quantum-Processors.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]] [[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Toward-Quantum-Superconducting-with-IQM-Quantum-Processors.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]] [[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Toward-Quantum-Superconducting-with-IQM-Quantum-Processors.pdf#page=19|PDF p.19]]

## 关键证据
Tier T为≲100µs实时、专用timing domain；M为0.1ms–1s共调度/同allocation；L为≳1s跨机构服务，需要identity/accounting/双队列协调。这是作者分类，不是所有qubit的普遍coherence阈值。decoder吞吐至少跟上syndrome rate，且要看worst-case而非均值。[[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Toward-Quantum-Superconducting-with-IQM-Quantum-Processors.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]] [[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Toward-Quantum-Superconducting-with-IQM-Quantum-Processors.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]] [[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Toward-Quantum-Superconducting-with-IQM-Quantum-Processors.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]

## 指标与条件
p.32引用Backline FPGA→coprocessor→FPGA均值CPU2.3µs/GPU4.4µs，NVQLink RoCE均值3.84µs、σ0.035µs、n=1000。配置与软件不同，不是CPU/GPU同任务优劣对照；均值/标准差也不是最坏延迟保证。[[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Toward-Quantum-Superconducting-with-IQM-Quantum-Processors.pdf#page=32|PDF p.32]]
p.21的248×是QC-AFQMC算法+实现使**projected**100orbital runtime从约500年降到约1.8年、假设1000万CPU cores；40qubit演示使用LUMI simulated data。不能称实物QPU快248倍或已有实用化学商业优势。[[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Toward-Quantum-Superconducting-with-IQM-Quantum-Processors.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]]
