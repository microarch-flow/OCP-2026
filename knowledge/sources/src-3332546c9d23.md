# OBMF-ICP状态：统一通道协议与USB/MCTP绑定的成熟度

> 阅读范围：已阅读本文件全部可提取文字（13页）。关键页已视觉核对：9, 10；不代表逐页视觉审核。

## 核心结论

- OBMF-ICP是CPU/device及物理接口无关的boot/config/runtime协议层，针对DC-SCM/HPM接口整合，不是只为Host CPU设计。[[systems-management/OBMF-Specifications-and-Workstream-Status-Update.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[systems-management/OBMF-Specifications-and-Workstream-Status-Update.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- 演讲称base1.0.0与USB1.0已发布，MCTP binding DSP0295仍WIP；不得混写成全部绑定已定稿。[[systems-management/OBMF-Specifications-and-Workstream-Status-Update.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 关键证据

- 1.0包括MMIO/I/O、GPIO/SPI/I2C/I3C及UART/IPMI/Memory Device通道；PWM/TACH/ADC、JTAG、TPM/KVM等在1.1或以后计划。[[systems-management/OBMF-Specifications-and-Workstream-Status-Update.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[systems-management/OBMF-Specifications-and-Workstream-Status-Update.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 同USB不同endpoint可共存OCP Recovery、OBMF-ICP与MCTP；OBMF over MCTP是可选路径。[[systems-management/OBMF-Specifications-and-Workstream-Status-Update.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- 产品/代码清单含AMD参考平台、Arm FVP PoC、ASPEED、Code Construct、Intel FPGA/AMC PoC等不同性质证据，不能一概归为量产部署。[[systems-management/OBMF-Specifications-and-Workstream-Status-Update.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]

7个同场demo为演示数量，未给互通通过矩阵/吞吐。FW efficiency/BW/reuse原则无实测收益值。[[systems-management/OBMF-Specifications-and-Workstream-Status-Update.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]][[systems-management/OBMF-Specifications-and-Workstream-Status-Update.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
