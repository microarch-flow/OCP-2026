# The-Classical-Infrastructure-of-Fault-tolerant-Quantum-Computing

## 核心结论
Dell把量子计算的classical支撑分成application调度、实时QEC、error mitigation与校准。对加速器架构最重要的是不同deadline：ms级hybrid应用不能代替µs级decoder闭环。[[fts-quantum-data-center-design/The-Classical-Infrastructure-of-Fault-tolerant-Quantum-Computing.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]] [[fts-quantum-data-center-design/The-Classical-Infrastructure-of-Fault-tolerant-Quantum-Computing.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[fts-quantum-data-center-design/The-Classical-Infrastructure-of-Fault-tolerant-Quantum-Computing.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 关键证据
QIO在HPC层做资源/运行时预测、circuit cutting/knitting、Slurm job管理与QPU driver；control层负责实时处理，QPU物理层为ns尺度。调度服务不应误放进每轮纠错实时路径。[[fts-quantum-data-center-design/The-Classical-Infrastructure-of-Fault-tolerant-Quantum-Computing.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 指标与条件
p.6厂商HQCC/NVQLink框架称<4µs roundtrip，以RoCEv2连接classical和控制设备；未给本讲样本数、tail/max、payload、decoder工作量或拥塞条件，不能当完整QEC逻辑错误率/实时SLA证明。[[fts-quantum-data-center-design/The-Classical-Infrastructure-of-Fault-tolerant-Quantum-Computing.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 图表与架构
已看p.5–6结构与时间边界。p.8用diffusion models做QEM，testbed为Dell/IQM Garnet；这里没有误差改善数值，QEM结果不等于已完成fault-tolerant QEC。[[fts-quantum-data-center-design/The-Classical-Infrastructure-of-Fault-tolerant-Quantum-Computing.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
