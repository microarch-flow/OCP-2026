# Quantum-Networking-for-Scale-Out-Nu-Quantum-s-Entanglement-Fabric

## 核心结论
Nu Quantum的scale-out是跨QPU建立纠缠，不是普通Ethernet把经典数据送到远端。系统含Qubit-Photon Interface、低损光switch/测量网络与低延迟classical控制，rate和fidelity必须同时达标。[[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Quantum-Networking-for-Scale-Out-Nu-Quantum-s-Entanglement-Fabric.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Quantum-Networking-for-Scale-Out-Nu-Quantum-s-Entanglement-Fabric.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Quantum-Networking-for-Scale-Out-Nu-Quantum-s-Entanglement-Fabric.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 关键证据
p.8在其简化模型中给成功概率P=η²/2，η为interface与router效率乘积；损耗会乘法累积并受平方项影响。误差图则作加法预算，模型不能无条件泛化到所有纠缠协议。[[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Quantum-Networking-for-Scale-Out-Nu-Quantum-s-Entanglement-Fabric.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
QPI随modality不同：trapped-ion/neutral-atom用optical microcavity，superconducting/spin需要microwave-to-optical transduction；不能把一款QNU支持当作所有QPU已经即插即用。[[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Quantum-Networking-for-Scale-Out-Nu-Quantum-s-Entanglement-Fabric.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]] [[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Quantum-Networking-for-Scale-Out-Nu-Quantum-s-Entanglement-Fabric.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 指标与条件
p.14 Gen1列“supports up to99.7% fidelity”、MHz entanglement **attempt** rate、300ns control latency；attempt不是成功纠缠rate，300ns不是远端逻辑门端到端延迟。p.17 testbed另称>97% fidelity、50×rate提升但未给baseline绝对rate，二者边界不能混用。[[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Quantum-Networking-for-Scale-Out-Nu-Quantum-s-Entanglement-Fabric.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]] [[fts-quantum-data-center-design/Quantum-Networking-for-Scale-Out-Nu-Quantum-s-Entanglement-Fabric.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]
