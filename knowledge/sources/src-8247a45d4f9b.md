# Edged可重配电源架构：在800V母线上兼容AC负载

## 核心结论
Fungible在此指基础设施可迟绑定输出类型，而非某一网络厂商。方案将SST、储能、燃气TurboCell与GridBlock power router接入共同DC骨干，再提供AC机械/网络及DC IT负载，避免原生DC设施忽略仍需AC的设备。[[expo-hall-sessions/Fungible Power Architectures for the 800V DC Transition presented by Edged Infrastructure.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Fungible Power Architectures for the 800V DC Transition presented by Edged Infrastructure.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Fungible Power Architectures for the 800V DC Transition presented by Edged Infrastructure.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- p5/6示例为四个bay之一、每bay5MW；旧方案另置500kVA机械变压器/UPS，新方案在800V总线用约500kW scalable GridBlock。kVA与kW不同，且新增router没有消除DC→AC转换，只是合并上游链与备电。[[expo-hall-sessions/Fungible Power Architectures for the 800V DC Transition presented by Edged Infrastructure.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Fungible Power Architectures for the 800V DC Transition presented by Edged Infrastructure.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 图中产品归属：GridTran SST、GridBlock energy router、ThermalWorks冷却、ISOVA储能、TurboCell燃气发电；垂直整合描述不等于已验证单点故障不会跨设备传播。[[expo-hall-sessions/Fungible Power Architectures for the 800V DC Transition presented by Edged Infrastructure.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]]
- 逐图检查p7–11：预制base skid连接发电阵列和SST/chiller/ESS/router，图例有600VAC mechanical、480VAC GPU、400–415VAC network及800VDC。p9仅是底座示意，无隐藏性能结果；图上“800VDC 5-9s”未定义统计/服务边界，不据此认定五个九可用率。[[expo-hall-sessions/Fungible Power Architectures for the 800V DC Transition presented by Edged Infrastructure.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Fungible Power Architectures for the 800V DC Transition presented by Edged Infrastructure.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Fungible Power Architectures for the 800V DC Transition presented by Edged Infrastructure.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Fungible Power Architectures for the 800V DC Transition presented by Edged Infrastructure.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 图表与架构
已看p6–11。软件定义AC/DC电压频率、可选resiliency与可扩展输出是架构能力主张；共同DC bus意味着电池/发电可共享，也需审核母线故障域和控制隔离。[[expo-hall-sessions/Fungible Power Architectures for the 800V DC Transition presented by Edged Infrastructure.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/expo-evidence-to-deployment]]；Vertiv的hybrid/dual-rated busway解决分配路径，GridBlock解决能量转换/路由，不能混为同一种“AC/DC兼容”。

## 不确定性与待验证
未给整链效率、容量分配限制、保护选择性、切换时间、故障演练、成本与量产部署。不能从3D渲染或“第二电力链消失”推出无冗余开销、无停机切换或全设施成本节省。
