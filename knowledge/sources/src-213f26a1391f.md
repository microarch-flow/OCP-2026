# A Practical Framework for SSD Qualification in Single-Phase Immersion Cooling

## 核心结论
SSD专用小型浸没平台用于隔离材料/流体的热化学老化，不是用部署槽直接代替资格试验。控制wetted BOM和污染来源，可提高可归因性；完整整机仍需另做验证。[[cooling-immersion/A-Practical-Infrastructure-Solution-for-Component-and-Device-Level-SSD-Qualification-in-Single-Phase-Immersion-Cooling.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]] [[cooling-immersion/A-Practical-Infrastructure-Solution-for-Component-and-Device-Level-SSD-Qualification-in-Single-Phase-Immersion-Cooling.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 关键证据
流程为BOM/FMEA/功能与流体基线→高温上电idle老化→常温周期读出→性能/热/SI/机械检验→拆解、截面与残留检查，并以结果修订FMEA风险优先数。只看老化后能否开机不足以覆盖这些边界。[[cooling-immersion/A-Practical-Infrastructure-Solution-for-Component-and-Device-Level-SSD-Qualification-in-Single-Phase-Immersion-Cooling.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]]

## 指标与条件
老化示例>60°C、无workload但powered-on idle；读出30–40°C执行功能与性能测试。平台能力目标约80°C，p.11声称各设计要求达到，但没有完整老化时间、流体型号、失效率或寿命加速因子。[[cooling-immersion/A-Practical-Infrastructure-Solution-for-Component-and-Device-Level-SSD-Qualification-in-Single-Phase-Immersion-Cooling.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[cooling-immersion/A-Practical-Infrastructure-Solution-for-Component-and-Device-Level-SSD-Qualification-in-Single-Phase-Immersion-Cooling.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[cooling-immersion/A-Practical-Infrastructure-Solution-for-Component-and-Device-Level-SSD-Qualification-in-Single-Phase-Immersion-Cooling.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
p.7展示12×4 SSD阵列，即48个位置；这是平台布局，不代表已完成48件某型号的长期统计资格试验。[[cooling-immersion/A-Practical-Infrastructure-Solution-for-Component-and-Device-Level-SSD-Qualification-in-Single-Phase-Immersion-Cooling.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 图表与架构
介电回路含槽、过滤、flow-through heater、plate heat exchanger、流量计、sensor manifold；隔离的冷却侧含液气换热器、膨胀罐和泵。p.8–10为同架构逐步展示，不能计作三个独立平台。[[cooling-immersion/A-Practical-Infrastructure-Solution-for-Component-and-Device-Level-SSD-Qualification-in-Single-Phase-Immersion-Cooling.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[cooling-immersion/A-Practical-Infrastructure-Solution-for-Component-and-Device-Level-SSD-Qualification-in-Single-Phase-Immersion-Cooling.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]] [[cooling-immersion/A-Practical-Infrastructure-Solution-for-Component-and-Device-Level-SSD-Qualification-in-Single-Phase-Immersion-Cooling.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/power-cooling-fluid-assurance|流体保证]]：部件隔离试验建立机制，部署系统引入额外材料、污染与控制交互。后者并不能被前者取代。

## 不确定性与待验证
标题原文件称Infrastructure Solution，封面实际称Practical Framework；以封面为卡标题。作者未展示具体SSD合格结果、持续I/O压力结果、BOM/流体替换矩阵或AF模型；不称已证明多年服役寿命。
