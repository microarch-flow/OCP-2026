# Arista/Broadcom Scale-Across：分层深缓冲与 BDP 仿真边界

## 核心结论
作者主张短距常态走 on-chip buffer、长距/拥塞才动用 HBM。有效证据是特定超售与拥塞控制下的缓冲扫描仿真，不是“任何跨园区网络都需固定 1.5×BDP”的定律，更不是推理 p99 实测。[[networking/Deep Buffers- The Essential Foundation for Scalable AI Training and Inference Infrastructure.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[networking/Deep Buffers- The Essential Foundation for Scalable AI Training and Inference Infrastructure.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[networking/Deep Buffers- The Essential Foundation for Scalable AI Training and Inference Infrastructure.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- 800Gb/s、10km RTT=100µs 对应 BDP=10MB；示例固定硬件每端口 200MB、总可用 HBM 最高 8GB。每端口上限不能同时在所有端口任意叠加。[[networking/Deep Buffers- The Essential Foundation for Scalable AI Training and Inference Infrastructure.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- 仿真限定长途 2:1 超售、没有竞争性本地流量、packet spraying、NSCC/RCCC，距离 10/30/100km，buffer 3 至 0.005×BDP；测 FCT 与重传，而非 GPU job 或在线推理尾延迟。[[networking/Deep Buffers- The Essential Foundation for Scalable AI Training and Inference Infrastructure.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- 可见图为 10km、NSCC、50/150/500MB 消息，横轴往右 buffer 更小；0.1×BDP 时 FCT 开销约 31.5/24.9/20.6%，重传约 55.1/31.2/23.4%。1.5×BDP 虽零重传，但 50MB 曲线 FCT 开销约 8%，比正文“few percent”更宽，需保留图文差异。[[networking/Deep Buffers- The Essential Foundation for Scalable AI Training and Inference Infrastructure.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[networking/Deep Buffers- The Essential Foundation for Scalable AI Training and Inference Infrastructure.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- 按 100Tb/s 聚合速率算 1.5×BDP，10/30/100km 约 1.9/5.6/18.8GB；这是容量规划推算，不是本页证明单一 8GB 产品可覆盖所有情况。[[networking/Deep Buffers- The Essential Foundation for Scalable AI Training and Inference Infrastructure.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 图表与架构
已核 p.9 两张图、方向、消息大小和百分数。演讲把层级部署描述为 production today，却未给生产流量与故障数据；1M+ GPU 是架构目标而非已验收规模。[[networking/Deep Buffers- The Essential Foundation for Scalable AI Training and Inference Infrastructure.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[networking/Deep Buffers- The Essential Foundation for Scalable AI Training and Inference Infrastructure.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/network-buffer-workload-placement]]、[[knowledge/comparisons/network-transport-and-buffer-context]]：与 Meta RBB 的浅缓冲+70%利用率预算不构成直接反证，二者流量、速率调节、超售和时延目标不同。

## 不确定性与待验证
需真实 incast 同步度、失败路径重分配、本地竞争流量、HBM 带宽/功耗、调度公平性和 p99；深缓冲能减少丢包，也能延长队列等待。训练 barrier 的“宁排队勿丢包”不应无条件推广到 deadline-sensitive decode。
