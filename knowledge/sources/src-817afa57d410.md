# OmniOn负载区间效率：真实能耗积分优先于峰值百分数

## 核心结论
AI负载波动使每级转换器在不同负载区间运行，应基于实际功率trace计算累计损耗，而非只看50%负载峰值。资料有实测曲线，也有不等边界比较、表头错误与示意NPV，需逐一拆开。[[expo-hall-sessions/Driving Efficiency Through Innovation Across the Operating Range and Power Chain, Aligning Power.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Driving Efficiency Through Innovation Across the Operating Range and Power Chain, Aligning Power.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Driving Efficiency Through Innovation Across the Operating Range and Power Chain, Aligning Power.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- SiC与GaN同277VAC但不同平台：SiC含fan，第三方12kW GaN启用phase shedding且**不含fan**（估33W≈0.27个百分点）。10/50/100%负载分别SiC95.54/97.65/96.38%，GaN95.85/97.60/96.23%；正文“差≤0.3点”与表0.31略不一致，不支持GaN必优或劣。[[expo-hall-sessions/Driving Efficiency Through Innovation Across the Operating Range and Power Chain, Aligning Power.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- p8把230VAC→12V PSU与51V输入→12V DC/DC放一图，后者>98%不能直接说明整个AC→GPU链效率高出图上差值，因为少算前级。15–45%GPU利用率到45–75%转换器负载只是illustrative映射，不是普遍电功率线性关系。[[expo-hall-sessions/Driving Efficiency Through Innovation Across the Operating Range and Power Chain, Aligning Power.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- p9在50–108A区间厂商效率波动0.23个百分点、竞品0.61；这两个是各自曲线**平坦度**而非两产品效率差。0.59点current-weighted收益另用于1000柜×132kW、PUE1.3、电价$0.05/kWh、8760h/年、5年8%贴现的$1.8M NPV示意；方法在speaker notes而当前PDF未给完整推导，不能称客户实际节省。[[expo-hall-sessions/Driving Efficiency Through Innovation Across the Operating Range and Power Chain, Aligning Power.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- Osprey40–60V→稳定12V，2.6kW型号50/100%效率98.5/97.9%，4kW仅3ms峰值；3.5kW型号仍development。p11“Time-weighted Efficiency”列0.83/0.48/0.60/0.30%，显然不能作为约98%器件的绝对效率，应索取究竟是变化幅度还是损耗指标。[[expo-hall-sessions/Driving Efficiency Through Innovation Across the Operating Range and Power Chain, Aligning Power.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Driving Efficiency Through Innovation Across the Operating Range and Power Chain, Aligning Power.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
