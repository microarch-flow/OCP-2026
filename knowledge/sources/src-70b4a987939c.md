# MediaTek：定制硅的效率必须在机柜系统边界兑现

## 核心结论
MediaTek将工作负载、定制计算/内存、互连、封装与机柜协同作为Perf/W和Perf/TCO的实现路径。本文是能力和路线图展示，没有提供同模型、同SLA下的端到端推理基准。[[executive-sessions/56_The Silicon Heartbeat of the AI Economy the Efficiency Paradox in Next.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[executive-sessions/56_The Silicon Heartbeat of the AI Economy the Efficiency Paradox in Next.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]][[executive-sessions/56_The Silicon Heartbeat of the AI Economy the Efficiency Paradox in Next.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]]

## 关键证据
- 内存路线并列标准/定制HBM、LPDDR/GDDR、SRAM、CNM/CIM和3D堆叠，而不是宣称单一介质适合所有训练/推理。[[executive-sessions/56_The Silicon Heartbeat of the AI Economy the Efficiency Paradox in Next.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- 互连跨封装UCIe/Mlink、板上PCIe/uLED、板外Ethernet/NPC/CPC/CPO。[[executive-sessions/56_The Silicon Heartbeat of the AI Economy the Efficiency Paradox in Next.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- 2.5D与3.5D图分别标注<10,000及10,000–20,000 mm² **Si**，不可误作单颗计算die面积或封装外形面积。[[executive-sessions/56_The Silicon Heartbeat of the AI Economy the Efficiency Paradox in Next.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
- 两条机柜路径：带NVLink C2C chiplet的客户IP ASIC与NVSwitch；或PCIe/Ethernet ASIC、OCP计算托盘与Ethernet伙伴。[[executive-sessions/56_The Silicon Heartbeat of the AI Economy the Efficiency Paradox in Next.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]

## 指标与条件
p.5将MCP/1×/2×归量产，3×+/5×+归设计，9×+归架构阶段；p.8“10×+reticle”是投资方向，不能称其已经量产。[[executive-sessions/56_The Silicon Heartbeat of the AI Economy the Efficiency Paradox in Next.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[executive-sessions/56_The Silicon Heartbeat of the AI Economy the Efficiency Paradox in Next.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 图表与架构
p.3能耗曲线把AI需求按每年翻倍外推并与全球能源线相交，是约束示意，不是“2030前一定耗尽全球能源”的已验证预测。p.9低电压/DTCO曲线无完整数值坐标，不可从图反推具体PPA增益。[[executive-sessions/56_The Silicon Heartbeat of the AI Economy the Efficiency Paradox in Next.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[executive-sessions/56_The Silicon Heartbeat of the AI Economy the Efficiency Paradox in Next.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 关联与比较
与OpenAI推理模式拆分、d-Matrix存算近置及800 V/热设计联读：更大异构封装只有在良率、热流密度、电源完整性与软件利用率可控时，才可能降低服务成本。

## 不确定性与待验证
需要真实SKU、精度、算子覆盖、HBM/互连利用率、封装良率、寿命及整柜功耗；合作/标准支持名单不等于客户平台已完成部署验证。
