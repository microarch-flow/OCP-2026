# 门式换热器合编：铝替代、Mega AALC 与规范的成熟度

> 原始题名：04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 36 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- 36页合编：门式HX项目p.1–14；OVHcloud/Valeo铝HX p.15–23；AMD/Meta Mega AALC p.24–30；Door HX规范p.31–36。总体更新与后续专讲存在不同对象和版本语境，不能把数字混成一套规格。 [[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=1|PDF p.1]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=24|PDF p.24]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=31|PDF p.31]]
- 铝HX展示比铜更低湿重和更高换热量，但空气压降并非更低；耐腐蚀与生命周期评估仍在进行。短期性能和4个月流体数据不证明全寿命等价。 [[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=19|PDF p.19]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]]
- Mega AALC面向保留机房空气冷却的高密度AI部署，需付出大量侧车空间并共同优化空气/水侧阻抗、冗余、噪声、物流和维修。 [[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=25|PDF p.25]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=26|PDF p.26]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=27|PDF p.27]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=28|PDF p.28]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=29|PDF p.29]]
- Door HX规范旨在共同比较与机械/维护基线，但ORW后门、全性能曲线公开、极高密度结构验证、并排气流和Redfish约束仍留缺口。 [[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=32|PDF p.32]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=33|PDF p.33]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=34|PDF p.34]]、[[cooling-environments/04_Door Heat Exchanger Sub-Project Updates and Lightning Talks.pdf#page=35|PDF p.35]]

## 不确定性与待验证

- 铝回路需确认完整接液材料、配方、温度循环、加速因子与剖检后寿命评估。
- Mega AALC没有提供统一场址占地成本/原设施改造成本及完整美元TCO。
- 标准中的门深、通道宽和重量等不是现场安装授权；采购与部署需核对正式版本和场址工程条件。
