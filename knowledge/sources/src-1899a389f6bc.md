# From Dirt to Tokens：将 AI 基础设施作为集成系统

## 核心结论
Axiom提出把电力、计算、冷却集成并预制成可复制产品，以减少一次性现场工程。重点是电力转换链和冷却联合优化；其效率/节约数据明确来自厂商分析，部分为illustrative。[[executive-sessions/26_From Dirt to Tokens Building AI Infrastructure as an Integrated System.pdf#page=1|PDF p.1]][[executive-sessions/26_From Dirt to Tokens Building AI Infrastructure as an Integrated System.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[executive-sessions/26_From Dirt to Tokens Building AI Infrastructure as an Integrated System.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[executive-sessions/26_From Dirt to Tokens Building AI Infrastructure as an Integrated System.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]
## 关键证据
路径从传统UPS/PDU+power rack，到3.5MW rectifier，再到带储能的grid-forming SST；“today/tomorrow/future”区分当前、下一步与目标。[[executive-sessions/26_From Dirt to Tokens Building AI Infrastructure as an Integrated System.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[executive-sessions/26_From Dirt to Tokens Building AI Infrastructure as an Integrated System.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
## 指标与条件
- 约92%→97%→98%+是**to the rack**的架构分析，不能写成到芯片端到端效率，也不能与单PSU峰值效率直接比较。[[executive-sessions/26_From Dirt to Tokens Building AI Infrastructure as an Integrated System.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
- 北弗吉尼亚40MW示例对比standard/Axiom cold plates，称最高47%冷却节约、400M升/年水、$2.5M/年；为示意估算，未给电价、水价、天气、全年负载和基准冷板参数。[[executive-sessions/26_From Dirt to Tokens Building AI Infrastructure as an Integrated System.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- “最高50%更快部署”缺项目起止点和比较基线，不是已验收进度数据。[[executive-sessions/26_From Dirt to Tokens Building AI Infrastructure as an Integrated System.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]][[executive-sessions/26_From Dirt to Tokens Building AI Infrastructure as an Integrated System.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]
