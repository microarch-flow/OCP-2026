# Beyond Copper: Optical Opportunities for AI Scale-up Fabrics

## 核心结论
Dell把“铜墙”定义为系统拓扑约束而非单一介质优劣：短距铜仍合理，跨机架需要在光学能耗、布线、带宽密度、可维修性和供应链之间联合选择。CPO不是唯一终态，XPO和Open CPX尝试保留光学解耦。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Beyond-Copper-Optical-Opportunities-for-AI-Scale-up-Fabrics.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Beyond-Copper-Optical-Opportunities-for-AI-Scale-up-Fabrics.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Beyond-Copper-Optical-Opportunities-for-AI-Scale-up-Fabrics.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Beyond-Copper-Optical-Opportunities-for-AI-Scale-up-Fabrics.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 关键证据
- 演讲称200 Gb/s/lane被动铜典型仅数米，400 Gb/s/lane及以上预期<1 m；这些是概括性reach判断，不是本演讲提供的channel sweep。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Beyond-Copper-Optical-Opportunities-for-AI-Scale-up-Fabrics.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]
- CPO短电通道可减少I/O/retimer功耗，但引入package/thermal/board协同、制造测试、不可单独维修、更大blast radius与vendor lock-in风险。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Beyond-Copper-Optical-Opportunities-for-AI-Scale-up-Fabrics.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- XPO用fly-over cable将光模块与ASIC/XPU分离；Open CPX用标准socket/电连接器连接可插拔光引擎，两者解耦边界不同。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Beyond-Copper-Optical-Opportunities-for-AI-Scale-up-Fabrics.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Beyond-Copper-Optical-Opportunities-for-AI-Scale-up-Fabrics.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 指标与条件
XPO示例64×200G=12.8T/module，对照OSFP 8×200G=1.6T/module，8倍容量、声称4倍前面板密度；图源Arista，是产品架构比较，不是吞吐或端到端功耗测量。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Beyond-Copper-Optical-Opportunities-for-AI-Scale-up-Fabrics.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 图表与架构
已核对p.7 XPO/OSFP图与lane数。XPO可支持DLC及LPO/高功耗coherent DWDM引擎，因此form factor不决定具体协议、功耗或reach。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Beyond-Copper-Optical-Opportunities-for-AI-Scale-up-Fabrics.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 不确定性与待验证
无统一成本、功耗、延迟或维修实验。光学生态开放是目标，不代表接口已跨厂大规模验证。架构应按真实rack距离/connector/thermal条件评估，不把“400G铜<1m”当所有实现的硬物理定律。
