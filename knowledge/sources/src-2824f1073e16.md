# 大型 AI 负荷并网：模型交付、穿越能力与控制权限

> 原始题名：Grid-Interconnection-Requirements-for-Large-Loads-and-Path-to-Next-Generation-Data-Center-Power-Architectures。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 18 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- 并网问题不只是平均 MW：大量电力电子负载可能在同一电压扰动下同步减载/脱网，进而改变电网频率和电压。演讲中的事件为作者案例叙述，本分析未独立核查新闻或事故报告。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Grid-Interconnection-Requirements-for-Large-Loads-and-Path-to-Next-Generation-Data-Center-Power-Architectures.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Grid-Interconnection-Requirements-for-Large-Loads-and-Path-to-Next-Generation-Data-Center-Power-Architectures.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Grid-Interconnection-Requirements-for-Large-Loads-and-Path-to-Next-Generation-Data-Center-Power-Architectures.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- 电网模型应包括 PSU/UPS 控制、备用电池、切换/旁路、冷却电机与 VFD，以及保护和恢复逻辑；只用静态负载或理想穿越模型会隐藏真实风险。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Grid-Interconnection-Requirements-for-Large-Loads-and-Path-to-Next-Generation-Data-Center-Power-Architectures.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Grid-Interconnection-Requirements-for-Large-Loads-and-Path-to-Next-Generation-Data-Center-Power-Architectures.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Grid-Interconnection-Requirements-for-Large-Loads-and-Path-to-Next-Generation-Data-Center-Power-Architectures.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
- 正序相量模型与 EMT 模型回答不同问题；EMT 的平均化转换器可降低成本，但不能同时声称保留全部开关谐波和器件级故障细节。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Grid-Interconnection-Requirements-for-Large-Loads-and-Path-to-Next-Generation-Data-Center-Power-Architectures.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- GPU/机架功率管理向上配合站点 EMS 和电网接口；站点 EMS、微网和保护仍保留并网点控制权，本地快速响应与上层协调不能互相代替。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Grid-Interconnection-Requirements-for-Large-Loads-and-Path-to-Next-Generation-Data-Center-Power-Architectures.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Grid-Interconnection-Requirements-for-Large-Loads-and-Path-to-Next-Generation-Data-Center-Power-Architectures.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Grid-Interconnection-Requirements-for-Large-Loads-and-Path-to-Next-Generation-Data-Center-Power-Architectures.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]
