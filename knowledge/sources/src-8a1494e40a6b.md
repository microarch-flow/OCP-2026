# Scaling AI Inference with Arm AGI CPU, Rebellions RebelCard, and OCP ORv3 Rack Design

## 核心结论
Arm/Rebellions将agent CPU步骤与Rebel100 prefill/decode配对，但性能论证混合测量、估算与未来rack方案；约2×tokens/W不能当作已可复现的同配置实测比较。[[ai-clusters/Scaling AI Inference with Arm AGI CPU, Rebellions RebelCard, and OCP ORv3 Rack Design.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[ai-clusters/Scaling AI Inference with Arm AGI CPU, Rebellions RebelCard, and OCP ORv3 Rack Design.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[ai-clusters/Scaling AI Inference with Arm AGI CPU, Rebellions RebelCard, and OCP ORv3 Rack Design.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 关键证据
CPU负责orchestration与工具/agent逻辑，加速器承担模型执行；CPU瓶颈可能在高并发下限制整体TPS，然而“60%步骤”不等于60%运行时间或固定Amdahl比例。[[ai-clusters/Scaling AI Inference with Arm AGI CPU, Rebellions RebelCard, and OCP ORv3 Rack Design.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[ai-clusters/Scaling AI Inference with Arm AGI CPU, Rebellions RebelCard, and OCP ORv3 Rack Design.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 指标与条件
p.8 customer-support PoC以36kW机架预算比较30×1U/8160cores与17×2U/4352cores，并注明estimates及measurements+projections；数量/密度不同，不是同样服务器数的CPU性能对照。p.9联合5U服务器约2×TPS/W，未给模型、量化、context、卡数、并发、SLO/p99或功耗边界，不能推导cost/token。[[ai-clusters/Scaling AI Inference with Arm AGI CPU, Rebellions RebelCard, and OCP ORv3 Rack Design.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[ai-clusters/Scaling AI Inference with Arm AGI CPU, Rebellions RebelCard, and OCP ORv3 Rack Design.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 图表与架构
已看p.8–9脚注。p.10的4台Rebel Server/32卡每rack、1–7racks与8+racks pod是扩展方案，未附实际运行的多rack结果。[[ai-clusters/Scaling AI Inference with Arm AGI CPU, Rebellions RebelCard, and OCP ORv3 Rack Design.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

p.11列torch-rbln、vllm-rbln、Kubernetes NPU operator及OS支持方向，清单不证明所有组合生产ready。需同模型同SLA的墙上功耗与p99对照、实际软件版本及error recovery证据。[[ai-clusters/Scaling AI Inference with Arm AGI CPU, Rebellions RebelCard, and OCP ORv3 Rack Design.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
