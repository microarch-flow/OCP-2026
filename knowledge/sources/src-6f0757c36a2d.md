# 800V直流安全：故障限流、反向阻断与保护时间必须联合建模

> 原始题名：Rethinking-Power-Safety-The-Inherent-Benefits-of-800V-Low-Voltage-DC-Architectures。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 12 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- Google报告的关键不是“800V天然安全”，而是故障限流型转换器、分布式源、机架反向阻断和快速保护共同改变故障电流/能量。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Rethinking-Power-Safety-The-Inherent-Benefits-of-800V-Low-Voltage-DC-Architectures.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Rethinking-Power-Safety-The-Inherent-Benefits-of-800V-Low-Voltage-DC-Architectures.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Rethinking-Power-Safety-The-Inherent-Benefits-of-800V-Low-Voltage-DC-Architectures.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Rethinking-Power-Safety-The-Inherent-Benefits-of-800V-Low-Voltage-DC-Architectures.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 反向阻断避免健康机架电容向故障点回灌；演讲仿真显示显著降低峰值及incident energy，但结果依赖电容、拓扑、清除时间和位置。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Rethinking-Power-Safety-The-Inherent-Benefits-of-800V-Low-Voltage-DC-Architectures.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Rethinking-Power-Safety-The-Inherent-Benefits-of-800V-Low-Voltage-DC-Architectures.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Rethinking-Power-Safety-The-Inherent-Benefits-of-800V-Low-Voltage-DC-Architectures.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- 讲者明确要求扩展converter-fed DC的弧闪模型并开展多保护器件实验；不能由单个仿真表推导现场PPE或免带电风险评估。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Rethinking-Power-Safety-The-Inherent-Benefits-of-800V-Low-Voltage-DC-Architectures.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Rethinking-Power-Safety-The-Inherent-Benefits-of-800V-Low-Voltage-DC-Architectures.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
