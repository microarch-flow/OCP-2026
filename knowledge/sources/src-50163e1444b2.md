# Open CPX Optical Engine with Integrated Laser

## 核心结论
TeraHop Diablo-1把6.4T Open CPX引擎、内部laser和独立冷板组合，以减少ELSFP/PM fiber/connector复杂度；其优势是一个具体实现主张，不可推导所有外置laser更贵更不可靠。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Open-CPX-Optical-Engine-With-Integrated-Laser.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Open-CPX-Optical-Engine-With-Integrated-Laser.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Open-CPX-Optical-Engine-With-Integrated-Laser.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]

## 关键证据
Open CPX支持host PCB NPO、ASIC substrate CPO、HDI+elastomer IOA等attach方式；同一接口家族不意味着相同repair/thermal边界。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Open-CPX-Optical-Engine-With-Integrated-Laser.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] p.4/5对release1.0日期分别写9/16与9/17，微小来源内部不一致保留；2027 deployment仍预期。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Open-CPX-Optical-Engine-With-Integrated-Laser.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Open-CPX-Optical-Engine-With-Integrated-Laser.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 指标与条件
- 6.4T引擎约5.5pJ/bit含内部laser；ELS>3dB额外loss→>2倍光功率/约4倍laser dissipation为厂商架构估算，无统一实测baseline。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Open-CPX-Optical-Engine-With-Integrated-Laser.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- p.9 preliminary32channel BER曲线、ASIC→NPO插损约12dB；横轴无清晰物理量/单位，不从图估接收sensitivity。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Open-CPX-Optical-Engine-With-Integrated-Laser.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- 1km demo截图4channel BER约6.2×10^-10至3.45×10^-9，样本bit count约6.8–6.9×10^12；不是所有32lane全温长时结果。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Open-CPX-Optical-Engine-With-Integrated-Laser.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 图表与架构
已核p.14 FIT versus cumulative device-hours；PIC、CW LD、OSFP与NPO多种对象混合，不能将PIC FIT<0.01直接标成整个6.4T engine FIT<0.01。>100B PIC device-hours仅支撑该元件历史，NPO独有connector/thermal/assembly需新增验证。[[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Open-CPX-Optical-Engine-With-Integrated-Laser.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]][[fti-workshop-short-reach-optical-interconnects/Open-CPX-Optical-Engine-With-Integrated-Laser.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]
