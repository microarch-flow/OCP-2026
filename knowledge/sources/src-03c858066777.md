# 04_Breaking the Data Center Walls Unifying AI Fabrics for Agentic AI at t

## 核心结论
Celestica提出以locator/identity分离及统一控制面协调跨域AI fabrics。可取之处是把endpoint identity、位置、tenant instance和管理职责拆开；但这是一份架构提案，不是WAN/RAN达到本地load/store延迟的证明。[[ai-computing-continuum/04_Breaking the Data Center Walls Unifying AI Fabrics for Agentic AI at t.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[ai-computing-continuum/04_Breaking the Data Center Walls Unifying AI Fabrics for Agentic AI at t.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[ai-computing-continuum/04_Breaking the Data Center Walls Unifying AI Fabrics for Agentic AI at t.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]

## 关键证据
LISP示例映射non-IP AccID/IP/MAC EID到RLOC，通过register/subscribe/publish更新；IIDs承载逻辑分区，跨域gateway仍通过WAN/RAN互连。逻辑统一不能消除物理距离。[[ai-computing-continuum/04_Breaking the Data Center Walls Unifying AI Fabrics for Agentic AI at t.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]] [[ai-computing-continuum/04_Breaking the Data Center Walls Unifying AI Fabrics for Agentic AI at t.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]] [[ai-computing-continuum/04_Breaking the Data Center Walls Unifying AI Fabrics for Agentic AI at t.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 指标与条件
p.4并列400–800Gbps/port、<1–100µs并以约250ns为target，且写even over WAN/RAN；p.11/14宣称sub-µs convergence。没有距离、故障检测、消息传播、mapping lookup、硬件或统计结果，不能当端到端保证。[[ai-computing-continuum/04_Breaking the Data Center Walls Unifying AI Fabrics for Agentic AI at t.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[ai-computing-continuum/04_Breaking the Data Center Walls Unifying AI Fabrics for Agentic AI at t.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]] [[ai-computing-continuum/04_Breaking the Data Center Walls Unifying AI Fabrics for Agentic AI at t.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]

## 图表与架构
已看p.4要求表和p.12/14协议比较。SRv6、BGP-EVPN、LISP的优缺点是作者定性判断；控制面协议选择不自动决定数据面带宽、租户隔离正确性或故障恢复下界。

## 不确定性与待验证
persistent KV、agent-state journaling、INC、统一编排缺一致性/失败语义和实测；<3pJ/b、100×带宽、picosecond timing为研究方向。需故障检测→通知→路由安装→应用恢复的时序证据。[[ai-computing-continuum/04_Breaking the Data Center Walls Unifying AI Fabrics for Agentic AI at t.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[ai-computing-continuum/04_Breaking the Data Center Walls Unifying AI Fabrics for Agentic AI at t.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]] [[ai-computing-continuum/04_Breaking the Data Center Walls Unifying AI Fabrics for Agentic AI at t.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]
