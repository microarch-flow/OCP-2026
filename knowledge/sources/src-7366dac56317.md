# Innovation on Diagnosis and Maintenance of AI Datacenters

## 核心结论
AiVRES用200+GPU系统案例说明：通过PI/SI/功能/热压力测试，不代表能覆盖真实应用与CPU调度交互；“BMC无异常”也不排除PCIe事务超时。[[executive-sessions/31_Innovation on Diagnosis and Maintenance of AI Datacenters presented by.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[executive-sessions/31_Innovation on Diagnosis and Maintenance of AI Datacenters presented by.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[executive-sessions/31_Innovation on Diagnosis and Maintenance of AI Datacenters presented by.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
## 关键证据
复现需客户特定软件+CPU压力；GPU请求CPU响应过慢触发PCIe Completion Timeout，而root port与endpoint超时可见性/可配置性不同。[[executive-sessions/31_Innovation on Diagnosis and Maintenance of AI Datacenters presented by.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[executive-sessions/31_Innovation on Diagnosis and Maintenance of AI Datacenters presented by.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[executive-sessions/31_Innovation on Diagnosis and Maintenance of AI Datacenters presented by.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
## 指标与条件
200+是部署系统量，不是故障样本量。文件未给超时阈值、复现频率、修复后失效率或预测模型准确度；“通过100%实验室验证”指其测试集合，不等于可靠性100%。[[executive-sessions/31_Innovation on Diagnosis and Maintenance of AI Datacenters presented by.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[executive-sessions/31_Innovation on Diagnosis and Maintenance of AI Datacenters presented by.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[executive-sessions/31_Innovation on Diagnosis and Maintenance of AI Datacenters presented by.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]]

已核对p.6、7、19、26；未来PCIe设备图提出in-band/OOB双路径与standby-powered寄存器域，保留状态/变化历史，属于规范提案而非既有通用能力。[[executive-sessions/31_Innovation on Diagnosis and Maintenance of AI Datacenters presented by.pdf#page=24|PDF p.24]][[executive-sessions/31_Innovation on Diagnosis and Maintenance of AI Datacenters presented by.pdf#page=25|PDF p.25]][[executive-sessions/31_Innovation on Diagnosis and Maintenance of AI Datacenters presented by.pdf#page=26|PDF p.26]]

## 不确定性与待验证
不能把增加timeout当作消除根因；要验证死锁、队列拥塞、CPU优先级及故障后的恢复行为。预测维护部分缺训练集、误报/漏报和独立测试。
