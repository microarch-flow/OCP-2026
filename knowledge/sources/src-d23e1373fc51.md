# Beyond Power Density：构建安全的 800V DC 生态

## 核心结论
- Eaton 指出 50V 与 800V 不只是电流和密度的区别：转换位置改变，同时移动了故障保护、隔离、人员接触与恢复边界。[[executive-sessions/10_Beyond Power Density Building a Safe 800V DC Ecosystem presented by Ea.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[executive-sessions/10_Beyond Power Density Building a Safe 800V DC Ecosystem presented by Ea.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- 系统需要检测、快速分断、物理隔离、后备保护四层；关掉转换器不等于电池/电容/回馈能量已隔离。[[executive-sessions/10_Beyond Power Density Building a Safe 800V DC Ecosystem presented by Ea.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[executive-sessions/10_Beyond Power Density Building a Safe 800V DC Ecosystem presented by Ea.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
## 关键证据
- side power rack 使用短点对点受保护路径；power center 使用共用 trunk 和分支，必须做选择性故障清除，防止单支路故障扩大到整 row。[[executive-sessions/10_Beyond Power Density Building a Safe 800V DC Ecosystem presented by Ea.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[executive-sessions/10_Beyond Power Density Building a Safe 800V DC Ecosystem presented by Ea.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[executive-sessions/10_Beyond Power Density Building a Safe 800V DC Ecosystem presented by Ea.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- 接地方式改变首故障电流与检测机制；直接接地、高阻接地、浮地没有脱离系统条件的统一优胜者。[[executive-sessions/10_Beyond Power Density Building a Safe 800V DC Ecosystem presented by Ea.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]
## 指标与条件
- 同功率下 800V 的电流约为 50V 的 1/16；125A×800V 约 100 kW/支路，是理想额定功率关系，不包括损耗和降额。[[executive-sessions/10_Beyond Power Density Building a Safe 800V DC Ecosystem presented by Ea.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[executive-sessions/10_Beyond Power Density Building a Safe 800V DC Ecosystem presented by Ea.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 讲者以 IEC 语境说明 800V 仍在广义低压 DC 域，但明确“低压”不等于低危险；本笔记转述来源，不提供法规合规认定。[[executive-sessions/10_Beyond Power Density Building a Safe 800V DC Ecosystem presented by Ea.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]

## 关联与比较
800V 降低铜/电流压力，同时增加运维隔离和认证复杂度。采购需把单支路隔离范围、带电更换状态、储能放电验证纳入 SLA 与服务成本。
## 不确定性与待验证
材料不是可直接执行的安装规范。应由合格电气工程师依据具体设备、当地法规和认证测试确定保护配合及安全作业流程；未给具体器件故障试验或项目证书。
