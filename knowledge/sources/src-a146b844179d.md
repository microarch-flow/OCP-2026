# 余热生态：先通过可部署性门槛，再优化本地价值

> 原始题名：01_Building a Scalable Waste Heat Ecosystem Datacenter Waste Heat, Offtak。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 19 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- 热量存在不等于可回收价值存在：先检查温度/流量/占空比、隔离与故障安全、真实业主和市场、当地许可，全部通过后才进行经济/碳/水/社区价值评分。 [[cooling-environments/01_Building a Scalable Waste Heat Ecosystem Datacenter Waste Heat, Offtak.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]、[[cooling-environments/01_Building a Scalable Waste Heat Ecosystem Datacenter Waste Heat, Offtak.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]、[[cooling-environments/01_Building a Scalable Waste Heat Ecosystem Datacenter Waste Heat, Offtak.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]、[[cooling-environments/01_Building a Scalable Waste Heat Ecosystem Datacenter Waste Heat, Offtak.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]
- 区域供热不是唯一去向，工业、水处理/制水、温室/水产与直接空气捕集都被列为候选；匹配结果高度依赖附近需求、土地、基础设施和运营方。 [[cooling-environments/01_Building a Scalable Waste Heat Ecosystem Datacenter Waste Heat, Offtak.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]、[[cooling-environments/01_Building a Scalable Waste Heat Ecosystem Datacenter Waste Heat, Offtak.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]、[[cooling-environments/01_Building a Scalable Waste Heat Ecosystem Datacenter Waste Heat, Offtak.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]、[[cooling-environments/01_Building a Scalable Waste Heat Ecosystem Datacenter Waste Heat, Offtak.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
- 规模化更需要共同热源/需求描述、责任分界、合同和证据治理，而非单独提高热回收设备效率。 [[cooling-environments/01_Building a Scalable Waste Heat Ecosystem Datacenter Waste Heat, Offtak.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]、[[cooling-environments/01_Building a Scalable Waste Heat Ecosystem Datacenter Waste Heat, Offtak.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]、[[cooling-environments/01_Building a Scalable Waste Heat Ecosystem Datacenter Waste Heat, Offtak.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]

## 不确定性与待验证

- 需要本地逐时供需曲线、热泵电耗、管路损失、投资/维护、热价、利用率、合同期限和替代热源。
