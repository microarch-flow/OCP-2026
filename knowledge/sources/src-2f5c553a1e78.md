# Memory-centric AI Rack System（MARS）与 HBF 路线

## 核心结论
SK hynix MARS把内存/SSD与compute解耦：rack内CXL、rack间RDMA，目标按需共享容量和KV/中间结果，而不是宣称远端内存无延迟替代HBM。[[executive-sessions/33_Introduction to Memory-centric AI Rack Systems (MARS) presented by SK .pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[executive-sessions/33_Introduction to Memory-centric AI Rack Systems (MARS) presented by SK .pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[executive-sessions/33_Introduction to Memory-centric AI Rack Systems (MARS) presented by SK .pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
## 关键证据
软件栈包含allocator、copy API、锁、RPC、KV tiering、图采样；容量池化效益依赖软件数据放置和并发协议，不只插上CXL。客户状态表述为支持customized pilot。[[executive-sessions/33_Introduction to Memory-centric AI Rack Systems (MARS) presented by SK .pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[executive-sessions/33_Introduction to Memory-centric AI Rack Systems (MARS) presented by SK .pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
## 指标与条件
- KV TTFT图：重算平均80.76s/tail478.11s，remote SSD33.56/259.03s，remote CXL DDR15.45/122.90s，remote CXL Hybrid21.92/136.95s；tail分位和模型/上下文未给，不能作为普遍SLA。[[executive-sessions/33_Introduction to Memory-centric AI Rack Systems (MARS) presented by SK .pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- 语音pipeline：RDMA TTFA0.67s、E2E8.08s；pool0.46s、5.73s，约1.46×/1.41×；未给模型/资源等价性。[[executive-sessions/33_Introduction to Memory-centric AI Rack Systems (MARS) presented by SK .pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- HBF示意base288GB对HBF3TB卡、约半HBM带宽/10×容量；p.15另用14×容量示意，口径不统一。规格1.0预计2027.1、cube2028Q1，product TBD。[[executive-sessions/33_Introduction to Memory-centric AI Rack Systems (MARS) presented by SK .pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]][[executive-sessions/33_Introduction to Memory-centric AI Rack Systems (MARS) presented by SK .pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]][[executive-sessions/33_Introduction to Memory-centric AI Rack Systems (MARS) presented by SK .pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]
