# PG25混液研究：当前交付的是测试方法，不是安全混合阈值

> 原始题名：Know-Before-You-Flow-PG25-Mixing-Thresholds-for-DLC。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 20 页）；未做关键图表视觉核对，不将图中空间关系作为已验证事实。

## 核心结论

- Meta/Castrol报告ASHRAE-OCP联合PG25混液研究；文件没有发布可用的“混多少才安全”结果，Phase1结果写为2026年10月下旬，Phase2预计11–12月开始六个月测试。 [[cooling-environments/Know-Before-You-Flow-PG25-Mixing-Thresholds-for-DLC.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]、[[cooling-environments/Know-Before-You-Flow-PG25-Mixing-Thresholds-for-DLC.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]、[[cooling-environments/Know-Before-You-Flow-PG25-Mixing-Thresholds-for-DLC.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]、[[cooling-environments/Know-Before-You-Flow-PG25-Mixing-Thresholds-for-DLC.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- 分阶段从化学/腐蚀/EPDM实验筛查转向带TTV与金属coupon的系统循环；方法包含启动、初始吸附平衡和重新充液，防止初始化学状态污染比较。 [[cooling-environments/Know-Before-You-Flow-PG25-Mixing-Thresholds-for-DLC.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]、[[cooling-environments/Know-Before-You-Flow-PG25-Mixing-Thresholds-for-DLC.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]、[[cooling-environments/Know-Before-You-Flow-PG25-Mixing-Thresholds-for-DLC.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]、[[cooling-environments/Know-Before-You-Flow-PG25-Mixing-Thresholds-for-DLC.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]
- 最大未决问题是实际热通量下层流冷板微通道腐蚀：现有等温coupon与湍流热管方法都不能直接代表冷板，仍依赖拆解。 [[cooling-environments/Know-Before-You-Flow-PG25-Mixing-Thresholds-for-DLC.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]、[[cooling-environments/Know-Before-You-Flow-PG25-Mixing-Thresholds-for-DLC.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]]
