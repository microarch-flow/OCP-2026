# Building the AI Factory：Wiwynn 的规模化基础设施协同设计

## 核心结论
- Wiwynn 将供电路径缩短、垂直供电/冷却融合、光互连放在同一个系统共设计框架；单个组件的效率不能替代封装到机架的热/电/机械闭合。[[executive-sessions/12_Building the AI Factory Overcoming the Infrastructure Challenges of AI.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[executive-sessions/12_Building the AI Factory Overcoming the Infrastructure Challenges of AI.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[executive-sessions/12_Building the AI Factory Overcoming the Infrastructure Challenges of AI.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[executive-sessions/12_Building the AI Factory Overcoming the Infrastructure Challenges of AI.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- CPO 展示定位为 system-ready/rack demo；封面/议程明确关联 2026 OCP，不能把展示的未来平台全部写成已规模量产。[[executive-sessions/12_Building the AI Factory Overcoming the Infrastructure Challenges of AI.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[executive-sessions/12_Building the AI Factory Overcoming the Infrastructure Challenges of AI.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
## 关键证据
- 电源概念对比 HVDC→50V→12V→VPD 与 HVDC→6V→VPD，目的是缩短低压大电流路径；未提供完整效率曲线。[[executive-sessions/12_Building the AI Factory Overcoming the Infrastructure Challenges of AI.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- VPD 板设计列底部 cavity、cut-out、顶部 cavity；双面冷板和 on-chip cooling 缩短热路径，但各路线的维护和封装约束不同。[[executive-sessions/12_Building the AI Factory Overcoming the Infrastructure Challenges of AI.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[executive-sessions/12_Building the AI Factory Overcoming the Infrastructure Challenges of AI.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
## 指标与条件
- CPO rack 概念列 64 个 AI CPO XPU、>800 TB/s 光带宽、100% 液冷；另页给 32 compute trays。10× 更低延迟、5–10× 带宽、3–5× 能效缺基线/测试条件，只保留为厂商概念目标。[[executive-sessions/12_Building the AI Factory Overcoming the Infrastructure Challenges of AI.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[executive-sessions/12_Building the AI Factory Overcoming the Infrastructure Challenges of AI.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- 高功率冷却页出现 6000W+，没有冷却液温度、流量、有效热面积与实测方法，不能据此计算热流密度或匹配具体芯片。[[executive-sessions/12_Building the AI Factory Overcoming the Infrastructure Challenges of AI.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
## 图表与架构
文字提取遗漏 CPO 关键指标与路线图；已对全部 16 页 OCR/视觉核对，区分概念示意、展台展示和性能测试。
## 关联与比较
这是从 accelerator chip 推向 rack 产品时的典型联合边界：电源高度影响冷板空间，冷板影响光学布置，光学改变机架拓扑和维护域。
## 不确定性与待验证
需可工作 demo 的端到端带宽/BER、双面冷却接触热阻、VPD 寿命与整机功耗；展台宣称 working COUPE IC 不证明 >800 TB/s 全机架已投产。[[executive-sessions/12_Building the AI Factory Overcoming the Infrastructure Challenges of AI.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]
