# Frore LiquidJet：低结温收益必须用设施级每任务能耗验证

## 核心结论
作者把GPU→TIM→冷板→冷却液→自由冷却/机械制冷视为整体热路径，并主张降低结温减少泄漏、释放功率预算。但“Tokens/Watt”不是唯一业务指标；没有相同SLO/质量和冷却功率边界，不能推出部署收益。[[expo-hall-sessions/Burning Cash- The Hidden Cost of Ignoring Thermal in Your AI Strategy presented by Frore.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Burning Cash- The Hidden Cost of Ignoring Thermal in Your AI Strategy presented by Frore.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Burning Cash- The Hidden Cost of Ignoring Thermal in Your AI Strategy presented by Frore.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- p4归一化曲线从约61–65°C的100%降至95°C约65%；TjA是整个GPU加权平均die温度，不是最热点或冷却液入口。未给GPU型号、模型、频率策略、功率封顶和数据来源，故只作厂商趋势陈述。[[expo-hall-sessions/Burning Cash- The Hidden Cost of Ignoring Thermal in Your AI Strategy presented by Frore.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- 单独列un-lidded+20%、液态金属TIM+10%、冷却液每降1°C+1%、流量2倍+10%、LiquidJet+35%；入口降温还明确附“只有无需额外功率时”。这些不能简单求和或相乘。[[expo-hall-sessions/Burning Cash- The Hidden Cost of Ignoring Thermal in Your AI Strategy presented by Frore.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Burning Cash- The Hidden Cost of Ignoring Thermal in Your AI Strategy presented by Frore.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Burning Cash- The Hidden Cost of Ignoring Thermal in Your AI Strategy presented by Frore.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Burning Cash- The Hidden Cost of Ignoring Thermal in Your AI Strategy presented by Frore.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Burning Cash- The Hidden Cost of Ignoring Thermal in Your AI Strategy presented by Frore.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
- p14同一基准是skived冷板+PTM7950+带盖封装约93°C。换LiquidJet保持TIM/封装，声称低18°C、+35%；再改液态金属与无盖，低33°C、+43%。这是两种组合相对共同基线，**不是35%再加43%**，也不是43个百分点。[[expo-hall-sessions/Burning Cash- The Hidden Cost of Ignoring Thermal in Your AI Strategy presented by Frore.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]]
- LiquidJet示意使用3D超短流路、多stage及热点集成diamond spreader，声称低16–22°C、+30–35%。制造方式称对铜片应用半导体工艺，缺良率、成本与规模数据。[[expo-hall-sessions/Burning Cash- The Hidden Cost of Ignoring Thermal in Your AI Strategy presented by Frore.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 图表与架构
已核p4/13/14，图支持归一化温度关联而不是独立的设施实测。流量提升还会改变泵功，降低供水温可能增加chiller能耗，不能只优化GPU局部。[[expo-hall-sessions/Burning Cash- The Hidden Cost of Ignoring Thermal in Your AI Strategy presented by Frore.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Burning Cash- The Hidden Cost of Ignoring Thermal in Your AI Strategy presented by Frore.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
