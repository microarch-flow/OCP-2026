# Lumentum 高密度VCSEL：32G PCIe光链路与封装验证

## 核心结论
1060nm VCSEL/PD阵列通过wafer-level fanout/3D堆叠接近CMOS，适合宽而慢的光I/O，并已展示PCIe5概念链路；不能把可工作的PoC、老化器件曲线和未来能效目标混成量产系统规格。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/Slow-and-Wide-12.8T-VCSEL-solutions.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/Slow-and-Wide-12.8T-VCSEL-solutions.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/Slow-and-Wide-12.8T-VCSEL-solutions.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- 1060nm VCSEL HTOL5000h，8mA、115°Coven推Tj181°C，图有阈值电流/光功率/电压漂移；未给样本数、加速模型、失效判据，因此不能从高温时数直接计算部署FIT。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/Slow-and-Wide-12.8T-VCSEL-solutions.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]]
- 1.6T阵列含spare时32/50/64Gb/s分别列54/36/30channel，“FIT<1”为设计目标，不是该页实测系统FIT。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/Slow-and-Wide-12.8T-VCSEL-solutions.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]
- PCIe PoC与Astera/Corning协作：同tray内Scorpio switches、多个32Gb/s NRZ、OM3 MMF、error-free PCIe5；未给观察bits/时长，error-free不意味着BER=0。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/Slow-and-Wide-12.8T-VCSEL-solutions.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
- 约1Tb/s/mm today，约4Tb/s/mm为line-of-sight；optical-engine<2.5pJ/bit为path-to，不能说现有demo已经达到。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/Slow-and-Wide-12.8T-VCSEL-solutions.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 图表与架构
已核高温曲线、FOWLP/3Dstack与demo。p.4包括driver、TIA、CDR/PHY，不能把VCSEL直接调制概括为完全没有接收/时钟恢复电路。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/Slow-and-Wide-12.8T-VCSEL-solutions.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/network-optical-integration]]：与µVCSEL/µLED的宽接口路线比较时，先确认native electrical width、是否需gearbox、每方向吞吐及fiber count。

## 不确定性与待验证
没有端到端延迟、准确reach、系统功率或价格。作者自己要求统一pJ/bit边界；需补复位训练、PCIe replay、lane sparing、温度下BER与可维护连接器测试。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/Slow-and-Wide-12.8T-VCSEL-solutions.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
