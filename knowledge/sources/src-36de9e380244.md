## 核心结论

BOM互换问题不只是名称：同一器件在OEM、integrator和customer之间的hierarchy/attributes发生漂移，人工ECO/PCN、表格与聊天补缝会造成下游等待。提案希望建立可校验格式和registry，保留各自工具，不要求公开价格或完整设计工艺。[[market-impact-adoption/The-Missing-Layer-Why-Compute-Hardware-Needs-a-BOM-Interchange-Standard.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[market-impact-adoption/The-Missing-Layer-Why-Compute-Hardware-Needs-a-BOM-Interchange-Standard.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[market-impact-adoption/The-Missing-Layer-Why-Compute-Hardware-Needs-a-BOM-Interchange-Standard.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]][[market-impact-adoption/The-Missing-Layer-Why-Compute-Hardware-Needs-a-BOM-Interchange-Standard.pdf#page=25|PDF p.25]][[market-impact-adoption/The-Missing-Layer-Why-Compute-Hardware-Needs-a-BOM-Interchange-Standard.pdf#page=34|PDF p.34]][[market-impact-adoption/The-Missing-Layer-Why-Compute-Hardware-Needs-a-BOM-Interchange-Standard.pdf#page=35|PDF p.35]][[market-impact-adoption/The-Missing-Layer-Why-Compute-Hardware-Needs-a-BOM-Interchange-Standard.pdf#page=36|PDF p.36]][[market-impact-adoption/The-Missing-Layer-Why-Compute-Hardware-Needs-a-BOM-Interchange-Standard.pdf#page=37|PDF p.37]]

## 关联与比较

与RuntimeSBOM相辅相成但对象不同：这里是hardware supply-chain BOM，RuntimeSBOM关联运行firmware产物。二者都需要版本、对象身份与证据关联；schema通过无法替代attestation或实物验收。

## 不确定性与待验证

需要解决替代料、effectivity、序列号/as-built/as-maintained关系、跨组织权限及商业敏感信息最小披露。p.32的“malformed不可传输”是理想接口门禁，不能保证valid数据真实或无恶意。[[market-impact-adoption/The-Missing-Layer-Why-Compute-Hardware-Needs-a-BOM-Interchange-Standard.pdf#page=32|PDF p.32]][[market-impact-adoption/The-Missing-Layer-Why-Compute-Hardware-Needs-a-BOM-Interchange-Standard.pdf#page=37|PDF p.37]]
