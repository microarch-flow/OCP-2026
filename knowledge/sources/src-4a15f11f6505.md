# 开放网络部署反馈：支持功能清单不足，必须验证组合与生命周期

> 阅读范围：已阅读本文件全部可提取文字（11页）。关键页已视觉核对：4, 7；不代表逐页视觉审核。

## 核心结论

三个真实部署方向共同揭示：开放hardware+SONiC不意味着任意光模块、QoS、overlay和AAA功能组合都可生产。需要按目标拓扑、feature组合和VM/endpoint生命周期做验证，并明确community与licensed/supported版本差异。[[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 关键证据

- AMD GPU/RoCE backend案例需对齐NIC/switch DSCP、MTU、PFCpriority与qualifiedoptics；普通counter正常但jobcompletion恶化时，需看ECN/PFC并在handover前建立xCCLbaseline。[[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- EVPN/VXLAN基础L2/L3可用不代表DHCPrelay、MC-LAG、QoS acrossVXLAN组合都齐全；MHpeer MAC/neighbor在ageout/relearn/VMmigration时可不同步。[[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- Factoryedge还需多种AAA，案例communitySONiC对ISE动态VLAN/ACL等不足，修复同时涉及NOS和ISE配置。[[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 指标与条件

800G/51.2Tbps是交换平台标称，图为representativetopology；资料没有jobcompletiontime、tail latency、丢包率或故障恢复秒数，不能宣称已量化证明nonblocking性能。[[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]

## 关联与比较

与CTAM、L10–L12测试、OpenBMC运营经验一致：接口存在性测试不能替代组合/异常测试。商业价值在于交付可支持配置和implementationreport，而不只是买到开放交换机。[[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 不确定性与待验证

厂商经验限于其所列hardware/SONiC版本，未提供版本号与完整复现用例，不能泛称CommunitySONiC当前永久缺少这些功能。建议保留configuration/firmware/optic资格记录以复核。[[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[market-impact-adoption/Recent-Open-Networking-Deployment-Feedback-and-Trends.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
