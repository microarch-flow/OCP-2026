# 800 V sidecar：把快速储能、供电转换与故障保护共同设计

> 原始题名：From-Principles-to-Practice-Implementing-OCP-LVDC-Frameworks-with-a-600kW-800VDC-Sidecar-Power-System-Design。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 13 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- Siemens/Skeleton 的方案将隔离 AC/DC、超级电容、冷却和保护移到 sidecar；目的是在保留既有 AC 配电的过渡路径上支持 800 V IT，并非证明上游容量、短路和热条件无需重审。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/From-Principles-to-Practice-Implementing-OCP-LVDC-Frameworks-with-a-600kW-800VDC-Sidecar-Power-System-Design.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/From-Principles-to-Practice-Implementing-OCP-LVDC-Frameworks-with-a-600kW-800VDC-Sidecar-Power-System-Design.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/From-Principles-to-Practice-Implementing-OCP-LVDC-Frameworks-with-a-600kW-800VDC-Sidecar-Power-System-Design.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- 给出了受 GB300 通用功率轮廓启发的实验室负载平滑波形与频率响应，而不是实际 GPU 作业的端到端效率、寿命或可靠性对照。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/From-Principles-to-Practice-Implementing-OCP-LVDC-Frameworks-with-a-600kW-800VDC-Sidecar-Power-System-Design.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/From-Principles-to-Practice-Implementing-OCP-LVDC-Frameworks-with-a-600kW-800VDC-Sidecar-Power-System-Design.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 同一组模块可组成 AC 并联平滑/备电或 DC 分配侧储能方案；连接位置改变保护域、转换损耗和故障供能，不能因模块相同就视为同一系统。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/From-Principles-to-Practice-Implementing-OCP-LVDC-Frameworks-with-a-600kW-800VDC-Sidecar-Power-System-Design.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/From-Principles-to-Practice-Implementing-OCP-LVDC-Frameworks-with-a-600kW-800VDC-Sidecar-Power-System-Design.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
- 标题 600 kW、布局 1.2 MW、CBU 单元与模块额定值处于不同聚合层；采购时需锁定实际 BOM、持续功率、储能量及故障时可用功率。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/From-Principles-to-Practice-Implementing-OCP-LVDC-Frameworks-with-a-600kW-800VDC-Sidecar-Power-System-Design.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/From-Principles-to-Practice-Implementing-OCP-LVDC-Frameworks-with-a-600kW-800VDC-Sidecar-Power-System-Design.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/From-Principles-to-Practice-Implementing-OCP-LVDC-Frameworks-with-a-600kW-800VDC-Sidecar-Power-System-Design.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]
