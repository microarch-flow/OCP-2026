# Operating Linear Pluggable Optics at Hyperscale: From Link Management to Production Reliability

## 核心结论
Meta 的实测并不支持“去掉模块 DSP 必然降低可靠性”：在其 **100G/lane、LPO-LPO、受控校准的特定部署**中，线性光学链路中断率比 retimed 对照低。但这不是所有 LPO、所有 host/光模块配对的保证。[[networking/08_Operating Linear Pluggable Optics at Hyperscale From Link Management t.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[networking/08_Operating Linear Pluggable Optics at Hyperscale From Link Management t.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[networking/08_Operating Linear Pluggable Optics at Hyperscale From Link Management t.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 关键证据
- 生产：DTSW-EDSW 一跳、两端 LPO，4k 模块、4个月、2×400G FR4 以1×800G模式运行、FBOSS。其余网络仍为 retimed；实验室约1k模块、MiniPack3、2×400G loopback、Diag OS。[[networking/08_Operating Linear Pluggable Optics at Hyperscale From Link Management t.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
- 比较必须控制 fleet age、network layer、link mode；模式改变 FEC 共享与故障影响范围。[[networking/08_Operating Linear Pluggable Optics at Hyperscale From Link Management t.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- “failure”是达到数据中心事故门槛且影响服务；“interruption”包括更短暂事件。对大规模训练，后者更接近真实风险，但本文件没有推理 p99/ITL 数据。[[networking/08_Operating Linear Pluggable Optics at Hyperscale From Link Management t.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 指标与条件
- 生产按链路、retimed=1归一化：中断约少2.0倍、服务影响故障约少2.6倍；最后60天中断少8.4倍。没有公开绝对次数、置信区间，不应把8.4倍当固定器件属性。[[networking/08_Operating Linear Pluggable Optics at Hyperscale From Link Management t.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- 实验室2.96M link-hours，0个模块可归因故障；median pre-FEC BER约1×10^-9。固定风速时模块功耗-46%、温度-17.5°C；不是整机/机群功耗下降46%。[[networking/08_Operating Linear Pluggable Optics at Hyperscale From Link Management t.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- 8 lanes、104天光功率稳定性：≤0.25 dB峰峰值、≤0.04 dB/lane drift。[[networking/08_Operating Linear Pluggable Optics at Hyperscale From Link Management t.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- 500 m、2.5 dB插损预算；生产仍用switch+module联合校准，解耦已证明但未用于本批部署；retimed-LPO互通并非此次广泛部署证据。[[networking/08_Operating Linear Pluggable Optics at Hyperscale From Link Management t.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]

## 不确定性与待验证
零事件不等于零风险。供应商分布、故障绝对次数、温度范围和校准成本未完整披露；200G/lane与NPO是后续迁移方向而不是这组统计的同一实验。推理架构选型需补KV迁移下短抖动、重试、p99/ITL及维护工时。
