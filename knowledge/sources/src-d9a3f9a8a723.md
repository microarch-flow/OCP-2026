# AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems: Industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption

## 核心结论
49页是**panel问题清单+多家附带材料**，不是回答逐字稿。统一主题是把multi-vendor可行性评估前移到RTL之前；实质内容包括Arm virtual silicon、Tenstorrent OCA功能模型/参考harness，以及Baya workload-driven fabric生成。必须分清提问、供应商主张、实际模型范围。

## 关键证据
- p.2/3把virtual ecosystem拆为virtual prototyping和AI-native搜索/DSE/ranking/工具harness，底层需要standards/governance/security；AI不替代模型与验证契约。[[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]
- p.5–16是向panel提出的问题，包括FCSA/OCA兼容、模型可移植、security/RoT/firmware signing、reference verification；**不能写成panel已经达成共识的答案**。[[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]]
- Arm CSS预配置CPU/system IP与reference implementation/software，virtual silicon把第三方IO和固件集成前移。9months tape-out/80engineering-years saved是Arm所报项目级价值，没有对照方法或项目样本。[[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=24|PDF p.24]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=26|PDF p.26]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=27|PDF p.27]]
- Tenstorrent OCA页为图像型，已查看实际渲染页：architecture/harness/compliance/chiplets/models组成生态；p.32为v0.8系统架构与AXI-over-UCIe草案。p.33 harness含SEP/SMC、design/test ports、management network和AXI-over-UCIe bridge，beta计划2026-10/Apache2.0。[[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=30|PDF p.30]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=31|PDF p.31]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=32|PDF p.32]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=33|PDF p.33]]
- **OCA p.34是loosely timed functional model，只验证behavioral compliance；timing、PHY、packaging属于各自规范**。bring-your-own model可早期co-sim，但不能据它预测p99、能效或SI margin。p.35 v1.0仍是year-end目标。[[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=34|PDF p.34]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=35|PDF p.35]]
- Baya把requirements→workload analysis→fabric generation→post-silicon tuning串联，以Python/SystemC API、floorplan/DEF、D2D packetizer把物理边界纳入设计。[[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=37|PDF p.37]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=38|PDF p.38]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=40|PDF p.40]]

## 指标与条件
Baya所列320GB/s/UCIe link@2.5GHz、256×1Tb/s ports/chiplet，以及next-generation32+chiplets、16×16mesh、2048-bit links、256TB/s aggregate/chiplet，是方案/规模声明，未提供流量矩阵、面积/功耗、端到端延迟或硅后benchmark。**aggregate internal throughput不等于package external bandwidth**。[[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=39|PDF p.39]] [[Server：Open Chiplet Economy/AI-native Virtual Chiplet Eco-systems with industry best practices to accelerate Chiplet adoption.pdf#page=41|PDF p.41]]
