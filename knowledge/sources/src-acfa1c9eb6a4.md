# 从需求到容量交付：标准化的收益是缩短关键路径，而非消除所有瓶颈

> 阅读范围：已阅读本文件全部可提取文字（10页）。关键页已视觉核对：2, 3, 5, 8；不代表逐页视觉审核。

## 核心结论

材料把AI需求转换为供电、设备产能、供应商资格和施工交付问题。开放spec的商业机制是共享验证、跨项目采购和IT/设施接口复用；电网、现场条件和特定产品资格仍不能自动消失。[[market-impact-adoption/From Compute Demand to Capacity Delivered Unlocking the Data Center Value Chain for the AI Build-Out.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[market-impact-adoption/From Compute Demand to Capacity Delivered Unlocking the Data Center Value Chain for the AI Build-Out.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[market-impact-adoption/From Compute Demand to Capacity Delivered Unlocking the Data Center Value Chain for the AI Build-Out.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]][[market-impact-adoption/From Compute Demand to Capacity Delivered Unlocking the Data Center Value Chain for the AI Build-Out.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[market-impact-adoption/From Compute Demand to Capacity Delivered Unlocking the Data Center Value Chain for the AI Build-Out.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 关键证据

- 高/中/低需求情景依赖GenAI采用、供给和监管约束，2030后按Scurve放缓；这不是无限指数增长。[[market-impact-adoption/From Compute Demand to Capacity Delivered Unlocking the Data Center Value Chain for the AI Build-Out.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]]
- 应对供给侧提出should-cost、端到端integration/logistics/assembly、新供应商按open spec资格、制造debottlenecking四条路径。[[market-impact-adoption/From Compute Demand to Capacity Delivered Unlocking the Data Center Value Chain for the AI Build-Out.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 优先标准化影响交付速度的power/cooling/rack/network/grid接口，而不只是产品外形。[[market-impact-adoption/From Compute Demand to Capacity Delivered Unlocking the Data Center Value Chain for the AI Build-Out.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 指标与条件

全球basecase2035约336GW **IT compute**，conservative254、accelerated472GW；不能混同utility input MW。2025–2030累计CAPEX$7.3T是跨价值链模型，页下注明未纳入operators/endusers资本投资，不能再与其他TAM直接相加。[[market-impact-adoption/From Compute Demand to Capacity Delivered Unlocking the Data Center Value Chain for the AI Build-Out.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[market-impact-adoption/From Compute Demand to Capacity Delivered Unlocking the Data Center Value Chain for the AI Build-Out.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]

US供需模型2030为121 vs106GW、gap15GW；p.4另称~100GW及65%announced online，是不同引述/模型口径，不能强行调成同一预测。[[market-impact-adoption/From Compute Demand to Capacity Delivered Unlocking the Data Center Value Chain for the AI Build-Out.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[market-impact-adoption/From Compute Demand to Capacity Delivered Unlocking the Data Center Value Chain for the AI Build-Out.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
