# Redfish转送SPDM签名测量：远端验证不应只信BMC状态字段

> 阅读范围：已阅读本文件全部可提取文字（13页）。关键页已视觉核对：8；不代表逐页视觉审核。

## 核心结论

- 远端租户/运营方可不完全信任BMC/BIOS；Redfish负责取回设备签名证据，而不是要求信任BMC自报Health/VerificationStatus。[[manageability-workshop/Remote Attestation of Devices with Redfish and SPDM.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[manageability-workshop/Remote Attestation of Devices with Redfish and SPDM.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[manageability-workshop/Remote Attestation of Devices with Redfish and SPDM.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- SPDMGetSignedMeasurements让验证方提供nonce与测量索引，取回包含协商和请求/响应的L2签名transcript，验证新鲜性、证书链与签名。[[manageability-workshop/Remote Attestation of Devices with Redfish and SPDM.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[manageability-workshop/Remote Attestation of Devices with Redfish and SPDM.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[manageability-workshop/Remote Attestation of Devices with Redfish and SPDM.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 关键证据

- 签名覆盖VCA协商、GET_MEASUREMENTS与不含签名字节的MEASUREMENTS；不能只验单个hash字段。[[manageability-workshop/Remote Attestation of Devices with Redfish and SPDM.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- ComponentIntegrity描述Requester/Responder间安全信息，并链接具体目标设备/证书。[[manageability-workshop/Remote Attestation of Devices with Redfish and SPDM.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
- Redfish Tacklebox示例脚本生成随机nonce、解析L2 log、比较nonce、验证证书链和签名。[[manageability-workshop/Remote Attestation of Devices with Redfish and SPDM.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 指标与条件

示例版本/算法/地址与输出为mockup，不是性能测试。无证明服务QPS、全机群扫描时延、nonce存储策略或带宽数字。[[manageability-workshop/Remote Attestation of Devices with Redfish and SPDM.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[manageability-workshop/Remote Attestation of Devices with Redfish and SPDM.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[manageability-workshop/Remote Attestation of Devices with Redfish and SPDM.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]][[manageability-workshop/Remote Attestation of Devices with Redfish and SPDM.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]

## 图表与架构

已视觉核对p.8端到端流程：BMC是Redfish–SPDM中转，设备提供签名transcript；不是BMC重签替代设备签名。

## 关联与比较

与[[knowledge/sources/src-bbddc7859662|hitless证明]]结合：fresh nonce防重放但不能单独解决证明之后状态变化；还需要历史和可接受的更新策略。

## 不确定性与待验证

验签证明真实性/完整性，不自动判断测量是否known-good；还需可信参考manifest、撤销/证书策略与目标设备绑定，且BMC仍可拒绝服务或隐藏设备。
