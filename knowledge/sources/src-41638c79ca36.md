# Murata电源路径：ORv3现货、800V开发与封装近负载供电分开看

## 核心结论
本讲覆盖AC侧柜→400/800V→50V→12/6V→超过1000A的近负载供电，但路线覆盖完整不等于所有转换级均量产。应按料号/电压/热设计逐级确认Released、In Development和Roadmap。[[expo-hall-sessions/Powering Progress- Murata's Role in Advancing OCP Innovation presented by Murata.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Powering Progress- Murata's Role in Advancing OCP Innovation presented by Murata.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- 典型ORv3 shelf为6×3.6kW=21.6kW，50.5/54V；HPR为6×5.5kW=33kW，19/21英寸。铭牌合计不等于N+1可用容量。[[expo-hall-sessions/Powering Progress- Murata's Role in Advancing OCP Innovation presented by Murata.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Powering Progress- Murata's Role in Advancing OCP Innovation presented by Murata.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 路线图明确50V/5.5kW PSU及33kW shelves已发布；54V/5.5kW版本标开发中Q4’26；72kW/12kW PSU及110kW/800V路线为后续规划，不能把33kW的成熟度外推至800V。[[expo-hall-sessions/Powering Progress- Murata's Role in Advancing OCP Innovation presented by Murata.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- 浸没版本为21英寸3OU，54kW ORv3或82.5kW HPR，使用经过改造以兼容介电液的3.6/5.5kW PSU，列50/54.5V可用版本。不是普通风冷PSU直接浸液。[[expo-hall-sessions/Powering Progress- Murata's Role in Advancing OCP Innovation presented by Murata.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- 另一页“Dielectric Cooling in development”属于后续架构，须与上述已列可用浸没产品区分；仍缺兼容液清单/鉴定状态。[[expo-hall-sessions/Powering Progress- Murata's Role in Advancing OCP Innovation presented by Murata.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]

## 图表与架构
已核p3/8/9/10/12/13。p12从lateral供电移到板背面vertical再到封装内iVR，目标缩短电流路径、降ESL/ESR、应对瞬态；p13同时把Si-cap/MLCC、inductor、VRM、热路径及PDN仿真放进封装剖面，iVR仍标R&D。[[expo-hall-sessions/Powering Progress- Murata's Role in Advancing OCP Innovation presented by Murata.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]] [[expo-hall-sessions/Powering Progress- Murata's Role in Advancing OCP Innovation presented by Murata.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/expo-evidence-to-deployment]]：与LITEON/MEGMEET比较时，先统一AC/DC输入、输出电压、备份方式和可用容量。更近负载的调节器可能节省配电损耗，也把热/可维修性移进封装，不能仅用功率密度判断TCO。

## 不确定性与待验证
无逐级效率/负载曲线、瞬态droop、稳定性、故障降额或MW级整柜实测。需完整端到端功率预算与辅助泵/风扇计入方式，确认供电与冷却冗余是否有公共故障点。
