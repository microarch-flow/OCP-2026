## 核心结论
52页panel deck包含Google、Meta、Microsoft、Broadcom多个部分，不能混成一项共同实验。共通约束是距离增加导致光层选择、BDP、拥塞反馈与部署复杂性同时上升；各方建议和成熟度不同。[[ai-clusters/Scale-across-Networking-Panel-discussion-on-routing-and-transport-equipment-implications.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[ai-clusters/Scale-across-Networking-Panel-discussion-on-routing-and-transport-equipment-implications.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]] [[ai-clusters/Scale-across-Networking-Panel-discussion-on-routing-and-transport-equipment-implications.pdf#page=36|PDF p.36]] [[ai-clusters/Scale-across-Networking-Panel-discussion-on-routing-and-transport-equipment-implications.pdf#page=45|PDF p.45]]

## 指标与条件
Broadcom p.46按800GE及约10µs/km RTT假设：5km→5MB BDP、100km→100MB、1000km→1GB。BDP是带宽×RTT，距离到RTT映射含路线假设，不等于可用应用buffer。[[ai-clusters/Scale-across-Networking-Panel-discussion-on-routing-and-transport-equipment-implications.pdf#page=46|PDF p.46]]
p.49–51为仿真：50–500MB消息及NSCC/RCCC，在测试的10/30/100km条件下，buffer threshold约≥1.5BDP接近理想FCT且零retransmission。100T switch按1.5BDP估算10/30/100km约1.9/5.6/18.8GB；不是所有拓扑/到达过程的无丢包定理。[[ai-clusters/Scale-across-Networking-Panel-discussion-on-routing-and-transport-equipment-implications.pdf#page=49|PDF p.49]] [[ai-clusters/Scale-across-Networking-Panel-discussion-on-routing-and-transport-equipment-implications.pdf#page=51|PDF p.51]]
