# LVDC 标准缺口：建立组件—系统—安装的可追溯清单

> 原始题名：Closing-the-Standards-Gap-for-LVDC-Power-Distribution。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 11 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- UL Solutions 将需求对齐、测试能力、产品标识与人员培训并列，说明 800 V 落地不仅是某个元件拿证，还需要系统和现场安装链条。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Closing-the-Standards-Gap-for-LVDC-Power-Distribution.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Closing-the-Standards-Gap-for-LVDC-Power-Distribution.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
- 标准盘点覆盖源、配电、保护、连接器、变换器、线缆、接地和 IT 负载；侧柜、SST、液冷电气组件和新型保护是优先补缺领域。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Closing-the-Standards-Gap-for-LVDC-Power-Distribution.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Closing-the-Standards-Gap-for-LVDC-Power-Distribution.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Closing-the-Standards-Gap-for-LVDC-Power-Distribution.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 表中 OOI、New OOI、CRD、空白映射及问号必须保留；US 与 IEC 的映射不是等效认证声明，也不证明任何产品已通过。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Closing-the-Standards-Gap-for-LVDC-Power-Distribution.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Closing-the-Standards-Gap-for-LVDC-Power-Distribution.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]、[[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Closing-the-Standards-Gap-for-LVDC-Power-Distribution.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- 弧闪、容性故障、安装规范与并网仍列为未解决问题；本卡保存演讲时点的标准索引，不提供现行法规判断。 [[ai-open-dc-power-distribution/Closing-the-Standards-Gap-for-LVDC-Power-Distribution.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
