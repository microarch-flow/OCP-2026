# Open Adapter Card (OAC): Sub-Rackscale Acceleration Lives On

## 核心结论
OAC瞄准仍重要的8卡级系统，扩大卡面积、供电与高速连接并保持protocol-agnostic机械边界；SW/DW/DWO宽度、air/DLC决定可装数量和热边界，不存在一种所有卡都能达到5000W的无条件规格。[[server/Open-Adapter-Card-OAC-Sub-Rackscale-Acceleration-Lives-On.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]] [[server/Open-Adapter-Card-OAC-Sub-Rackscale-Acceleration-Lives-On.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]

## 关键证据
卡示意400×190mm，三width profiles允许19英寸chassis最多16×SW或8×DW/DWO；最多7 high-speed connectors/224 differential pairs，与112 lanes概念必须区分发送/接收pair计数。CID/SCD负责协议/slot兼容，不由外形推断。[[server/Open-Adapter-Card-OAC-Sub-Rackscale-Acceleration-Lives-On.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[server/Open-Adapter-Card-OAC-Sub-Rackscale-Acceleration-Lives-On.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[server/Open-Adapter-Card-OAC-Sub-Rackscale-Acceleration-Lives-On.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 指标与条件
|配置/主张|限定|
|---|---|
|5000W capable|50V下最大5000W，12V下1200W；SW为4000W/960W。供电能力不是芯片TDP或实测散热能力。[[server/Open-Adapter-Card-OAC-Sub-Rackscale-Acceleration-Lives-On.pdf#page=3\|PDF p.3]]|
|最多4.5×usable PCB area|PCIe CEM对DWO的比较，不是任意两卡外接矩形面积比；volume另列3.2× DW对DW。[[server/Open-Adapter-Card-OAC-Sub-Rackscale-Acceleration-Lives-On.pdf#page=3\|PDF p.3]]|
|air-cooled约700W/DW、约1200W/DW Tall|示例8RU平台下的方案目标，不代表5000W可风冷。[[server/Open-Adapter-Card-OAC-Sub-Rackscale-Acceleration-Lives-On.pdf#page=6\|PDF p.6]]|
|112L×200Gbps HSIO|连接能力描述，不是特定fabric有效带宽/延迟基准。[[server/Open-Adapter-Card-OAC-Sub-Rackscale-Acceleration-Lives-On.pdf#page=3\|PDF p.3]]|

## 图表与架构
5RU DLC与8RU air example都复用DC-MHS HPM、DC-SCM、NIC和M-CRPS生态；模块化并未移除baseboard、冷却、供电及管理共同验证。[[server/Open-Adapter-Card-OAC-Sub-Rackscale-Acceleration-Lives-On.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[server/Open-Adapter-Card-OAC-Sub-Rackscale-Acceleration-Lives-On.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/compute-memory-modular-platform-contract|模块化平台契约]] · [[knowledge/concepts/compute-memory-power-path-and-dynamics|供电动态约束]]。比较系统成本应按有效卡数、cooling envelope和fabric拓扑，而不是只比较PCB面积或峰值瓦数。

## 不确定性与待验证
会议为v0.9，缺channel BER、thermal resistance、full-load power、插拔可靠性与跨vendor互换数据。Protocol-agnostic是可承载多类接口，不是不同协议能直接互通。[[server/Open-Adapter-Card-OAC-Sub-Rackscale-Acceleration-Lives-On.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
