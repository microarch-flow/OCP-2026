# 余热利用路径：热品位、净电耗与减碳基线决定结果

> 原始题名：06_Waste Heat Recovery Pathways for AI Data Centers Emerging Opportunitie。阅读范围：完整可提取文字（物理 PDF 共 25 页），关键引用图表另做视觉核对。

## 核心结论

- 本材料是案例/文献综述加简化计算，比较供热、制冷、发电、DAC和空气取水；没有同一场址五条路线的实测对照。 [[cooling-environments/06_Waste Heat Recovery Pathways for AI Data Centers Emerging Opportunitie.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]、[[cooling-environments/06_Waste Heat Recovery Pathways for AI Data Centers Emerging Opportunitie.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]、[[cooling-environments/06_Waste Heat Recovery Pathways for AI Data Centers Emerging Opportunitie.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]、[[cooling-environments/06_Waste Heat Recovery Pathways for AI Data Centers Emerging Opportunitie.pdf#page=23|PDF p.23]]
- 把余热先用电力热泵升温，再发电或驱动制冷，未必有净收益：演讲的指定COP示例反而增加用电；应该比较净输出与被替代方案。 [[cooling-environments/06_Waste Heat Recovery Pathways for AI Data Centers Emerging Opportunitie.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]、[[cooling-environments/06_Waste Heat Recovery Pathways for AI Data Centers Emerging Opportunitie.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]
- 各路径减碳潜力强烈依赖被替代能源、电网碳强度、热泵辅助功、供需匹配和实际利用率。图中DAC上限高不能直接得出任意场址应优先DAC。 [[cooling-environments/06_Waste Heat Recovery Pathways for AI Data Centers Emerging Opportunitie.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]、[[cooling-environments/06_Waste Heat Recovery Pathways for AI Data Centers Emerging Opportunitie.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]、[[cooling-environments/06_Waste Heat Recovery Pathways for AI Data Centers Emerging Opportunitie.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]]、[[cooling-environments/06_Waste Heat Recovery Pathways for AI Data Centers Emerging Opportunitie.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]]、[[cooling-environments/06_Waste Heat Recovery Pathways for AI Data Centers Emerging Opportunitie.pdf#page=23|PDF p.23]]
