# Cu Sintering BGA Interconnect for Mitigating Electromigration in High-Current AI Power Delivery

## 核心结论
高电流 BGA 的可靠性可能先于电源拓扑成为限制。研究以 Cu nanoparticle sintering 替代 SAC305 solder，展示相同加速电流应力下更稳定的导通和微结构；还不是量产大面积 BGA 寿命认证。[[fts-ai-hpc/Copper-Sintering-BGA-Interconnect-for-Mitigating-Electromigration-in-High-Current-AI-Power-Delivery.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[fts-ai-hpc/Copper-Sintering-BGA-Interconnect-for-Mitigating-Electromigration-in-High-Current-AI-Power-Delivery.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[fts-ai-hpc/Copper-Sintering-BGA-Interconnect-for-Mitigating-Electromigration-in-High-Current-AI-Power-Delivery.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 关键证据
daisy-chain top/bottom PCB 结构用于比较两类接点；p.4 包含制作、剪切、阻值分布与电流源/采样电路，p.5 给五条样本曲线。SAC305 阴极裂纹/IMC 生长与开路相符，sintered Cu 900h 后截面变化较小。[[fts-ai-hpc/Copper-Sintering-BGA-Interconnect-for-Mitigating-Electromigration-in-High-Current-AI-Power-Delivery.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[fts-ai-hpc/Copper-Sintering-BGA-Interconnect-for-Mitigating-Electromigration-in-High-Current-AI-Power-Delivery.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[fts-ai-hpc/Copper-Sintering-BGA-Interconnect-for-Mitigating-Electromigration-in-High-Current-AI-Power-Delivery.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[fts-ai-hpc/Copper-Sintering-BGA-Interconnect-for-Mitigating-Electromigration-in-High-Current-AI-Power-Delivery.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[fts-ai-hpc/Copper-Sintering-BGA-Interconnect-for-Mitigating-Electromigration-in-High-Current-AI-Power-Delivery.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 指标与条件
- 测试 current density=50A/mm²，SAC305 约300h 开路，Cu sintering 到900h以上仍保持导通。**900h 是观测截止仍存活，不是测得寿命900h或确定3×寿命。**[[fts-ai-hpc/Copper-Sintering-BGA-Interconnect-for-Mitigating-Electromigration-in-High-Current-AI-Power-Delivery.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]
- p.2 所说未来器件 >2A/mm² 是应用动机，不能与50A/mm²加速应力混为额定工作条件。[[fts-ai-hpc/Copper-Sintering-BGA-Interconnect-for-Mitigating-Electromigration-in-High-Current-AI-Power-Delivery.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]]
- p.8 的原子浓度/电流密度图为模型/场分布式展示，全文缺完整求解设置；不把它自动当独立实测原子迁移计量。[[fts-ai-hpc/Copper-Sintering-BGA-Interconnect-for-Mitigating-Electromigration-in-High-Current-AI-Power-Delivery.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 图表与架构
Cu 接点形状和截面也不同于 solder ball，性能可能同时受材料、接触面积、几何电流拥挤影响。要归因“只换材料”需匹配这些条件。

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/compute-memory-power-path-and-dynamics|供电路径与动态约束]]：垂直供电缩短高电流路径，但接点电迁移、机械应力和散热仍需独立验证。

## 不确定性与待验证
缺完整环境温度/结温、热循环、机械可靠性、统计置信区间、加速寿命模型与量产良率。p.9 明确大面积数百接点是下一步，不应直接宣布数据中心服役寿命已证明。
