# Delta/NVIDIA：800 VDC 供电与冷却方案

## 核心结论
材料把 800V 迁移拆成 rack power rack、cluster power center、data-hall power block，分别面向现有场地升级和新建，而非要求所有站点一步切换原生800V服务器。[[executive-sessions/16_Delta 800 VDC Power and Cooling Solutions for Data Centers presented b.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[executive-sessions/16_Delta 800 VDC Power and Cooling Solutions for Data Centers presented b.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]][[executive-sessions/16_Delta 800 VDC Power and Cooling Solutions for Data Centers presented b.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[executive-sessions/16_Delta 800 VDC Power and Cooling Solutions for Data Centers presented b.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]][[executive-sessions/16_Delta 800 VDC Power and Cooling Solutions for Data Centers presented b.pdf#page=19|PDF p.19]]
## 关键证据
储能既承担故障穿越/备用，也用于高频负载平滑和跨 SST 平均分配；功率密度提升必须与保护、控制和液冷一起交付。[[executive-sessions/16_Delta 800 VDC Power and Cooling Solutions for Data Centers presented b.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]][[executive-sessions/16_Delta 800 VDC Power and Cooling Solutions for Data Centers presented b.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]]
## 指标与条件
- 660kW power rack 示例：415–480VAC输入、8条125A DC输出，可配BBU；这是配置额定，不是实际持续GPU功率。[[executive-sessions/16_Delta 800 VDC Power and Cooling Solutions for Data Centers presented b.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
- 800→54V shelf：90/108kW，峰值效率98.5%；未给完整负载温度曲线。[[executive-sessions/16_Delta 800 VDC Power and Cooling Solutions for Data Centers presented b.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]
- CDU 标称2.5MW@4°C approach、3850L/min@60psi，2+1泵；额定散热需同时保留流量和温差条件。[[executive-sessions/16_Delta 800 VDC Power and Cooling Solutions for Data Centers presented b.pdf#page=26|PDF p.26]]
- readiness 图标 Q3 2026 power rack、Q2 2027 power center、Q1 2028 power block；后两项是路线图。[[executive-sessions/16_Delta 800 VDC Power and Cooling Solutions for Data Centers presented b.pdf#page=23|PDF p.23]]

## 关联与比较
与 [[knowledge/sources/src-d23e1373fc51|800V安全分区]]、[[knowledge/sources/src-8e92642e8e25|微电网]] 连接：迁移位置决定故障隔离和运维授权边界，SST与储能的效率不能简单等同grid-to-chip效率。
## 不确定性与待验证
需要转换链完整效率、动态稳态、保护协调、建筑改造成本和认证结果；标称ready与展台图片不足证明目标客户量产部署。
