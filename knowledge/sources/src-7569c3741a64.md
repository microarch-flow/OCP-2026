# The Interconnect Inflection Point: Evolving Intra- and Multi-Rack Scale-Up AI Networks

## 核心结论
Celestica主张按reach与维护边界分层，而非用同一种介质覆盖所有scale-up/out/across；XPO提供统一机械/热包络承载多种引擎，但每种引擎仍需单独验证。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The-Interconnect-Inflection-Point-Evolving-Intra-and-Multi-Rack-Scale-Up-AI-Networks.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The-Interconnect-Inflection-Point-Evolving-Intra-and-Multi-Rack-Scale-Up-AI-Networks.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The-Interconnect-Inflection-Point-Evolving-Intra-and-Multi-Rack-Scale-Up-AI-Networks.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 关键证据
XPO是前面板密度/液冷方向，Open CPX是socketed optics/copper引擎接口，OCI是slow-wide、DWDM、双向的silicon-centric scale-up方向。三者不是相互替代的同层协议。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The-Interconnect-Inflection-Point-Evolving-Intra-and-Multi-Rack-Scale-Up-AI-Networks.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]

## 指标与条件
- XPO设计宣称12.8T/module、1RU内204.8T密度、每slot支持400W+冷却；不代表实际满配交换机已测得这些端到端指标。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The-Interconnect-Inflection-Point-Evolving-Intra-and-Multi-Rack-Scale-Up-AI-Networks.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- reach示意：224G passive DAC≤1m、ACC 1.2–2m、AEC 2–5m、LPO 2–10m+、LRO/FRO 10–500m、coherent 500m–10km+。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The-Interconnect-Inflection-Point-Evolving-Intra-and-Multi-Rack-Scale-Up-AI-Networks.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- ARC预期2–10m；MicroLED声称sub-pJ/bit，未给lane速率、完整链路能耗边界和测量平台，属于技术方向，不纳入同口径能耗排名。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The-Interconnect-Inflection-Point-Evolving-Intra-and-Multi-Rack-Scale-Up-AI-Networks.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 不确定性与待验证
未给224G/400G各模式完整channel loss、FEC/latency、成本和环境条件。阶段性路线从可维修XPO/socketed NPO试点→多厂qualification→量产，是建议而非已经完成的部署报告。[[ocp-special-focus-photonics/The-Interconnect-Inflection-Point-Evolving-Intra-and-Multi-Rack-Scale-Up-AI-Networks.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
