# Phoenix Contact：工业直流电网经验如何迁移至数据中心

## 核心结论
实际证据来自自有工业工厂的700 V直流电网，而不是已规模化运行的800 V AI数据中心。可迁移的是多能源接入、保护、控制和接口标准化经验；负载瞬态及可用性要求仍需重新验证。[[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]][[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=30|PDF p.30]]

## 关键证据
- 工厂拓扑连接400 VAC与700 VDC，包含700 kW双向AC/DC、110 kWp直流光伏、300 kWh电池、2×150 kW+8×60 kW充电；另有1,500 kWp经逆变器接AC侧的光伏，不能全部算作直流母线直接输入。[[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]]
- 建设挑战依次为短路耐受、连接器电弧、缺少技术规格、缺少DC产品和智能电网管理；解法包括短路测试、ArcZero连接器、VDE SPEC90037及PLCnext控制应用。[[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=18|PDF p.18]][[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=20|PDF p.20]][[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=23|PDF p.23]][[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=25|PDF p.25]]

## 指标与条件
300 kWh是储能量，700 kW是转换功率，不能混作同类容量。p.21展示320 V/20 A的AC/DC连接器断开对照，p.22标称ArcZero800 V/20 A；PDF静态帧并不提供灭弧时间或完整测试波形。[[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]][[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=22|PDF p.22]]

## 图表与架构
p.15是全系统拓扑，p.16提出按电压进行DC网控制；p.30时间轴明确为预计采用阶段，不是数据中心2034年才能/必然采用的事实。[[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=15|PDF p.15]][[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=16|PDF p.16]][[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=30|PDF p.30]]

## 关联与比较
与Eaton800 V保护、Google多时间尺度电网到芯片协同联读：标准电压只解决接口的一部分，选择性切断、储能、接地和控制环才决定故障是否扩散。

## 不确定性与待验证
未给工业项目的全链路效率、故障历史及AI脉冲负载下控制稳定性；700 V/±350 V与800 V/±400 V之间不能仅改标签即互换。[[executive-sessions/49_Scaling Direct Current in Data Centers From Proven Industrial Concepts.pdf#page=31|PDF p.31]]
