# 07_The Autonomous AI-RU Closing the RIC intelligence gap to the radio thr

## 核心结论
UGECO/Bell Canada 的 Au-RU 原型把 ruApp 的快速 wake 决策放进 radio unit，由 RIC/SMO 的慢环设 policy envelope。设计论点是不同时间尺度的控制职责分离，而不是简单增加 RIC 带宽。[[ai-computing-continuum/07_The Autonomous AI-RU Closing the RIC intelligence gap to the radio thr.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[ai-computing-continuum/07_The Autonomous AI-RU Closing the RIC intelligence gap to the radio thr.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 关键证据
RIC E2 为 10ms–1s、A1/O1 >1s；本地 radio 事件要求 µs–ms。原型使用 x86 DU/SMO、Jetson AGX Thor、Lattice FPGA board，NETCONF/YANG M-Plane，GPIO 反映 carrier/stimulus；是回放/实验室系统，不是大规模运营网切换。[[ai-computing-continuum/07_The Autonomous AI-RU Closing the RIC intelligence gap to the radio thr.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[ai-computing-continuum/07_The Autonomous AI-RU Closing the RIC intelligence gap to the radio thr.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[ai-computing-continuum/07_The Autonomous AI-RU Closing the RIC intelligence gap to the radio thr.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 指标与条件
- 明确实验日期 **2026-09-18**；29 cells×8 days KPI 训练，单真实 cell 的 held-out night 在实验室回放。管理路径 SLEEP→pin LOW 约 400ms；本地 wake **decision 56µs**；stimulus seen→carrier HIGH **29ms**，作者明说后者受 host software/Ethernet 支配。不能把 56µs 当 RF 链完整唤醒时间。[[ai-computing-continuum/07_The Autonomous AI-RU Closing the RIC intelligence gap to the radio thr.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 深度集：3 sites、30天、末5天 held out，3,537 train/720 validation windows；节能15.6%，0 missed windows，rApp-only17.6%却漏15 windows。广度集：29 cells、8天、末2天 held out，8,352/2,784 windows；节能12.1%、0 missed windows，rApp-only17.8%却漏166 windows。[[ai-computing-continuum/07_The Autonomous AI-RU Closing the RIC intelligence gap to the radio thr.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 能量用实验室 200W idle→400W full load 曲线、sleep carrier 60W 估算；p.10–11 又要求真实 radio sleep power 实测，故不能当运营网实测节电。广度中位 cell 节省6.3%，总量被安静 cell 主导。[[ai-computing-continuum/07_The Autonomous AI-RU Closing the RIC intelligence gap to the radio thr.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[ai-computing-continuum/07_The Autonomous AI-RU Closing the RIC intelligence gap to the radio thr.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[ai-computing-continuum/07_The Autonomous AI-RU Closing the RIC intelligence gap to the radio thr.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]

## 不确定性与待验证
样本短、回放覆盖有限；0 missed windows 不等于生产零风险。缺 RF 全链唤醒分布、实际 sleep power、coverage/干扰与真实业务 QoS；policy→M-Plane→ruApp contract 标准化为下一阶段。[[ai-computing-continuum/07_The Autonomous AI-RU Closing the RIC intelligence gap to the radio thr.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
