# Hyperscaler 到 GPUaaS：服务感知 T0 边界网关

## 核心结论
外租 GPU 仍需访问云内存储、控制面和私网，因此边界不只是高带宽 DCI，而是跨信任域的租户隔离、服务路由与报文重写。深缓冲仅是这套网关能力之一。[[networking/Scaling AI Beyond the Cloud Boundary- High-Capacity Interconnects Between Hyperscalers and GPUaaS.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]][[networking/Scaling AI Beyond the Cloud Boundary- High-Capacity Interconnects Between Hyperscalers and GPUaaS.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]

## 关键证据、指标与条件
- 已交付角色给出 256K VXLAN tunnel next-hop；1M-class 是 headroom/方向，不能混算现有能力。2048-way tunnel ECMP、每 next-hop rewrite、一致性哈希和 2K+ per-VNET BGP 为能力主张，未给同时开启全部特性后的容量矩阵。[[networking/Scaling AI Beyond the Cloud Boundary- High-Capacity Interconnects Between Hyperscalers and GPUaaS.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- Cisco8223/P200 为 51.2T、3RU、64×800G、MACsec 深缓冲固定系统；“最节能”无瓦数/对照条件，不能据此排 TCO 名次。[[networking/Scaling AI Beyond the Cloud Boundary- High-Capacity Interconnects Between Hyperscalers and GPUaaS.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- 可靠 config replay 必须防止重启时配置尚未重放完就启用数据面；underlay BGP 与 overlay link-state 两边的故障要传播，避免两套独立控制域产生黑洞。[[networking/Scaling AI Beyond the Cloud Boundary- High-Capacity Interconnects Between Hyperscalers and GPUaaS.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- >250K 级 inner MAC/VNI 重写、2K+ overlay ECMP，与千级 per-VRF BGP 分属不同资源维度；页12/13 社区目标又写 1.5K+，不能统一误报为一条已验证控制面极限。[[networking/Scaling AI Beyond the Cloud Boundary- High-Capacity Interconnects Between Hyperscalers and GPUaaS.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]][[networking/Scaling AI Beyond the Cloud Boundary- High-Capacity Interconnects Between Hyperscalers and GPUaaS.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]][[networking/Scaling AI Beyond the Cloud Boundary- High-Capacity Interconnects Between Hyperscalers and GPUaaS.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[networking/Scaling AI Beyond the Cloud Boundary- High-Capacity Interconnects Between Hyperscalers and GPUaaS.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]]

## 图表与架构
已核物理架构 p.5：多 T0 edge active-active，经 MACsec 400/800GE 长途连接 NeoCloud spine。p.11 显示 SDN→gNMI→CONFIG_DB→BGP/SWSS→ASIC_DB/SDK，STATE_DB 回传编程状态；配置接收不等于硬件生效。[[networking/Scaling AI Beyond the Cloud Boundary- High-Capacity Interconnects Between Hyperscalers and GPUaaS.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[networking/Scaling AI Beyond the Cloud Boundary- High-Capacity Interconnects Between Hyperscalers and GPUaaS.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]]

## 不确定性与待验证
需同时满规模 tunnel/VRF/ECMP/MACsec 的编程时延、内存、重启恢复、哈希成员变更扰动与有效 buffer；没有端到端 TTFT/TPOT 或丢包/恢复的分位数。
