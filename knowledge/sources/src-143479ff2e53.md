## 核心结论

- NVIDIA/Meta的基础规范主张统一移动工具接口和工作范围，而非限定泵/控制/包装产品设计；目标是PG25的CDU-to-rack TCS，不含设施水、两相或介电液。 [[cooling-environments/Fluid-Flush-Fill-Cart-for-Pre-Deployment-Conditioning-of-Liquid-Cooled-IT-Systems.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]]、[[cooling-environments/Fluid-Flush-Fill-Cart-for-Pre-Deployment-Conditioning-of-Liquid-Cooled-IT-Systems.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]
- 核心是清洁与回收液不混流、用测量判定验收、记录同步遥测和cycle logs；这将调试质量从操作习惯变为可复核过程。 [[cooling-environments/Fluid-Flush-Fill-Cart-for-Pre-Deployment-Conditioning-of-Liquid-Cooled-IT-Systems.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]、[[cooling-environments/Fluid-Flush-Fill-Cart-for-Pre-Deployment-Conditioning-of-Liquid-Cooled-IT-Systems.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]、[[cooling-environments/Fluid-Flush-Fill-Cart-for-Pre-Deployment-Conditioning-of-Liquid-Cooled-IT-Systems.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
- 流程区分required的flush、one-way fill、drain与recommended的closed-loop condition、sample、N2 purge/dry，不能把六项全称强制要求。 [[cooling-environments/Fluid-Flush-Fill-Cart-for-Pre-Deployment-Conditioning-of-Liquid-Cooled-IT-Systems.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
