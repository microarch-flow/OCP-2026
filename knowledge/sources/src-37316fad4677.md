## 核心结论

- Microsoft演讲称OpenBMC已在超大规模生产运行，其价值是全栈控制、更新节奏、自主集成与共享upstream；新的瓶颈是多ODM/代际/SKU行为差异。[[systems-management/Operating-OpenBMC-at-Hyperscale-What-Works-What-Breaks-and-What-Needs-to-Change.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[systems-management/Operating-OpenBMC-at-Hyperscale-What-Works-What-Breaks-and-What-Needs-to-Change.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[systems-management/Operating-OpenBMC-at-Hyperscale-What-Works-What-Breaks-and-What-Needs-to-Change.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
- 支持同样协议不代表互通：event形状、重试超时、依赖顺序、升级/回滚/证书生命周期都需要共同契约与跨厂商测试。[[systems-management/Operating-OpenBMC-at-Hyperscale-What-Works-What-Breaks-and-What-Needs-to-Change.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[systems-management/Operating-OpenBMC-at-Hyperscale-What-Works-What-Breaks-and-What-Needs-to-Change.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 关键证据

- 生产教训包括systemd依赖拼错静默破坏顺序、每设备retry叠加突破服务级timeout、日志差异让fleet关联变人工归一化。[[systems-management/Operating-OpenBMC-at-Hyperscale-What-Works-What-Breaks-and-What-Needs-to-Change.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
