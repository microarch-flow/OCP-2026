# The Compliance Gap- ESS Safety in the Age of AI Infrastructure

## 核心结论
此ESS safety panel文件主要是人员背景与倡议，核心提醒为储能生命周期跨storage/shipping/installation/decommissioning及不同国家、code、insurer，不能只看机柜运行阶段。并无逐条法规或安全测试结果。[[ai-open-data-center/The Compliance Gap- ESS Safety in the Age of AI Infrastructure.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]] [[ai-open-data-center/The Compliance Gap- ESS Safety in the Age of AI Infrastructure.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

p.7指向Requirements for Energy Storage Systems Used in Data Centers Rev1.0；p.8提出Audit/Align/Act，p.9提出争取OCP在UN运输危险品专家小组observer status。倡议/申请不能写成已取得席位或规则已改变。[[ai-open-data-center/The Compliance Gap- ESS Safety in the Age of AI Infrastructure.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[ai-open-data-center/The Compliance Gap- ESS Safety in the Age of AI Infrastructure.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]] [[ai-open-data-center/The Compliance Gap- ESS Safety in the Age of AI Infrastructure.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 指标与条件
