# Customer-Contextual Fault Management for Bare Metal GPU Infrastructure

## 核心结论
OCI裸金属GPU存在tenant in-band与provider OOB的遥测不对称，安全限制又阻止provider直接装agent；CREW让客户自愿提交诊断、隔离清洗后关联repair事件，形成渐进自动检测闭环。[[ai-clusters/Customer-Contextual Fault Management for Bare Metal GPU Infrastructure.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[ai-clusters/Customer-Contextual Fault Management for Bare Metal GPU Infrastructure.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 关键证据
流程Report（ComputeReportedFaults）→Sanitize（隔离sandbox，untrusted files不得进substrate）→Triage→Repair&Track（MaintenanceEvent）→Learn&Automate。尊重信任边界是方案组成，不应为可观测性绕过客户隔离。[[ai-clusters/Customer-Contextual Fault Management for Bare Metal GPU Infrastructure.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 指标与条件
图p.5为某一AD的GB200 incoming terminations按月及annualized host rate，CREW introduction前后呈趋势变化；纵轴没有可读取的数值标度、未给host population或暴露时间，不能计算故障率下降百分比，也不能作因果证明。[[ai-clusters/Customer-Contextual Fault Management for Bare Metal GPU Infrastructure.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

已看image-only p.4–5，区分RDMA/scale-up/front-end/management路径。原页有“Confidential – Oracle Restricted Employees Only”标记；本卡不复制原图或补造未披露指标，只分析用户已提供文件的边界。[[ai-clusters/Customer-Contextual Fault Management for Bare Metal GPU Infrastructure.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]

## 关联与比较

## 不确定性与待验证
“strong improvement”无定量口径；行业白皮书仍征集。需诊断schema、PII/sensitive-log处理、签名/权限、误归因率、MTTR和自动化回归证据。[[ai-clusters/Customer-Contextual Fault Management for Bare Metal GPU Infrastructure.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
