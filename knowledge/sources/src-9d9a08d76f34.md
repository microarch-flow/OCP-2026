# MX v1.1- Advancing Open Standards for Low-Precision AI with MicroXcaling formats

## 核心结论
Microsoft介绍MX v1.1的重点是转换语义与命名澄清，而非新的硬件吞吐纪录。对加速器架构更重要的是block scale、私有元素格式、舍入、溢出和特殊值在硬件—编译器—框架之间的契约。[[ai-clusters/MX v1.1- Advancing Open Standards for Low-Precision AI with MicroXcaling formats.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[ai-clusters/MX v1.1- Advancing Open Standards for Low-Precision AI with MicroXcaling formats.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[ai-clusters/MX v1.1- Advancing Open Standards for Low-Precision AI with MicroXcaling formats.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 关键证据
v1.1将E8M0改称UE8M0，并明确FP8/FP6/FP4相关转换。p.6的expAdj(V)允许实现自行调整，以降低block最大值饱和误差；因此格式兼容并不自动保证不同实现bit-identical。[[ai-clusters/MX v1.1- Advancing Open Standards for Low-Precision AI with MicroXcaling formats.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[ai-clusters/MX v1.1- Advancing Open Standards for Low-Precision AI with MicroXcaling formats.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 指标与条件
UE8M0使用bias 127，指数范围−127…127，无zero/Inf，11111111表示NaN。FP8提供SAT/NONSAT：E4M3在NONSAT遇溢出/Inf映射NaN，E5M2可映射Inf，SAT映射有限最大值；这些会影响异常传播与测试向量。[[ai-clusters/MX v1.1- Advancing Open Standards for Low-Precision AI with MicroXcaling formats.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[ai-clusters/MX v1.1- Advancing Open Standards for Low-Precision AI with MicroXcaling formats.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 不确定性与待验证
p.10支持名单不能证明各产品已完整支持同一v1.1转换规则；p.11近无损和计算成本平方关系为概括，无可复现实验。应补block长度、scale选择、舍入模式、NaN/饱和黄金向量及跨实现一致性报告。p.12列规范和emulator链接，本卡未外读其版本内容。[[ai-clusters/MX v1.1- Advancing Open Standards for Low-Precision AI with MicroXcaling formats.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]] [[ai-clusters/MX v1.1- Advancing Open Standards for Low-Precision AI with MicroXcaling formats.pdf#page=11|PDF p.11]] [[ai-clusters/MX v1.1- Advancing Open Standards for Low-Precision AI with MicroXcaling formats.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
