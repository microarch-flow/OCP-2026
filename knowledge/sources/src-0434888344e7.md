# Enabling Grid-Compliant Data Center Growth Through Advanced Energy Storage

## 核心结论
EPC Power把ESS作为IT动态负载与POI稳定接口之间的active buffer；关键不是仅有电池，而是converter/control响应、ride-through和模型—硬件一致性。[[ai-open-data-center/Enabling Grid-Compliant Data Center Growth Through Advanced Energy Storage.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[ai-open-data-center/Enabling Grid-Compliant Data Center Growth Through Advanced Energy Storage.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[ai-open-data-center/Enabling Grid-Compliant Data Center Growth Through Advanced Energy Storage.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]

## 关键证据
作者区分近乎即时grid-forming初始响应与持续控制，主张advanced controls可sub-cycle应对step，而standard controls常在sub-second。没有统一测试条件，不能把这些词直接换算成GPU负载p99保护保证。[[ai-open-data-center/Enabling Grid-Compliant Data Center Growth Through Advanced Energy Storage.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 指标与条件
p.4列多家utility/operator的VRT/FRT、ramp、reactive power、monitoring等，表内已有explicit、study-based、proposed差别；这是讲者整理快照，不是本知识库核实的现行法规/并网意见。具体项目需原规则、版本、POI定义和专业评估。[[ai-open-data-center/Enabling Grid-Compliant Data Center Growth Through Advanced Energy Storage.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[ai-open-data-center/Enabling Grid-Compliant Data Center Growth Through Advanced Energy Storage.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
