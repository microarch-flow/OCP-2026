# 包级完整性原型：发布透明度不等于实时执行证明

> 阅读范围：已阅读本文件全部可提取文字（22页）。关键页已视觉核对：10, 12；不代表逐页视觉审核。

## 核心结论

- 作者把包作为可构建/审计/更新单位，通过每包文件hash叶子、append-only日志、按包名排序重建root与inclusion proof连接build和boot。[[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]][[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]][[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]
- 原型能发现合法签名整镜像内未登记包；它证明发布/内容差异，不自动证明包无漏洞、当前运行字节或抗TOCTOU。[[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=20|PDF p.20]]
- 文件主动披露尚未OCP提交/评审/背书，且单log单key无monitor/gossip；不应把它称已部署透明生态。[[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=13|PDF p.13]][[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=14|PDF p.14]][[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=20|PDF p.20]]

## 关键证据

- 演示5个签名rpi3-openbmc镜像，B中2,131包有1个dropbear版本缺本image_line日志记录；记录规范对canonical JSON、newline与signed tree head有明确要求。[[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]][[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
- 设计将device_root extend PCR14；无人归属文件也作为(unowned)叶子，包排序使同镜像不因安装顺序改变root。[[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]]
- 作者称演示板无TPM、swtpm晚于IMA初始化导致PCR10不extend；IMA日志在此只是未硬件绑定文本，不能用来证明现场runtime。[[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]][[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=20|PDF p.20]]

## 指标与条件

- p.12视觉可见2,484/3,758文件（66%）在runtime policy scope，1,274为out-of-scope数据；这是覆盖范围分析，不是运行观测或真实PCR证据。[[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=12|PDF p.12]]
- 第三镜像新增1叶复用2,130为原型delta例，不代表生产日志增长/验证延迟基准。[[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=19|PDF p.19]]
- 对相同字节可继承已发现问题，不能继承“没有发现”的保证；后者取决于评审努力。[[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=21|PDF p.21]]

## 图表与架构

已视觉核对p.10 Merkle/PCR示意与p.12 66%图及小字限制，避免把醒目覆盖比例误读为运行时证明成功。

## 关联与比较

与Runtime SBOM、组件late-binding互补：组件身份可追踪还需证明真实加载/执行；whole-image anti-rollback仍保留，日志仅知道版本存在不自动拒旧版。[[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=10|PDF p.10]][[Security/Package-Aware Integrity- From Source-Agnostic Publication to Full-System Attestation.pdf#page=17|PDF p.17]]

## 不确定性与待验证

缺可信硬件IMA链、实时TOCTOU闭合、多日志见证/一致性监视、生命周期撤销及政策更新；原稿对SAFE制度的比较不是独立政策核验。
