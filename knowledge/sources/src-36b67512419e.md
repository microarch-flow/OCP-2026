# 05_Bridging the AI Continuum Trust, Orchestration, and Open Standards

## 核心结论
此panel配套材料提出AICC Deployment Software Workstream，以硬件描述、silicon root-of-trust、远程验证和钥匙释放建立跨站点可信作业执行；不是完整panel逐字稿，也不是已验证安全产品。[[ai-computing-continuum/05_Bridging the AI Continuum Trust, Orchestration, and Open Standards.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]] [[ai-computing-continuum/05_Bridging the AI Continuum Trust, Orchestration, and Open Standards.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]] [[ai-computing-continuum/05_Bridging the AI Continuum Trust, Orchestration, and Open Standards.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

## 关键证据
数据owner给job+rules，匹配executor拉取；芯片测量启动状态并签名，独立verifier通过后，key manager才在enclave内释放数据。图中verifier/key manager由data owner控制，基础设施provider与owner是不同信任域。[[ai-computing-continuum/05_Bridging the AI Continuum Trust, Orchestration, and Open Standards.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]

## 指标与条件
无attestation latency、签名频率/带宽、作业吞吐、断连时长或攻击测试。不能以“signed receipt”推导所有执行行为都被不可伪造地完整记录。

## 图表与架构
已看p.4–5信任边界。断连后按预先规则继续、封存receipts、恢复连接先重新验证再同步是设计意图；“without gaps”仍需防回滚、时钟、单调计数器与状态机证据。[[ai-computing-continuum/05_Bridging the AI Continuum Trust, Orchestration, and Open Standards.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/ai-infra-deployment-readiness]]；[[knowledge/concepts/ai-infra-observability-contract]]。可信机器描述应绑定加速器固件、DMA隔离、设备测量和数据生命周期，而非只CPU enclave。

## 不确定性与待验证
workstream为提议阶段。缺具体TEE/attestation协议、撤销/过期策略、offline授权边界、receipt schema、GPU保护范围与side-channel模型。[[ai-computing-continuum/05_Bridging the AI Continuum Trust, Orchestration, and Open Standards.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]
