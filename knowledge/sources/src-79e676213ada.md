# Test-Methodology-and-Practice-for-Multi-Accelerator-Copper-Scaleup-Domain-Interconnect-and-Communication-Library

## 核心结论
铜 scale-up 的合格标准不能止于 PRBS 低 BER：必须向真实 traffic、collectives、带载故障注入递进。边缘链路继续留在活动 plane，可能比明确断链更坏，因为它持续制造 straggler。[[ai-clusters/Test-Methodology-and-Practice-for-Multi-Accelerator-Copper-Scaleup-Domain-Interconnect-and-Communication-Library.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[ai-clusters/Test-Methodology-and-Practice-for-Multi-Accelerator-Copper-Scaleup-Domain-Interconnect-and-Communication-Library.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[ai-clusters/Test-Methodology-and-Practice-for-Multi-Accelerator-Copper-Scaleup-Domain-Interconnect-and-Communication-Library.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 关键证据
- POC→EVT→DVT→PVT 每阶段应有量化退出标准；每 lane 误码向量及 FEC histogram 比单一 pass/fail 或平均 BER 更有信息。[[ai-clusters/Test-Methodology-and-Practice-for-Multi-Accelerator-Copper-Scaleup-Domain-Interconnect-and-Communication-Library.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]
- “重插好了”不是根因；区分 link down 与 flap，检测 flap 速率/时长，并在满配 rack 验证机械配合间隙。[[ai-clusters/Test-Methodology-and-Practice-for-Multi-Accelerator-Copper-Scaleup-Domain-Interconnect-and-Communication-Library.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
- collective 验收矩阵 = operation×message size×scale×algorithm，观察 scaling efficiency 与算法切换拐点。[[ai-clusters/Test-Methodology-and-Practice-for-Multi-Accelerator-Copper-Scaleup-Domain-Interconnect-and-Communication-Library.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]]

## 指标与条件
72 加速器/单跳是典型示例，不是全部部署约束。原文“99.9% 仍约 10 坏链路”未给清晰连接总数/统计独立性，不能直接用于可靠性预算。[[ai-clusters/Test-Methodology-and-Practice-for-Multi-Accelerator-Copper-Scaleup-Domain-Interconnect-and-Communication-Library.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]] 带载故障注入应测 detection time、recovery time、retained bandwidth，但文中明确缺统一 pass/fail 数字。[[ai-clusters/Test-Methodology-and-Practice-for-Multi-Accelerator-Copper-Scaleup-Domain-Interconnect-and-Communication-Library.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]]

厂商中立 conformance suite 是倡议；缺阈值、工作负载、硬件/软件版本、实际 BER/CCL 数字。要向供应商索取可复现测试矩阵和故障注入原始轨迹。[[ai-clusters/Test-Methodology-and-Practice-for-Multi-Accelerator-Copper-Scaleup-Domain-Interconnect-and-Communication-Library.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]
