# The New Focus on Energy Storage Systems Safety

## 核心结论
ESS 从facility向row/rack靠近IT负载，安全论证必须跟部署位置、拓扑、duty cycle、故障能量、通风与维护一起变化。产品有证书不等于该集成安装已被接受。[[ai-open-data-center/The New Focus on Energy Storage Systems Safety.pdf#page=2|PDF p.2]] [[ai-open-data-center/The New Focus on Energy Storage Systems Safety.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]]

## 关键证据
文件分 product、application、installation、acceptance 四层，强调 jurisdiction/AHJ/owner/insurer 可能接受不同证据，集成后可需重评。[[ai-open-data-center/The New Focus on Energy Storage Systems Safety.pdf#page=3|PDF p.3]] [[ai-open-data-center/The New Focus on Energy Storage Systems Safety.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]
已发布 Requirements for ESS Used in Data Centers 白皮书是 requirements landscape 的入口，不替代local code/AHJ；两个后续workstreams分别做技术参考V2和选型/部署框架。[[ai-open-data-center/The New Focus on Energy Storage Systems Safety.pdf#page=5|PDF p.5]] [[ai-open-data-center/The New Focus on Energy Storage Systems Safety.pdf#page=6|PDF p.6]] [[ai-open-data-center/The New Focus on Energy Storage Systems Safety.pdf#page=7|PDF p.7]] [[ai-open-data-center/The New Focus on Energy Storage Systems Safety.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 指标与条件
无特定电芯的热失控数据、分隔距离、能量限额、效率/寿命/价格；IEEE1679/IEC62933被列作可适用框架，不能从提及推断某产品已符合。[[ai-open-data-center/The New Focus on Energy Storage Systems Safety.pdf#page=8|PDF p.8]]

## 图表与架构
仅全部文字审阅；不声称消防或电气工程批准。标题页明确 OCP Global Summit2026/San Jose。[[ai-open-data-center/The New Focus on Energy Storage Systems Safety.pdf#page=1|PDF p.1]]

## 关联与比较
[[knowledge/concepts/ai-infra-deployment-readiness]]；功率平滑的性能/寿命方案必须与安全/安装证据并行，不要到commissioning才发现证据不被接受。

## 不确定性与待验证
本卡只记录演讲框架，并非现行法律/合规建议；实际项目需依地区、日期、安装方案由相应专业人员/AHJ核验。V2/framework仍在征集真实冲突、incident与部署案例。[[ai-open-data-center/The New Focus on Energy Storage Systems Safety.pdf#page=9|PDF p.9]]
